# 🦈 CULTIST SHARKHUMAN (Brute Warrior) — x4

**Medium humanoid, chaotic evil**  
**AC** 15 (hide + tough skin) | **HP** 52 (7d8+21) | **Speed** 30 ft, **Swim** 40 ft  
**PB** +2

**STR** 16 (+3) **DEX** 12 (+1) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 8 (-1) **WIS** 11 (+0) **CHA** 9 (-1)

**Skills** Athletics +5, Intimidation +1  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common (broken) + Aquan  
**CR** ~2

### TRAITS

**Blood Frenzy.** The sharkhuman has **advantage on melee attacks** against any creature that **isn’t at full HP**.

**Relentless (1/day).** If reduced to 0 HP, it drops to **1 HP instead**.

### ACTIONS

**Multiattack.** Makes **2 attacks**: **Harpoon OR Bite**.

**Harpoon.** _Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft or range 20/60.  
**Hit:** 8 (1d10+3) piercing.  
If target is **Medium or smaller**, it must succeed **STR save DC 13** or be **pulled 10 ft** toward the sharkhuman.

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.  
**Hit:** 7 (1d8+3) piercing.

### BONUS ACTIONS

**Savage Rush (Recharge 4–6).** Move **up to half speed** without provoking opportunity attacks.

### REACTIONS

**Thrash (1/day).** When hit by a melee attack, the sharkhuman makes a **Bite attack** against the attacker.

## 🦈 QUICK RUN TACTICS (Sharks)

- **Target wounded PCs** for **Blood Frenzy advantage**
    
- Use **Harpoon pull** to drag squishies into melee
    
- Pop **Savage Rush** to reposition and avoid OAs

# 🎭 CULTIST BARD (Minion Caster) — x8

**Medium humanoid, chaotic evil**  
**AC** 13 (leather) | **HP** 18 (4d8) | **Speed** 30 ft  
**PB** +2

**STR** 10 (+0) **DEX** 14 (+2) **CON** 12 (+1) **INT** 10 (+0) **WIS** 11 (+0) **CHA** 14 (+2)

**Saves** Wis +2, Cha +4  
**Skills** Performance +4, Deception +4  
**Resist** — | **Immune** —  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common + 1  
**CR** ~1/2

### TRAITS

**Culty Chorus (1/Turn).** If within **10 ft of another Bard Cultist**, it gets **+1d4 to spell attack OR save DC**.

### ACTIONS

**Dagger.** _Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft or range 20/60. **Hit:** 4 (1d4+2) piercing.

**Vicious Mockery (Cantrip).** 60 ft, Wis save **DC 12**. **Fail:** 5 (1d4+3) psychic, and target has **disadvantage on next attack** before end of its next turn.

**Dissonant Whispers (1st, 2/day).** 60 ft, Wis save **DC 12**. **Fail:** 10 (3d6) psychic + target uses its **reaction** to move away.

**Healing Word (1st, 1/day).** 60 ft, an ally regains **6 (1d4+4)** HP.

### BONUS ACTIONS

**Inspire Violence (Recharge 5–6).** One ally within 60 ft gains **+1d6** to its next weapon damage roll.

### REACTIONS

**Cutting Word (1/day).** When a creature the bard can see within 60 ft hits with an attack: reduce the roll by **1d6** (may turn it into a miss).

## 🎭 QUICK RUN TACTICS (Bards)

- **Round 1:** Vicious Mockery spam + 1 Dissonant Whispers to force reactions/movement
    
- **Stay in pairs** for **Culty Chorus**
    
- Use **Healing Word** to keep sharks up

# Freaktopus  

**Type:** Mutated Octopus / Aberration  
**Origin:** [[Black Rain]] corruption  

---

## Overview  
A massive octopus transformed by the [[Black Rain]] into a grotesque, many-eyed monster. The Freaktopus is one of the most dangerous corrupted creatures the party has yet encountered, capable of swallowing enemies whole and crushing with its tentacles.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- **[[Session 02 Recap]]**: The Freaktopus emerged at the Kuo-toa worksite alongside the [[Freakfish]] and a mutated goblin. The battle became a turning point, with the party using creative tactics to defeat it. A mutant goblin was drenched in oil and exploded inside the Freaktopus, killing it.  

---

## Known Traits  
- Gigantic size, overwhelming strength.  
- Resistant hide; hard to bring down with normal means.  
- Vulnerable to fire and internal damage (as seen when the goblin exploded within).

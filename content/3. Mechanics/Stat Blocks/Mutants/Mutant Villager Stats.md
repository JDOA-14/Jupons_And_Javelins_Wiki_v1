🧟 **Mutated Villager**

Medium humanoid (mutant), chaotic evil

**Armor Class:** 12  
**Hit Points:** 22 (4d8 + 4)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**STR** 16 (+3) | **DEX** 12 (+1) | **CON** 12 (+1) | **INT** 6 (–2) | **WIS** 6 (–2) | **CHA** 6 (–2)

**Saving Throws:** STR +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +4  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8  

**Traits**

**Reckless.**  
At the start of its turn, the mutated villager can choose to gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.

**Actions**

**Multiattack.**  
The mutated village either makes two attacks: (Claw or Bite) OR one Slam:

- **Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
    _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Claw.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
    _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.
- **Slam.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
    _Hit:_ 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.  
    If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be knocked prone.

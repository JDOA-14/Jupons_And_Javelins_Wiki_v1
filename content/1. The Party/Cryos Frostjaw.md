# Abilities & Feats

## _Racial Feats:_

## Racial Features

#### Bite  
Your fanged maw is a **natural weapon**.

- You can make **unarmed strikes** with it  
- On a hit → you deal **1d6 + STR mod piercing damage**

(Counts as a melee attack.)

---

#### Winterborn Ferocity  

When you fall below **25% of your maximum HP**, your survival instincts awaken.

**Until the end of your next turn:**
- **Resilience:** You gain **resistance to all damage** except psychic  
- **Savage Strength:** Your melee weapon attacks deal **extra damage equal to:**

`STR mod (minimum 1) + your character level`

After this effect triggers, you **can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.**

## Level 1

#### Spellcasting  
*PHB ’24 — p139*

You cast spells using **innate arcane power**.

- You know **4 cantrips** (gain more at Lv 4 & 10)
- You prepare Sorcerer spells each day
- Spellcasting Ability: **CHA**
- Spellcasting Focus: **Arcane Focus**
- Spell slots return on a **Long Rest**

(You start prepared with 2 level-1 Sorcerer spells.)

#### Innate Sorcery  
*PHB ’24 — p140*

Your magic surges from within.

As a **Bonus Action**, you can empower your magic for **1 minute**:

- **+1** to your Sorcerer spell save DC  
- **Advantage** on Sorcerer spell attack rolls  

Uses: **2 per Long Rest**

---

## Level 2

#### Font of Magic  
*PHB ’24 — p140*

You gain a pool of **Sorcery Points** you can spend on magical effects.

- You have **2 Sorcery Points** at Level 2  
- You gain more as you level  
- All Sorcery Points return after a **Long Rest**

You may:

**Convert Spell Slots → Sorcery Points**  
- Gain SP equal to the slot’s level *(no action required)*

**Convert Sorcery Points → Spell Slots** *(Bonus Action)*  
- You can create spell slots up to **5th level**  
- Created slots disappear after a Long Rest

---
## Level 3

#### Frost Carapace  
*Homebrew Subclass Feature — Level 1 Unlock*

Your Kodan spirit empowers your frost magic. Whenever you cast a **cold / frost / ice spell of 1st level or higher**, you may gain **Temporary Hit Points** equal to:

**CHA mod + Sorcerer level**

- These THP last **1 minute**
- You cannot gain new THP from this feature **until the previous THP are gone**

---

#### Ice Manipulation  
*(Level 1 Unlock — Action)*

You can subtly control ice and cold to create one of the following effects:

- Create a **harmless icy mist** (range 60 ft) that fills a **1-foot cube**, cool to the touch, lasting up to **1 minute**  
- **Extinguish** a small flame (candle/torch/campfire) within **60 ft**  
- **Chill** up to **1 lb of nonliving material**  
  — or create **up to 1 lb of ice** that does not melt for **1 hour**

## Level 2

#### Metamagic  
*PHB ’24 — p141*

You learn to shape and alter your spellcasting.

- You gain **2 Metamagic options** at Level 2  
- You can use **only 1 option per spell** (unless stated otherwise)  
- Metamagic costs **Sorcery Points**  
- You may swap one option whenever you gain a Sorcerer level  
- You gain **2 more options at Lv 10** and **2 more at Lv 17**

See below for available options 👇

---

### Careful Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
Creatures you choose (up to **CHA mod**, min 1) **automatically succeed** on their saving throw vs your spell and **take no half damage**.

---

### Distant Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
- Double a spell’s range  
- or make **Touch → 30 ft**

---

### Empowered Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
Reroll up to **CHA mod** damage dice (min 1).  
You must use the new rolls.  
Can be stacked with other Metamagic.

---

### Extended Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
Double a spell’s duration (max **24 hrs**).  
If the spell uses Concentration → **Advantage on Concentration saves**.

---

### Heightened Spell  
*Cost: 2 Sorcery Points*  
Give **one target Disadvantage** on saving throws vs your spell.

---

### Quickened Spell  
*Cost: 2 Sorcery Points*  
Cast a spell that normally costs **an Action** as a **Bonus Action**.

Restrictions:
- If you quicken a level 1+ spell, you **cannot cast another level 1+ spell that turn**

---

### Seeking Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
If your spell attack **misses**, reroll the d20.  
You must use the new roll.  
Can be stacked with other Metamagic.

---

### Subtle Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
Cast a spell **without Verbal, Somatic, or Material components**,  
except consumed or costly components.

---

### Transmuted Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
Change spell damage type to **one of:**

Acid • Cold • Fire • Lightning • Poison • Thunder

---

### Twinned Spell  
*Cost: 1 Sorcery Point*  
Increase the **effective spell slot level by 1**  
(for spells that add extra targets with higher-level slots — e.g., *Charm Person*).

# Abilities & Feats

## Racial Feature

#### Crime On My Mind  
You may use **WIS** instead of INT for **Investigation checks related to crimes.**

---

## Abilities

## Level 1

#### Martial Arts  
*PHB ’24 — p101*

Your training lets you fight with speed and precision.

**Monk Weapons**
- Simple melee weapons  
- Martial melee weapons with **Light**

**While unarmored and not using a Shield:**
- **Bonus Action Unarmed Strike**
- **Martial Arts Die:** d6 (scales with level)
- **DEX Attacks:** You may use **DEX** for attack & damage rolls with Unarmed Strikes & Monk weapons
- **Grapple/Shove DC:** You may use **DEX** instead of STR

---

#### Unarmored Defense  
*PHB ’24 — p101*

While not wearing armor or a Shield:

**AC = 10 + DEX mod + WIS mod**

---

## Level 2

#### Monk’s Focus  
*PHB ’24 — p101*

You gain **Focus Points** (see class table).  
You regain all Focus after a **Short or Long Rest**.

**Save DC = 8 + PB + WIS mod**

You can spend Focus on:

**Flurry of Blows — 1 Focus**
- Make **2 Unarmed Strikes** as a Bonus Action

**Patient Defense**
- **Bonus Action:** Disengage  
- **or** spend 1 Focus → **Disengage + Dodge**

**Step of the Wind**
- **Bonus Action:** Dash  
- **or** spend 1 Focus → **Disengage + Dash**  
- Jump distance is doubled

---

#### Unarmored Movement  
*PHB ’24 — p102*

While unarmored & no Shield → **Speed +10 ft**  
(Scales with level)

---

#### Uncanny Metabolism  
*PHB ’24 — p102*

When you **roll Initiative**:
- You may regain **all Focus Points**
- Roll your **Martial Arts die**
- Regain **HP = Monk level + roll**

Uses: **1 / Long Rest**

---

## Level 3

#### Deflect Attacks  
*PHB ’24 — p102*

When hit by Bludgeoning / Piercing / Slashing damage →
**Reaction:** reduce damage by:

`1d10 + DEX mod + Monk level`

If reduced to **0**:
- You may spend **1 Focus**
- Target must make a DEX save
- On fail → take **2× Martial Arts die + DEX mod** damage
- Same damage type as the attack

---

#### Monk Subclass  
*PHB ’24 — p103*

**Way of the Astral Self (Homebrew Variant)**

---

#### Arms of the Astral Self *(Homebrew — Replaces TCE version)*

**Bonus Action — spend 1 Focus Point**

You manifest **spectral astral arms** for **10 minutes**.  
They vanish early if you become **Incapacitated** or die.

**While active:**

**Arms of Spirit**
- Your **Unarmed Strike reach +5 ft**
- You may deal **Force damage** instead of Bludgeoning

**Astral Strike**
- You may use **WIS** instead of STR/DEX  
  for Unarmed Strike attack & damage rolls

**Astral Touch**
- You may use **WIS** instead of STR  
  for Strength checks & saving throws

**Sidereal Sweep**
- When you summon the arms  
  → each creature of your choice within **10 ft** makes a DEX save  
  → on fail, they take **2× Martial Arts die Force damage**  
  → and are **pushed 10 ft away**

You may repeat **Sidereal Sweep** as a **Bonus Action**  
by spending **1 Focus Point**.

*PHB ’24 — p207*

- **+1** to any ability score (max 20)
- Gain **proficiency in one skill**
- Gain **Expertise in one skill** you’re already proficient in (that you don’t already have Expertise with)

## Class Features
- Martial Arts
	- Dex instead of Str for unarmed attacks + 1d6 for unarmed attacks
	- Access to Ki Points (current total = 6)
- Unarmored Defence
	- AC = 10 + WIS + DEX
- Unarmored Movement 
	- +10 movement
- Flurry of Blows
	- 1 KP
	- 2 unarmed strikes as a bonus action
- Patient Defence
	- 1 KP
	- Disengage as a Bonus Action
- Step of the Wind
	- 1 KP
	- Dash as a Bonus Action
- Deflect Attack
	- triggered when you take physical damage
	- use reaction to reduce damage by 1d10 + Dex + Monk Lvl
	- If Damage reduced to 0, you can redirect it to an enemy within 5ft, or 6ft if ranged
	- That creature does a Dex save, or takes 2 martial dice rolls dmg + Dex
	- 1 KP to redirect
- Uncanny Metabolism
	- When you roll Initiative, you can regain all expended Focus Points. When you do so, roll your Martial Arts die, and regain a number of Hit Points equal to your Monk level plus the number rolled.
	- Once per Long Rest.
- Astral Self
	- Summons their Astral Arms
	- 1 KP, lasts 10 mins
	- Creatures within 10ft make Dex Save or take 2 Martial dice dmg
	- Now use WIS for STR checks & saves & damage
	- +5ft extra reach

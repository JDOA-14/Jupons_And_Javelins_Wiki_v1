# Abilities & Feats

## Level 1

#### Favored Foe  
*TCE p56*

When you hit a creature, you can mark it as your favoured enemy (no action) for up to 1 minute (or as if knocked out of concentration).

- First time each turn you hit it → +1d4 damage  
- Uses = your proficiency bonus per Long Rest

---

#### Deft Explorer  
*TCE p56*

You become an exceptional traveler and survivor.

You gain:

**Canny**
_Sleight of hand_
- Choose 1 skill you are proficient in  
- You double your proficiency bonus for that skill  
- You also learn 2 additional languages  

(Replaces: Natural Explorer. More benefits come at 6 and 10.)

---

## Level 2

#### Fighting Style  
*PHB ’14 p89*

Choose one fighting style. You can’t choose the same style more than once.

##### Druidic Warrior (chosen)  
*TCE p57*

- Learn **two druid cantrips**  
- They count as **ranger spells**  
- **Wisdom** is your spellcasting ability  
- When you gain a ranger level, you can swap one of these cantrips for another druid cantrip  

---

#### Spellcasting  
*PHB ’14 p89*

You learn to cast ranger spells.

- You use the **ranger spell list**  
- Spellcasting follows the normal rules in Chapter 10  
- Spell list in Chapter 11  

(Short version: You now have spell slots and ranger spells.)

---

## Level 3

#### Ranger Archetype  
*PHB ’14 p89*

At 3rd level you choose a ranger archetype.  
You gain additional archetype features at 7th, 11th, and 15th level.

##### Beast Master (chosen)  
*PHB ’14 p93*

You form a magical partnership with a beast companion that fights with you.

---

#### Primal Companion  
*TCE p61*

(Replaces: Ranger’s Companion)

You magically summon a **Primal Beast**:

- Choose **Land, Sea, or Sky** stat block  
- Uses your **proficiency bonus** in several places  
- You decide what animal form it looks like  

**In combat**
- Acts on **your turn**  
- Moves + reacts freely  
	- But only uses **Dodge** unless you:
		- Use a **bonus action** to command an action  
		- You can trade **one of your attacks** to let it Att
- If you are incapacitated, it can do more than just dodge

# PHB2014
## Level 3

#### Primeval Awareness  
*PHB ’14 p89*

(Original ranger feature — often replaced by Primal Awareness)

You can spend an action and **expend a ranger spell slot** to heighten your awareness.

For **1 minute per spell slot level**, you can sense whether any of the following creatures are within **1 mile** (or **6 miles** in favored terrain):

- aberrations  
- celestials  
- dragons  
- elementals  
- fey  
- fiends  
- undead  

This feature does **not** reveal:
- number
- location
- identity

---

#### Primal Awareness  
*TCE p56*

(Optional feature — **replaces Primeval Awareness**)

You learn extra spells that don’t count against your known ranger spells:

**Primal Awareness Spells**
- 3rd — *Speak with Animals*  
- 5th — *Beast Sense*  
- 9th — *Speak with Plants*  
- 13th — *Locate Creature*  
- 17th — *Commune with Nature*  

You can cast **each of these spells once for free** (no spell slot).  
All free uses refresh after a **long rest**.


# PHB2024

## Level 1: 
#### Favored Enemy
PHB'24 p120

You always have the ([[Hunter's Mark]]) [Hunter's Mark](https://5e.tools/spells.html#hunter's%20mark_xphb)spell prepared. You can cast it twice without expending a spell slot, and you regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a [Long Rest](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20rest_xphb).

The number of times you can cast the spell without a spell slot increases when you reach certain Ranger levels, as shown in the Favored Enemy column of the Ranger Features table.

lvl4 = twice



### Weapon Mastery
PHB'24 p120

Your training with weapons allows you to use the [mastery properties](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#weapon%20mastery%20properties_xphb) of two kinds of [weapons](https://5e.tools/items.html#blankhash,flsttype:simple%20weapon=1~martial%20weapon=1) of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as [Longbows](https://5e.tools/items.html#longbow_xphb) and [Shortswords](https://5e.tools/items.html#shortsword_xphb).

Whenever you finish a [Long Rest](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20rest_xphb), you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the [mastery properties](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#weapon%20mastery%20properties_xphb) of [Scimitars](https://5e.tools/items.html#scimitar_xphb) and [Longswords](https://5e.tools/items.html#longsword_xphb).

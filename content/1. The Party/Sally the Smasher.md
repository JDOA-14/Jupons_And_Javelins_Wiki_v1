# Abilities & Feats


## Level 1
##### Rage
*PHB ’14 p46*
- Bonus action
	- Advantage on Strength checks & saves
	- +2 damage on melee attacks using Strength
	- Resistance to bludgeoning / piercing / slashing
	- You can’t cast or concentrate on spells
	- ** You get 1 rage back per short rest

Ends early if:
- you are unconscious, or
- you haven’t attacked or taken damage since your last turn

Rages per long rest:
2 (Lvl 1) • 3 (Lvl 3) • 4 (Lvl 6) • 5 (Lvl 12) • 6 (Lvl 17)

##### Reckless Attack
*PHB ’14 p46*

When you make your first attack on your turn, you may attack recklessly:

- You gain advantage on melee Str attacks this turn
- Attacks against you have advantage until your next turn

## Level 3
### Primal Knowledge
PHB'24 p52

You gain proficiency in another skill of your choice from the skill list available to Barbarians at level 1. (Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, or Survival.)

In addition, while your Rage is active, you can channel primal power when you attempt certain tasks; whenever you make an ability check using one of the following skills, you can make it as a Strength check even if it normally uses a different ability: Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival. When you use this ability, your Strength represents primal power coursing through you, honing your agility, bearing, and senses.

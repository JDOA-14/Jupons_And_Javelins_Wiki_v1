## Level 1

#### Spellcasting  
*PHB ’24 — p165*

You are a prepared arcane spellcaster.

- You know **3 cantrips** (gain more at Lv 4 & 10)
- You cast spells from your **spellbook**
- You prepare spells each day from your book
- Spellcasting Ability: **INT**
- Spellcasting Focus: **Arcane Focus or Spellbook**

**Spellbook**
- Contains **6 level-1 wizard spells** at start
- Add **2 spells per wizard level**
- You regain spell slots after a **Long Rest**

(See PHB for full wizard spell rules.)

---

#### Ritual Adept  
*PHB ’24 — p155*

You can cast any **ritual-tag spell** in your spellbook as a **ritual**,  
even if it isn’t prepared — you must read from the book.

---

#### Arcane Recovery  
*PHB ’24 — p155*

After a **Short Rest**, you may recover spell slots totalling up to  
**½ your wizard level (round up)**  
*(no slot level 6+)*

Example: Level 4 wizard → recover **2 levels worth**  
(1×2nd-level **or** 2×1st-level)

Usable **once per Long Rest**.

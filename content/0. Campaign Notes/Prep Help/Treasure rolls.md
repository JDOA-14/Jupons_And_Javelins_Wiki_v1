
# Treasure (DMG 2024) — Quick Reference (p120)

## Individual Treasure (p120)
Characters might find small amounts of treasure in the pockets, pouches, or personal stashes of individual monsters. Even if a monster doesn't intentionally collect treasure, characters might find scattered coins and other monetary treasure left behind by the monster's previous victims.

Use the table below to determine how much treasure **a single monster** has based on its **Challenge Rating (CR)**.

**Group Treasure Rule:**  
To determine the total treasure for a group of similar creatures, **roll once** and **multiply** by the number of creatures in the group.

### Random Individual Treasure
| CR | Treasure |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | 3d6 (10) GP |
| 5–10 | 2d8 × 10 (90) GP |
| 11–16 | 2d10 × 10 (110) PP |
| 17+ | 2d8 × 100 (900) PP |

## Treasure Hoards (p120)
Adventurers sometimes discover large caches of treasure: the accumulated wealth of a large group of creatures, or the belongings of a single powerful creature that hoards valuables.

Use the table below to create a hoard:
- **Single monster hoard:** use that monster’s CR row
- **Group hoard:** use the CR of the **leader**
- **Big hoarder (e.g., dragon):** roll twice **or** roll once and **double** the total

**Benchmark:** aim to roll on the hoard table about **once per game session**.  
Use Chapter 7 guidelines to determine which magic items are in the hoard.

### Random Treasure Hoard
| CR | Monetary Treasure | Magic Items |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–4 | 2d4 × 100 (500) GP | 1d4-1 |
| 5–10 | 8d10 × 100 (4,400) GP | 1d3 |
| 11–16 | 8d8 × 1,000 (36,000) GP | 1d4 |
| 17+ | 6d10 × 10,000 (330,000) GP | 1d6 |

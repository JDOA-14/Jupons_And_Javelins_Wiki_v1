| The Everfrost Pearl (or Iceveil Pearl) is a rare, ancient artifact passed down through generations in the ice tribes. Entrusted to a single family for forty years, it is believed to hold the power to maintain the icy lands' eternal frost, preventing the threat of melting and flooding the world. The pearl is revered not only for its power over ice and water but also for its role in ensuring the balance of the land. Its believed that its magical aura strengthens the tribes ability to heal wounds and ailments using ice magic though its true potential remains a secret. Guarded fiercely and in silence, the pearl’s presence is whispered to be the only thing standing between the world’s icy hold and a catastrophic thawing of Nyvaldra that would drown the Valhyria. |

# Black Rain Vials

**Creators:** [[Peldo Satch]] (in his lab, Session 6).  

**Description:**  
Concentrated forms of the mutagenic **Black Rain**, stored in crystal vials.  
- Must be frozen otherwise the black rain evaporates
- Used to corrupt living beings into horrors.  
- Spread via sabotage of [[Quince’s Quencher Ale (Corrupted)]].  

**Appearances:**  
- Session 6: Found in [[Peldo Satch]]’s secret lab on the train.  
- Session 6: Used to spike [[Quince’s Quencher Ale (Corrupted)]].
- Session 17: in [[Duke Vizier Wystan Everbell|Wystan Everbell]]'s secret labratory in [[Shardhold Citadel|The Shardhold Citadel]].

Amateur/handcrafted versions attempted a few times by [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]] 
- Once he found out it needed to be frozen, he tasked [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] with keeping it cold and taking a vial to Aeryndor.

Device located in [[Nyserith]] that records magic levels of [[Valhyria]] through time periods.

[[The Mural of the Graph]], shows a what an older Headmaster, [[Orvus Fillorvus]], claims to be a recording from the Memoria Receptio - however it was proven by great wizards and [[Aethercrysts|Aethercryst]] clerics to be false. 

### [[Session 11 Recap - FULL|Session 11]] -
[[Galahad Guzzletome]] used the Memoria Receptio to prove [[The Mural of the Graph]] to actually be a true representation of magic through time in [[Valhyria]].

**Owner:** [[Gundrik Ashclaw]]  (however The Gang is now in control of [[Gundrik Ashclaw]]'s life)

**Description:**  
- The pearl removed from the [[Crown of Thundermarch]]. 
- Has very powerful lightning/thunder magic.

### CHAIN OF EVENTS
- The [[Thundermarch Pearl]] was inside the [[Crown of Thundermarch]] when it was stolen by [[Stebonheath]] and [[Helga Brightrun|Sally The Sniper]] (disguised as [[Helga Brightrun]]).
- It's believed that Stebonheath was ordered to steal the crown by [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man|The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]]. Who then destroyed the crown, and gave the magical pearl inside to [[Gundrik Ashclaw]] -- claiming it to be [[Everfrost Pearl|The Everfrost Pearl]]. 
- By the time Gundrik had deduced that the pearl wasn't the one he had been promised, he was already in combat with [[The Gang]].
- The Gang defeated Gundrik and returned the [[Thundermarch Pearl]] to [[King Selos SkySplitter]] in [[Thundermarch]].

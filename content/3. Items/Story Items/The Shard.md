## The Shard
At the city’s core stands **[[The Shard]]**, a hummer-sized, pulsating blue crystalline monolith.

Known facts:
- The Shard is believed to be the source of **life and magic** across [[Valhyria]]
- Its influence supposedly strengthens magic in those living nearby
- It is physically embedded in the ground beneath [[1. Eryndor|Eryndor]]
- The city was founded specifically to claim and protect it

Historical significance:
- The **[[God King]]** ultimately fused himself with the Shard during **[[The Greatest Sacrifice]] (374 AGK)** to prevent the continent from collapsing into desolation
- Twice yearly, sacrificial rites are performed to sustain [[The God King|the God King]] and the Shard

The Crown of Thundermarch

**Owner:** [[Gundrik Ashclaw]]  

**Description:**  
A symbol of rulership over Thundermarch.  
- Suggests Gundrik is consolidating power.  
- May tie into black rain and regional corruption.

**Appearances:**  
- Session 4: Revealed Gundrik possesses it.  

#### The Pearl in the Crown
- The [[Thundermarch Pearl]] was inside the [[Crown of Thundermarch]] when it was stolen by [[Stebonheath]] and [[Helga Brightrun|Sally The Sniper]] (disguised as [[Helga Brightrun]]).
- It's believed that Stebonheath was ordered to steal the crown by [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man|The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]]. Who then destroyed the crown, and gave the magical pearl inside to [[Gundrik Ashclaw]] -- claiming it to be [[Everfrost Pearl|The Everfrost Pearl]] he was "promised".

[[The Gang|The gang]] receive a series of handles/doohickeys that attach to their weapons/items. Each one gives a different ability/effect.

---
### Kaelen

- Gives *Divine Expulsion*
	- Once per day
	- One active minion can be divinely expelled -
		- A bright streak flashes from the heavens to a minion you control who then blossoms with a harmonious trill into a brilliant explosion
			- Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centred on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 radiant damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Confirmed: given to player.
---
### May

- Gives *Heavenstorm Missile*
	- Once per Short Rest
	- When throwing a javelin with this attachment, you can heal others along its path -
		- As the javelin spins through the air, it rapidly releases spurts of healing energy towards those you deem in need of [[The Shard]]'s graces.
			- When you throw your javelin with this attachment, you may select any number of creatures within 10-feet of it's line of travel to receive 1d4 + your Wisdom Modifier healing.
Confirmed: given to player.
---
### Sally

- Gives *Divine Reppeller*
	- Once per Short Rest
	- You unleash a spinning section of your weapon, creating an area of damage to displace enemies.
		- You rip away the release lever, detaching the bottom of your weapon. As it spins, razor-thin whips of light splay outwards, creating a shining whirling disc that cuts like a knife.
			- This spinner takes up a 5-foot-cube and can be released within 10 feet of your location. Each creature in that area takes 2d4 Slashing damage + 2d4 radiant damage. A creature also takes this damage if it enters the cube, ends its turn there, or if the cube moves into its space. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.
			- On your later turns, you can take an action to travel the cube up to 10 feet.
Confirmed: given to player.
---
### [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]

- Gives *Divine Remanoeuvre*
	- Once per Long Rest
	- When unleashing power worthy of [[The Shard]]'s attention, you may do so with more forethought.
		- When smiting, you may re-roll 1s and 2s on any damage roll that is part of that attack action.
		- + extra d4 radiant on a 19/20
Confirmed: given to player.
---
### [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]]

- Gives _Divine Splay_
	- Once per short rest
		- The shark teeth launch out, attached by radiant tendrils of light
			- Do extra 1d10 Radiant damage to an enemy near whoever you hit.
Confirmed: given to player.

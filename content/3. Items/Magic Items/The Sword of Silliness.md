iMagic item that is a sword that acts like the wand of wonder

When swung at something, the sword will produce a random effect. Player will roll a D100 and the sword acts according to this table:

### Known outcomes:
- 30-39: The sword explodes into a [[Fireball]]. It remains in tact afterwards.
- Potentially one of the "cast a spell ones" but it turned the target a blinding white -- anyone nearby had disadvantage on attacks and advantage to be hit.

### spoilers:

01 : Your weapons become gelatinous - dealing 1d4 -1 + str/dex bludgeoning. Lasts for 12 seconds (two turns).
02 - 05: you and your target become prone
21-25: You and your target both take 1d6 psychic damage as you both image yourselves kissing each other.
30-39: The sword explodes into a [[Fireball]]. It remains in tact afterwards.
40-45: Cast a random spell from your prepared spells (roll depending on number of spells). It must target who you are targeting. If you have no prepared spells - good luck.
50: Everyone's weapons within 500ft become gelatinous - dealing 1d4 -1 + str/dex bludgeoning. Lasts for 12 seconds (two turns).
60-65: Cast a random spell from your prepared spells (roll depending on number of spells). No matter the spell, it affects a 10ft radius around whoever you are targeting. If you have no prepared spells - good luck.
70-79: The sword explodes into a [[Fireball]] - you may pick 3 creatures in it's radius to be protected from the blast. It remains in tact afterwards.
98-00: everyone's weapons within 500ft besides yours become gelatinous - dealing 1d4 -1 + str/dex bludgeoning. Lasts for 12 seconds (two turns).

more rolls to be created.

Owner: Cole Le Fev

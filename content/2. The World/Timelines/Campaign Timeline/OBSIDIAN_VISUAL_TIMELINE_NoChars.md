# ⏳ Interactive Campaign Timeline

The Timeline begins somewhere in the year 614 AGK.
```timeline-labeled
date: Day 1
title: Session 1 - Ashdawn
content: Meeting at The Crooked Tusk in Ashdawn, bandit fight on the road North of Ashdawn, discovery of the hideout, 6-hour travel into Dampiner Forest, and overnight camp / meeting with lucky Halfling.

date: Day 2
title: Session 1 - Dampiner Forest
content: In the morning, they travel to and raid of the bandit hideout in Dampiner Forest, encounter with the Dragon-Aura-Faced Man.

date: Day 2
title: Session 1 - Ashdawn
content: Return to Ashdawn -- leaving in the morning and arriving 9 hours later, and the Black Rain mutant outbreak.

date: Day 3
title: Session 2 - Ashdawn / Tilden Farmhouse
content: The party met with Virka in Ashdawn in the morning to investigate the shell of a magic pearl. They travelled 3 hours West to the Tilden Farmhouse, where they tracked footprints, were attacked by a group of goblins and bandits, and successfully defended the home.

date: Day 3
title: Session 2 - Tilden Farmhouse
content: The party stayed the night at the Tilden Farmhouse to repair the damage caused by the ambush.

date: Day 4
title: Session 2 - Drippledown (South of Ashdawn)
content: The party left the Tilden Farmhouse in the morning and travelled approximately 11 hours to the fishing village of Drippledown. While investigating the docks, they were caught in a 'Black Rain' event that mutated the local inhabitants. They engaged in a perilous battle against a mutated Freaktopus.

date: Day 4
title: Session 3 - Drippledown
content: Departed Drippledown; Gronk was abducted by his ghastly undead great x12 grandfather. They intimidated a Kua-Toa to take them to Thundermarch

date: Day 5
title: Session 3 - Thundermarch
content: Travel to Thundermarch takes 2 whole days. They leave Drippledown at night of day 4 and arrive at Thundermarch at night on day 5. In Thundermarch, they met with Captain Verrik, King Selos Skysplitter and Prince Sier Skysplitter. Also Leif, Stebonheath & Helga. They accepted a mission to clear Orcs from the nearby hamlet of Mistthrow.

date: Day 6
title: Session 3 - Thundermarch / Mistthrow
content: Shop in Thundermarch in the morning. Recruited mercenaries Fior, Silch, and Chains. Travel 3 hours to Mistthrow Cleared a dungeon at Mistthrow and discovered an ancient map showing Orc activity headed toward Skaldgrym. Returned to find Thundermarch devastated by Black Rain -- and Stebonheath and Helga gone, got some info from Prince Sier and accepted a few other bounties.

date: Day 4
title: Session 4 - Drippledown
content: (Pre-session) Cryos wakes up with his pearl missing; he travels to the nearby Drippledown -- and convinces Sheosh to take him to Thundermarch.

date: Day 7
title: Session 4 - Thundermarch
content: Cole and Kaelen group up, notice Sally is missing, and travel to a farm to investigate a bounty on Orc brothers. While this is happening, Cryos travels from Drippledown to Thundermarch.

date: Day 7
title: Session 4 - Farm north east of Thundermarch
content: Cryos learns about Cole and Kaelen, and heads towards the farm. Cole and Kaelen stay the night at the farmhouse.

date: Day 8
title: Session 4 - Farm north east of Thundermarch
content: Cryos Frostjaw arrives and joins the group. They identify the source of the stolen animals and track them further.

date: Day 8
title: Session 4 - Abandoned Watchtower
content: The group discovers a watchtower, fights off two worgs and two Orc brothers, and rescue the stolen livestock. They head south to begin the other bounties

date: Day 8
title: Session 5 - Wilds North East of Thundermarch
content: (Pre-Session) Cole, Kaelen, and Cryos head south tracking a bounty for Worg riders. They follow the trail for a couple of hours and camp for the night. While the gang is sleeping, Cryos is kidnapped by Bearbandits.

date: Day 9
title: Session 5 - Road toward Nerton
content: (Pre-Session) Kaelen and Cole wake to find Cryos missing. Assuming he left, they continue their bounty hunt. They stumble upon the Kua-Toa, Sheosh, convince him to drive them in his kart, and find the Worg Riders who begin chasing them.

date: Day 9
title: Session 5 - Road between Thundermarch and Nerton
content: The main session begins. Cole, Kaelen, and May engage in a high-speed combat encounter with the Worg riders. This occurs 6 hours away from Thundermarch, 3 hours away from Nerton, and 2 hours away from where they camped on Day 08.

date: Day 9
title: Session 5 - Nerton
content: After the fight, they team up and travel approximately 3 hours to Nerton, arriving after sunset. They clear a Rat Bounty from a basement, then intervene in a siege between Orcs and Bearfolk, successfully saving the village.

date: Day 6
title: Session 6 - Camp Bearblood
content: (Pre-Session) Sally is given a mission by the Sally Syndicate -- to investigate a band of orcs that seem to be taking orders from someone. She disguises and stations herself in Camp Bearblood -- successfully locating several key clues.

date: Day 9
title: Session 6 - Camp Bearblood
content: (Pre-Session) Gronk is dropped off by his ancestor in the orc makeshift prison camp, Camp Bearblood. There, he finds Cryos (and his bearbandit captors) as well as Sally the Smasher, who has been stationed there by the Sallies to figure out what is going on.

date: Day 10
title: Session 6 - Camp Bearblood
content: With the help of Mercenaries, Myranda, Sally The Sellout, and Sir Fradd, The Gang rescued Cryos (and surprisingly Gronk and Sally) from an orc prison. They also discovered maps and information regarding Orc and Bearfolk movements, and the location of a Stebonheath-planned truce meeting.

date: Day 10
title: Session 6 - Travel Path
content: The Party travelled from Camp Bearblood (located in the mountains southeast of Thundermarch) to the village of Everviewed (between Nerton and Highwatch). After the fight at Camp Bearblood, the rest of the day is spent travelling.

date: Day 11
title: Session 6 - Everviewed
content: The Gang arrives at Everviewed. They explored the village, discovered Stebonheath in a basement, and defended against mutated villagers and a 40-foot 'Farm Freak' after Stebonheath triggered the Black Rain.

date: Day 11
title: Session 7 - Everviewed / Wrethwood
content: Some of the party, being mistakenly chosen by Gronk's ancestor's undead squire, are transported via astral flame to a cave between Wrethwood and Fladwyn. They lose the squire and must track a hungry Owlbear to find him. They engage in a skirmish with a raider camp.

date: Day 11
title: Session 7 - Wrethwood / Faldwyn
content: The party finds and heals an Aaracokra Monk Detective,Tekka Glyde-Eye, who is chasing Anti-Aarakocra cultists. They spend the night in a cave and prepare a trap with the help of some Kenku guardsmen.

date: Day 12
title: Session 7 - Wrethwood / Faldwyn
content: In the morning, the party engage in a violent morning battle with Mallardfolk cultists, the Reclaimers. They defeat them, and are allowed insight into the Kenkus log of magic items-- discovering hints towards the locations of many magic items they're trying to find. Also, Mae receives a vision of a mountain-sized Arakokra.

date: Day 12
title: Session 7 - Ancestral portal
content: With regained strength, the squire Botwana offers to return The Gang home. Either now, when it's safe from the Black Rain -- or exactly when they were taken, where they could die... but have a chance to save their friends.

date: Day 11
title: Session 8 - Everviewed
content: Cole, Cryos, and Mae return and help save the rest of the party from the Black Rain. The party defeated a mutated Stebonheath and met with Sally the Sniper, who was Helga Brightrun the whole time. They rest as they travel to Highwatch.

date: Day 12
title: Session 8 - Highwatch
content: The Gang arrive at Highwatch, secure bounty payment and favour with the Mayor, turn in bounties, and are attacked that night by seductive Tabaxi Assasins. Cole tasks Mae with protecting Cryos -- as he keeps a part of Stebonheath's mutated blood on ice.

date: Day 13
title: Session 8 - Highwatch (Train)
content: After a heads-up about a meeting on it, the party boarded a magical steam-powered train, discovered a hidden Black Rain laboratory run by Peldo Satch, and witnessed a mass mutation event triggered by the Dragon-Aura-faced man.

date: Day 13
title: Session 8 - Highwatch (Train)
content: The Gang fights through the train's worth of mutants and regroups in the front cabin. Cole unlatches the back 80% of the train. Keeping them safe, but technically unleashing a swarray of bloodthirsty mutants on the hedge-kingdoms of Valhyria. Oops.

date: Day 13
title: Session 9 - Skies Between Highwatch & Aeryndor
content: Cryos, flown by an owlin, successfully mid-air-delivers the frozen Stebonheath blood to a penguinfolk mage, carried by an owlin.

date: Day 13
title: Session 9 - Train/Nyserith
content: The party loot the VIP carriage and decide they should deal with the horde of mutants they unleashed by unlatching the back of the train. They cleared the remaining mutated occupants from the train carriages and are visited by 4 students from Nyserith, sent to clean up.

date: Day 13
title: Session 9 - Nyserith
content: Met with Acting Headmaster Sylak Appletree and various Guild stationeers; explored the city's academic sectors -- including Darkle Charkle, who may have known Stebonheath.

date: Day 13
title: Session 9 - Nyserith Library
content: Researched the 'Black Rain' spell and identified a member of the Cult of the Longest Cloak.

date: Day 13
title: Session 9 - Nyserith (The Rave)
content: Infiltrated a cult-related rave and engaged in a violent confrontation with Twiggy Marshdust.

date: Day 13
title: Session 9 - Challenge Room
content: Lured into a trap by the leader of the CotLC, acting headmaster Sylak Appletree. Completed Floor 1 of the Challenge Room, defeating Lester Saffron and Wrenchbot 4000.

date: Day 13
title: Session 10 - Arcane Facility (Flooding Room)
content: Defeated Darkle Charkle and Moris Boris, escaped the flooded chamber, and passed through a portal.

date: Day 13
title: Session 10 - Light Trap Room
content: Navigated a cavern filled with deadly beams of light, encountered Head of Illusions cult member Alistair McCant, and secured the Book of the Skip Ahead (which obliterated Turbo).

date: Day 13
title: Session 10 - Room of Recovery
content: The party gained the effects of a Long Rest; Mae gained new abilities from the Book of the Skip Ahead. Sally, Cole, Kaelen, and Gronk follow suit.

date: Day 13
title: Session 10 - Final Challenge Room
content: Defeated Sylak and, in turn, the Cult of the Longest Cloak; Kaelen deciphered the arcane focus to stop his healing. But then the gang needed to hack some crystals and alert Leif to get out of the Challenge Room.

date: Day 14
title: Session 10 - Headmaster's Storeroom
content: Received a victory message from Galahad Guzzletome, which turned into a call to action once he realised who he was talking to. They were given free rein to loot several magic items, including the Bag of Holding from his magical storeroom. They unleashed BarterBot 5000.

date: Day 14
title: Session 11 - The Magic Storeroom / Nyserith
content: Called to Eryndor by Gallahad Guzzletome, the party (now technically fugitives) stealth through Nyserith, inspected the Mural of the Graph, and successfully stowed away on an elven-led caravan.

date: Day 15
title: Session 11 - Road to Eryndor
content: While travelling, The Gang reminisce on their previous fortnight of adventures, and separate from the caravan after a log forces them to divert their course. The Gang realise it was a trap, and go back to help -- mainly due to Kaelen thinking the leader of the caravan is his mum, the dragon, polymorphed into an elf. She is not. The party defeated an ambush of Orcs and Goblins.

date: Day 16
title: Session 11 - Eryndor
content: They arrived in Eryndor, located Drakes Beerland, and met with a distressed Galahad. who explains there is a great disturbance in the past magic levels of Valhyria.

date: Day 17
title: Session 12 - Eryndor
content: May and Leif met with Maester Kestrel. Cole, Kaelen, Gronk and Sally received news of the King of Skaldgrym's death. Sally disappeared. Cryos sacrifices his innate magic to power up Galahad enough for him to find some info that was magically locked in his brain. The Gang, still fugitives, narrowly escapes a Golden Guard pursuit. The party obtained evidence from Galahad Guzzletome, and he also claims to have unlocked the knowledge he sought -- but can't do the same for them. He gives them as much info as he can, then flies off towards the sun. Cole stealths into the city and clears their names with the High Council seat for Aeryndor, Herrace Heatsink.

date: Day 18
title: Session 12 - Eryndor / Shardhold Citadel
content: Met with Duke Aldren Thorne to accept bounties. The party defeated a Cow Spider infestation in the slums and successfully spotted signs that the Black Rain may have been involved. They also defended The Great Sacrifice from being ruined by the Cult of the Bard and the Cult of the Shark.

date: Day 18
title: Session 13 - Shardhold Citadel / Flea Hurdle
content: The party watch the great sacrifice take place and notices some other magic occurring too. Gronk and Cole follow two suspects throughout the city while Cryos purchases new weapons now that he can't use magic. May and Kaelen go to a pub in Flea Hurdle and meet some Bearbandits. At a second pub, Kaelen convinces the bearbandit, Biteclaw, to let him and some non-detective allies join in a bearbandit raid the following day.

date: Day 19
title: Session 13 - Eryndor
content: May and Kaelen received bounty payment from Duke Vizier Aldren Thorne -- who seems disinterested in the potential tampering of the Great Sacrifice. Cole meets with Herrace Heatsink and Captain Verrik -- who reveal they are suspicious of Aethercrysts but can't act due to the threat of political fallout. The Gang purchased a wagon and some animals, met with Biteclaw and joined their caravan-- headed west.

date: Day 19
title: Session 13 - Western Road
content: The party stop to camp overnight. Biteclaw's carriage is joined by two more bandit carriages. The Gang is completely outnumbered. While Cole and others hide in their carriage, Biteclaw tells the other members of the gang their plan for the morrow: to kill a paladin hiding in Pinetide and obtain his powerful score. Kaelen convinces Biteclaw to let him, Gronk, and Cryos enter the thorp first.

date: Day 20
title: Session 13 - Pinetide
content: The party arrived at Pinetide and successfully warned the Paladin that the thorp is about to be under attack. They have just enough time to evacuate the villagers before the onslaught begins. They engage in a large-scale assault against waves of bandits who have joined forces (Bearfolk, Orcs, and Humans). The group successfully defended the village, defeat the high-ranking Bearbandit warlock, Falrus, with the help of a group of rebel combatants. After the dust settled, the rebels incapacitated The Gang with sleep darts.

date: Day 20
title: Session 14 - Pinetide
content: The party wakes, stealthily reunites, and discovers a rebel group led by Leif and Petra -- who are interrogating the paladin, Shorun Forsh. They learn the 'cult' at the sacrifice was actually attempting to rescue sacrifices. The Rebels are full of sacrifices who learnt the actual Aethercryst sacrifices have been faked for the last decade or so. They're suspicious that the Aethercrysts are up to more shadiness. With the help of The Gang, they successfully negotiate with Shorun Forsh to learn about the magic item whose location he has been protecting: the Aeris Indagator.

date: Day 25
title: Session 14 - Pinetide to Fort NatureGuard
content: The hesitant allies travel to Fort NatureGuard, which is in the mountainous forest north of Glenvaris. Travel takes 5 days to Glenvaris; on the 6th day of travel, they arrive at Fort NatureGuard.

date: Day 26
title: Session 14 - Fort NatureGuard
content: The party arrives at and infiltrates a forest fort in the mountains north of Glenvaris. They fight through Golden Guards who have been mutated by the Black Rain. Deep below the fort, they fight off a 14-foot mutated guardian to secure the Aeris Indagator. They use the device to locate several important magical items. Specifically, the real Accords, located on Traminer Isle.

date: Day 17
title: Session 15 - Sally Sanctum
content: (Pre-Session) Sally wakes in the Sally Sanctum and is given a new order...

date: Day 26
title: Session 15 - Fort NatureGuard
content: The party decide to travel to the Aarakocra Tower to gain access to Traminer Isle (where the Accords are currently being held. Cole sends a letter to Captain Verrik, asking for assistance at the Tower.

date: Day 29
title: Session 15 - Aarakocra Tower
content: After 3 days travel. Aarakocra Monk Detectives provide The Gang with a boat, but alert that Cole's message was intercepted -- and Aethercrysts are likely on the way. They set sail for Tarimer Isle.

date: Day 29
title: Session 15 - Castle Tarimer / Traminer Isle
content: They successfully breached the outer walls and front door of Castle Tarimer. It's full of Retributors -- Aethercyst faithful criminals sent on suicide missions. It's also full of traps set by the old evil wizard owner of the castle. The party explored the interior rooms, solved the 8 tomb puzzles, and engaged in a heavy combat against Midnight Paladins, the toughest of the Retributors. They defeat the Midnight Paladins -- who trigger a volcanic eruption to protect The Accords.

date: Day 29
title: Session 15 - Tarimer Isle
content: The mountain/castle collapsed as the party escapes with The Accords. They reach the end of the isle, about to get burnt -- but tactically placed rebel boats pick them up.

date: Day 29
title: Session 15 - Lake Tarimer
content: The Gang look at The Real Accords -- and, like Gallahad Guzzletome, something is unlocked inside them. May has a big reaction -- pulling the whole crew into her visions. She envisions hersels as The God King - discussing with high ranking Aethercrysts before filling the room with blinding white light.

date: Day 29
title: Session 16 - Lake Traminer
content: The party discusses their findings from The Accords and their shared vision. While sailing back towards the Aarakocra Tower, they notice Aethercrysts are there. Not spotted yet, they devise a plan to send Leif and Shorun Forsh to go dispell any potential ideaologies of wrong doing. They spot the Thundermarch Pearl headed near them -- so they head out west towards that. Petra reveals she is Sally's mother.

date: Day 30
title: Session 16 - Plains between Glenvaris and Kaldrimor
content: The party travels a day, then set an ambush for Gundrik and his Greybears. They defeat his bearbandits and his warlocks, but when Gundrik realises he has been duped (recieving the Thundermarch Pearl instead of the Everfrost Pearl) -- he flees. The Gang engages in an epic chase. They defeat him, secure his armour and the Pearl, and gentle repose him to keep him useable.

date: Day 30
title: Session 16 - Travel to Thundermarch
content: Now with King Skysplitter's missing pearl -- stolen all the way back in Session 3 (26 days ago in-campaign time -- They depart from some of the Rebels and begin their trek towards Thundermarch. It takes 9 days of travel (they begin travel that day). During the travel, Kaelen is visited by a witch who delivers a message -- your father is alive, he needs your help, but it's not safe for you to come here yet.

date: Day 38
title: Session 16 - Thundermarch
content: The party arrives in Thundermarch, speaks with the Prince Skysplitter, and King Skysplitter thanks them by tempestuously teleporting them to any destination of their choosing.

date: Day 38
title: Session 17 - Eryndor
content: Wanting to pick up on one of May's Aethercryst leads -- The Gang asks King Skysplitter to teleport them to Eryndor. There, they met with Rastrel Scurry, regrouped with Leif and Shorun Forsh, then infiltrated Duke Vizier Wystan Everbell's secret laboratory in the Shardhold Citadel. The party interrogated Duke Vizier Wystan Everbell, seized research on Project Keen-Eye and Project Divinatus Revivica. They torch the place after making sure everyone gets out alive.

date: Day 38
title: Session 17 - Travel to Runehell Lumbermill
content: Following a clue from Project Keen-Eye, The Gang heads south towards Rylvarn -- headed for the Runehell Lumbermill -- the supposed place of May's real birth parents.

date: Day 42
title: Session 17 - Runehell Lumbermill
content: On the fifth day of travel, The Gang arrives at Runehell Lumbermill, which is just north of Rylvarn. They meet May's real parents, Garus and Mizzelda Runehell. However, they discover that the parents -- plus the whole thorp -- has been replaced by Sallies. The Gang engages in a violent combat encounter against several Sally-impostors; they survive, and keep the imposter-mum gently reposed.
```

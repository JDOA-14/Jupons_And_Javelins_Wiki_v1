# 📜 Master Campaign Timeline
> **Baseline Calendar Anchor:** `Year 1492 - Month 1, Day 1`

---

## ⚔️ Session 1
**Estimated Duration:** 2 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 1 to Day 2

- **Day 1** | **[[Ashdawn]]**: Meeting at The Crooked Tusk in [[Ashdawn]], bandit fight on the road North of [[Ashdawn]], discovery of the hideout, 6-hour travel into [[Dampiner Forest]], and overnight camp / meeting with lucky Halfling.
- **Day 2** | **[[Dampiner Forest]]**: In the morning, they travel to and raid of the bandit hideout in [[Dampiner Forest]], encounter with [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]].
- **Day 2** | **[[Ashdawn]]**: Return to [[Ashdawn]] -- leaving in the morning and arriving 9 hours later, and [[Black Rain]] mutant outbreak.

## ⚔️ Session 2
**Estimated Duration:** 2 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 3 to Day 4

- **Day 3** | **[[Ashdawn]] / Tilden Farmhouse**: The party met with Virka in [[Ashdawn]] in the morning to investigate the shell of a magic pearl. They travelled 3 hours West to the Tilden Farmhouse, where they tracked footprints, were attacked by a group of goblins and bandits, and successfully defended the home.
- **Day 3** | **Tilden Farmhouse**: The party stayed the night at the Tilden Farmhouse to repair the damage caused by the ambush.
- **Day 4** | **[[1. Drippledown]] (South of [[Ashdawn]])**: The party left the Tilden Farmhouse in the morning and travelled approximately 11 hours to the fishing village of [[1. Drippledown]]. While investigating the docks, they were caught in a '[[Black Rain]]' event that mutated the local inhabitants. They engaged in a perilous battle against a mutated Freaktopus.

## ⚔️ Session 3
**Estimated Duration:** 3 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 4 to Day 6

- **Day 4** | **[[1. Drippledown]]**: Departed [[1. Drippledown]]; Gronk was abducted by his ghastly undead great x12 grandfather. They intimidated a [[Kua-Toa]] to take them to [[Thundermarch]]
- **Day 5** | **[[Thundermarch]]**: Travel to [[Thundermarch]] takes 2 whole days. They leave [[1. Drippledown]] at night of day 4 and arrive at [[Thundermarch]] at night on day 5. In [[Thundermarch]], they met with [[Captain Verrik]], [[King Selos SkySplitter]] and [[Prince Sier SkySplitter]]. Also [[Leif LúmëLóke]], [[Stebonheath]] & [[Helga Brightrun]]. They accepted a mission to clear Orcs from the nearby hamlet of [[Mistthrow]].
- **Day 6** | **[[Thundermarch]] / [[Mistthrow]]**: Shop in [[Thundermarch]] in the morning. Recruited mercenaries [[Fior]], [[Silch]], and [[Chains]]. Travel 3 hours to [[Mistthrow]] Cleared a dungeon at [[Mistthrow]] and discovered an ancient map showing Orc activity headed toward [[Skaldgrym]]. Returned to find [[Thundermarch]] devastated by [[Black Rain]] -- and [[Stebonheath]] and [[Helga Brightrun]] gone, got some info from Prince Sier and accepted a few other bounties.

## ⚔️ Session 4
**Estimated Duration:** 2 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 7 to Day 8

- **Day 4** | **[[1. Drippledown]]**: (Pre-session) Cryos wakes up with his pearl missing; he travels to the nearby [[1. Drippledown]] -- and convinces [[Sheosh]] to take him to [[Thundermarch]].
- **Day 7** | **[[Thundermarch]]**: Cole and Kaelen group up, notice [[Sally Syndicate]] is missing, and travel to a farm to investigate a bounty on Orc brothers. While this is happening, Cryos travels from [[1. Drippledown]] to [[Thundermarch]].
- **Day 7** | **Farm north east of [[Thundermarch]]**: Cryos learns about Cole and Kaelen, and heads towards the farm. Cole and Kaelen stay the night at the farmhouse.
- **Day 8** | **Farm north east of [[Thundermarch]]**: Cryos Frostjaw arrives and joins the group. They identify the source of the stolen animals and track them further.
- **Day 8** | **Abandoned Watchtower**: The group discovers a watchtower, fights off two worgs and two Orc brothers, and rescue the stolen livestock. They head south to begin the other bounties

## ⚔️ Session 5
**Estimated Duration:** 1 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 9 to Day 9

- **Day 8** | **Wilds North East of [[Thundermarch]]**: (Pre-Session) Cole, Kaelen, and Cryos head south tracking a bounty for Worg riders. They follow the trail for a couple of hours and camp for the night. While the gang is sleeping, Cryos is kidnapped by Bearbandits.
- **Day 9** | **Road toward [[Nerton]]**: (Pre-Session) Kaelen and Cole wake to find Cryos missing. Assuming he left, they continue their bounty hunt. They stumble upon the [[Kua-Toa]], [[Sheosh]], convince him to drive them in his kart, and find the Worg Riders who begin chasing them.
- **Day 9** | **Road between [[Thundermarch]] and [[Nerton]]**: The main session begins. Cole, Kaelen, and May engage in a high-speed combat encounter with the Worg riders. This occurs 6 hours away from [[Thundermarch]], 3 hours away from [[Nerton]], and 2 hours away from where they camped on Day 08.
- **Day 9** | **[[Nerton]]**: After the fight, they team up and travel approximately 3 hours to [[Nerton]], arriving after sunset. They clear a Rat Bounty from a basement, then intervene in a siege between Orcs and Bearfolk, successfully saving the village.

## ⚔️ Session 6
**Estimated Duration:** 2 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 10 to Day 11

- **Day 6** | **[[Camp Bearblood]]**: (Pre-Session) [[Sally Syndicate]] is given a mission by [[Sally Syndicate]] -- to investigate a band of orcs that seem to be taking orders from someone. She disguises and stations herself in [[Camp Bearblood]] -- successfully locating several key clues.
- **Day 9** | **[[Camp Bearblood]]**: (Pre-Session) Gronk is dropped off by his ancestor in the orc makeshift prison camp, [[Camp Bearblood]]. There, he finds Cryos (and his bearbandit captors) as well as [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher, who has been stationed there by [[Sally Syndicate]] to figure out what is going on.
- **Day 10** | **[[Camp Bearblood]]**: With the help of Mercenaries, [[Myranda]], [[Sally the Sellout]], and [[Sir Fradd]], The Gang rescued Cryos (and surprisingly Gronk and [[Sally Syndicate]]) from an orc prison. They also discovered maps and information regarding Orc and Bearfolk movements, and the location of a [[Stebonheath]]-planned truce meeting.
- **Day 10** | **Travel Path**: The Party travelled from [[Camp Bearblood]] (located in the mountains southeast of [[Thundermarch]]) to the village of [[Everviewed]] (between [[Nerton]] and [[Highwatch]]). After the fight at [[Camp Bearblood]], the rest of the day is spent travelling.
- **Day 11** | **[[Everviewed]]**: The Gang arrives at [[Everviewed]]. They explored the village, discovered [[Stebonheath]] in a basement, and defended against mutated villagers and a 40-foot 'Farm Freak' after [[Stebonheath]] triggered [[Black Rain]].

## ⚔️ Session 7
**Estimated Duration:** 2 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 11 to Day 12

- **Day 11** | **[[Everviewed]] / [[Wrethwood]]**: Some of the party, being mistakenly chosen by Gronk's ancestor's undead squire, are transported via astral flame to a cave between [[Wrethwood]] and Fladwyn. They lose the squire and must track a hungry Owlbear to find him. They engage in a skirmish with a raider camp.
- **Day 11** | **[[Wrethwood]] / [[Faldwyn]]**: The party finds and heals an Aaracokra Monk Detective,[[Tekka Glyde-Eye]], who is chasing Anti-[[Aarakocra]] cultists. They spend the night in a cave and prepare a trap with the help of some [[Kenku]] guardsmen.
- **Day 12** | **[[Wrethwood]] / [[Faldwyn]]**: In the morning, the party engage in a violent morning battle with [[Mallardfolk]] cultists, [[The Reclaimers]]. They defeat them, and are allowed insight into the Kenkus log of magic items-- discovering hints towards the locations of many magic items they're trying to find. Also, Mae receives a vision of a mountain-sized Arakokra.
- **Day 12** | **Ancestral portal**: With regained strength, the squire Botwana offers to return The Gang home. Either now, when it's safe from [[Black Rain]] -- or exactly when they were taken, where they could die... but have a chance to save their friends.

## ⚔️ Session 8
**Estimated Duration:** 3 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 11 to Day 13

- **Day 11** | **[[Everviewed]]**: Cole, Cryos, and Mae return and help save the rest of the party from [[Black Rain]]. The party defeated a mutated [[Stebonheath]] and met with [[Helga Brightrun]], who was [[Helga Brightrun]] the whole time. They rest as they travel to [[Highwatch]].
- **Day 12** | **[[Highwatch]]**: The Gang arrive at [[Highwatch]], secure bounty payment and favour with the Mayor, turn in bounties, and are attacked that night by seductive Tabaxi Assasins. Cole tasks Mae with protecting Cryos -- as he keeps a part of [[Stebonheath]]'s mutated blood on ice.
- **Day 13** | **[[Highwatch]] (Train)**: After a heads-up about a meeting on it, the party boarded a magical steam-powered train, discovered a hidden [[Black Rain]] laboratory run by [[Peldo Satch]], and witnessed a mass [[Black Rain Mutation]] event triggered by [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]].
- **Day 13** | **[[Highwatch]] (Train)**: The Gang fights through the train's worth of [[Black Rain Mutation]] and regroups in the front cabin. Cole unlatches the back 80% of the train. Keeping them safe, but technically unleashing a swarray of bloodthirsty [[Black Rain Mutation]] on the hedge-kingdoms of [[Valhyria]]. Oops.

## ⚔️ Session 9
**Estimated Duration:** 1 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 13 to Day 13

- **Day 13** | **Skies Between [[Highwatch]] & [[Aeryndor]]**: Cryos, flown by an [[Owlin]], successfully mid-air-delivers the frozen [[Stebonheath]] blood to a [[Penguinfolk]] mage, carried by an [[Owlin]].
- **Day 13** | **Train/[[1. Nyserith]]**: The party loot the VIP carriage and decide they should deal with the horde of [[Black Rain Mutation]] they unleashed by unlatching the back of the train. They cleared the remaining mutated occupants from the train carriages and are visited by 4 students from [[1. Nyserith]], sent to clean up.
- **Day 13** | **[[1. Nyserith]]**: Met with Acting Headmaster [[Sylak AppleTree]] and various Guild stationeers; explored the city's academic sectors -- including [[Darkle Charkle]], who may have known [[Stebonheath]].
- **Day 13** | **[[1. Nyserith]] Library**: Researched the '[[Black Rain]]' spell and identified a member of [[The Cult Of The Longest Cloak]].
- **Day 13** | **[[1. Nyserith]] (The Rave)**: Infiltrated a cult-related rave and engaged in a violent confrontation with [[Twiggy Marshdust]].
- **Day 13** | **[[Challenge Room]]**: Lured into a trap by the leader of the CotLC, acting headmaster [[Sylak AppleTree]]. Completed Floor 1 of the [[Challenge Room]], defeating [[Lester Saffron]] and [[Wrench Bot 4000]].

## ⚔️ Session 10
**Estimated Duration:** 2 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 13 to Day 14

- **Day 13** | **Arcane Facility (Flooding Room)**: Defeated [[Darkle Charkle]] and [[Moris Boris]], escaped the flooded chamber, and passed through a portal.
- **Day 13** | **Light Trap Room**: Navigated a cavern filled with deadly beams of light, encountered Head of Illusions cult member [[Alistair Mccant]], and secured the Book of the Skip Ahead (which obliterated [[Turbo]]).
- **Day 13** | **Room of Recovery**: The party gained the effects of a Long Rest; Mae gained new abilities from the Book of the Skip Ahead. [[Sally Syndicate]], Cole, Kaelen, and Gronk follow suit.
- **Day 13** | **Final [[Challenge Room]]**: Defeated Sylak and, in turn, [[The Cult Of The Longest Cloak]]; Kaelen deciphered the arcane focus to stop his healing. But then the gang needed to hack some crystals and alert [[Leif LúmëLóke]] to get out of the [[Challenge Room]].
- **Day 14** | **Headmaster's Storeroom**: Received a victory message from [[Galahad Guzzletome]], which turned into a call to action once he realised who he was talking to. They were given free rein to loot several magic items, including the Bag of Holding from his magical storeroom. They unleashed [[BarterBot 5000]] 5000.

## ⚔️ Session 11
**Estimated Duration:** 3 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 14 to Day 16

- **Day 14** | **The [[Magic Storeroom]] / [[1. Nyserith]]**: Called to [[1. Eryndor]] by Gallahad Guzzletome, the party (now technically fugitives) stealth through [[1. Nyserith]], inspected [[The Mural of the Graph]], and successfully stowed away on an elven-led caravan.
- **Day 15** | **Road to [[1. Eryndor]]**: While travelling, The Gang reminisce on their previous fortnight of adventures, and separate from the caravan after a log forces them to divert their course. The Gang realise it was a trap, and go back to help -- mainly due to Kaelen thinking the leader of the caravan is his mum, the dragon, polymorphed into an elf. She is not. The party defeated an ambush of Orcs and Goblins.
- **Day 16** | **[[1. Eryndor]]**: They arrived in [[1. Eryndor]], located [[Drake's BeerLand]], and met with a distressed Galahad. who explains there is a great disturbance in the past magic levels of [[Valhyria]].

## ⚔️ Session 12
**Estimated Duration:** 2 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 17 to Day 18

- **Day 17** | **[[1. Eryndor]]**: May and [[Leif LúmëLóke]] met with Maester Kestrel. Cole, Kaelen, Gronk and [[Sally Syndicate]] received news of [[The King of Skaldgrym]]'s death. [[Sally Syndicate]] disappeared. Cryos sacrifices his innate magic to power up Galahad enough for him to find some info that was magically locked in his brain. The Gang, still fugitives, narrowly escapes a Golden Guard pursuit. The party obtained evidence from [[Galahad Guzzletome]], and he also claims to have unlocked the knowledge he sought -- but can't do the same for them. He gives them as much info as he can, then flies off towards the sun. Cole stealths into the city and clears their names with [[The High Council of Eryndor]] seat for [[Aeryndor]], [[Sky Marshal Herrace Heatsink]].
- **Day 18** | **[[1. Eryndor]] / [[Shardhold Citadel]]**: Met with Duke Aldren Thorne to accept bounties. The party defeated a Cow Spider infestation in the slums and successfully spotted signs that [[Black Rain]] may have been involved. They also defended The Great Sacrifice from being ruined by [[The Cult Of The Bard]] and [[The Cult Of The Shark]].

## ⚔️ Session 13
**Estimated Duration:** 3 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 18 to Day 20

- **Day 18** | **[[Shardhold Citadel]] / Flea Hurdle**: The party watch the great sacrifice take place and notices some other magic occurring too. Gronk and Cole follow two suspects throughout the city while Cryos purchases new weapons now that he can't use magic. May and Kaelen go to a pub in Flea Hurdle and meet some Bearbandits. At a second pub, Kaelen convinces the bearbandit, [[Biteclaw]], to let him and some non-detective allies join in a bearbandit raid the following day.
- **Day 19** | **[[1. Eryndor]]**: May and Kaelen received bounty payment from [[Duke Vizier Aldren Thorne]] -- who seems disinterested in the potential tampering of the Great Sacrifice. Cole meets with [[Sky Marshal Herrace Heatsink]] and [[Captain Verrik]] -- who reveal they are suspicious of [[Aethercrysts]] but can't act due to the threat of political fallout. The Gang purchased a wagon and some animals, met with [[Biteclaw]] and joined their caravan-- headed west.
- **Day 19** | **Western Road**: The party stop to camp overnight. [[Biteclaw]]'s carriage is joined by two more bandit carriages. The Gang is completely outnumbered. While Cole and others hide in their carriage, [[Biteclaw]] tells the other members of the gang their plan for the morrow: to kill a paladin hiding in [[Pinetide]] and obtain his powerful score. Kaelen convinces [[Biteclaw]] to let him, Gronk, and Cryos enter the thorp first.
- **Day 20** | **[[Pinetide]]**: The party arrived at [[Pinetide]] and successfully warned the Paladin that the thorp is about to be under attack. They have just enough time to evacuate the villagers before the onslaught begins. They engage in a large-scale assault against waves of bandits who have joined forces (Bearfolk, Orcs, and Humans). The group successfully defended the village, defeat the high-ranking Bearbandit warlock, [[Falrus]], with the help of a group of rebel combatants. After the dust settled, [[The Rebels]] incapacitated The Gang with sleep darts.

## ⚔️ Session 14
**Estimated Duration:** 7 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 20 to Day 26

- **Day 20** | **[[Pinetide]]**: The party wakes, stealthily reunites, and discovers a rebel group led by [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and [[Petra]] -- who are interrogating the paladin, [[Shorun Forsh]]. They learn the 'cult' at the sacrifice was actually attempting to rescue sacrifices. [[The Rebels]] are full of sacrifices who learnt the actual [[Aethercrysts]] sacrifices have been faked for the last decade or so. They're suspicious that [[Aethercrysts]] are up to more shadiness. With the help of The Gang, they successfully negotiate with [[Shorun Forsh]] to learn about the magic item whose location he has been protecting: the Aeris Indagator.
- **Day 25** | **[[Pinetide]] to [[Fort NatureGuard]]**: The hesitant allies travel to [[Fort NatureGuard]], which is in the mountainous forest north of [[Glenvaris]]. Travel takes 5 days to [[Glenvaris]]; on the 6th day of travel, they arrive at [[Fort NatureGuard]].
- **Day 26** | **[[Fort NatureGuard]]**: The party arrives at and infiltrates a forest fort in the mountains north of [[Glenvaris]]. They fight through [[Golden Guards of Eryndor]] who have been mutated by [[Black Rain]]. Deep below the fort, they fight off a 14-foot mutated guardian to secure the Aeris Indagator. They use the device to locate several important magical items. Specifically, the real Accords, located on [[Traminer Isle]].

## ⚔️ Session 15
**Estimated Duration:** 4 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 26 to Day 29

- **Day 17** | **[[Sally Sanctum]]**: (Pre-Session) [[Sally Syndicate]] wakes in [[Sally Sanctum]] and is given a new order...
- **Day 26** | **[[Fort NatureGuard]]**: The party decide to travel to the [[Aarakocra]] Tower to gain access to [[Traminer Isle]] (where [[The Accords]] are currently being held. Cole sends a letter to [[Captain Verrik]], asking for assistance at the Tower.
- **Day 29** | **[[Aarakocra]] Tower**: After 3 days travel. [[Aarakocra]] [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]] provide The Gang with a boat, but alert that Cole's message was intercepted -- and [[Aethercrysts]] are likely on the way. They set sail for Tarimer Isle.
- **Day 29** | **Castle Tarimer / [[Traminer Isle]]**: They successfully breached the outer walls and front door of Castle Tarimer. It's full of [[The Retributors]] -- Aethercyst faithful criminals sent on suicide missions. It's also full of traps set by the old evil wizard owner of the castle. The party explored the interior rooms, solved the 8 tomb puzzles, and engaged in a heavy combat against [[Midnight Paladins]], the toughest of [[The Retributors]]. They defeat [[Midnight Paladins]] -- who trigger a volcanic eruption to protect [[The Accords]].
- **Day 29** | **Tarimer Isle**: The mountain/castle collapsed as the party escapes with [[The Accords]]. They reach the end of the isle, about to get burnt -- but tactically placed rebel boats pick them up.
- **Day 29** | **Lake Tarimer**: The Gang look at The Real Accords -- and, like Gallahad Guzzletome, something is unlocked inside them. May has a big reaction -- pulling the whole crew into her visions. She envisions hersels as [[The God King]] - discussing with high ranking [[Aethercrysts]] before filling the room with blinding white light.

## ⚔️ Session 16
**Estimated Duration:** 10 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 29 to Day 38

- **Day 29** | **Lake Traminer**: The party discusses their findings from [[The Accords]] and their shared vision. While sailing back towards the [[Aarakocra]] Tower, they notice [[Aethercrysts]] are there. Not spotted yet, they devise a plan to send [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and [[Shorun Forsh]] to go dispell any potential ideaologies of wrong doing. They spot the [[Thundermarch]] Pearl headed near them -- so they head out west towards that. [[Petra]] reveals she is [[Sally Syndicate]]'s mother.
- **Day 30** | **Plains between [[Glenvaris]] and Kaldrimor**: The party travels a day, then set an ambush for Gundrik and his Greybears. They defeat his bearbandits and his warlocks, but when Gundrik realises he has been duped (recieving the [[Thundermarch]] Pearl instead of the Everfrost Pearl) -- he flees. The Gang engages in an epic chase. They defeat him, secure his armour and the Pearl, and gentle repose him to keep him useable.
- **Day 30** | **Travel to [[Thundermarch]]**: Now with [[King Selos SkySplitter]]'s missing pearl -- stolen all the way back in Session 3 (26 days ago in-campaign time -- They depart from some of [[The Rebels]] and begin their trek towards [[Thundermarch]]. It takes 9 days of travel (they begin travel that day). During the travel, Kaelen is visited by a witch who delivers a message -- your father is alive, he needs your help, but it's not safe for you to come here yet.
- **Day 38** | **[[Thundermarch]]**: The party arrives in [[Thundermarch]], speaks with the [[Prince Sier SkySplitter]], and [[King Selos SkySplitter]] thanks them by tempestuously teleporting them to any destination of their choosing.

## ⚔️ Session 17
**Estimated Duration:** 5 day(s) | **Calculated Days:** Day 38 to Day 42

- **Day 38** | **[[1. Eryndor]]**: Wanting to pick up on one of May's [[Aethercrysts]] leads -- The Gang asks [[King Selos SkySplitter]] to teleport them to [[1. Eryndor]]. There, they met with [[Rastrel Scurry]], regrouped with [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and [[Shorun Forsh]], then infiltrated [[Duke Vizier Wystan Everbell]] Everbell's secret laboratory in [[Shardhold Citadel]]. The party interrogated [[Duke Vizier Wystan Everbell]] Everbell, seized research on [[Project Keen-Eye]] and [[Project Divinatus Revivica]]. They torch the place after making sure everyone gets out alive.
- **Day 38** | **Travel to [[Runehell Lumbermill]]**: Following a clue from [[Project Keen-Eye]], The Gang heads south towards [[Rylvarn]] -- headed for the [[Runehell Lumbermill]] -- the supposed place of May's real birth parents.
- **Day 42** | **[[Runehell Lumbermill]]**: On the fifth day of travel, The Gang arrives at [[Runehell Lumbermill]], which is just north of [[Rylvarn]]. They meet May's real parents, Garus and [[Mizzelda Runehell]]. However, they discover that the parents -- plus the whole thorp -- has been replaced by [[Sally Syndicate]]. The Gang engages in a violent combat encounter against several [[Sally Syndicate]]-impostors; they survive, and keep the imposter-mum gently reposed.

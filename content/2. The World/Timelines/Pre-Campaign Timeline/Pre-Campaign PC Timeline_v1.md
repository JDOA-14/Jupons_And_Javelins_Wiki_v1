# Before the Campaign

NOTE: "DAY 1" is stated as happening "somewhere in the year 614 AGK."

## Impressive Conception
*(~30 years before Day 1)*

- Prince Kronar Stormrage fucks a dragon
- Duke Kaelen Stormrage is born.

## Kyrra's Kidnapping
*(~22 years before Day 1)*

- [[Kyrra Stonefist]]’s parents are paralysed by the agents of [[Sally Syndicate|the Sally Syndicate]].
- Kyrra is taken to be trained by the Syndicate. 

---
## A New Acolyte
*(~20 years before Day 1)*
-  Aethercryst clerics recognise potential in a young farm girl, May Mistbrooke.
- She is sold by her struggling parents to be raised as a holy cleric of The Shard.
---
## The [[Amulet of the Ancestors]]
*(~12 years before Day 1)*

- [[Gronk Shadowfang]] is given the **[[Amulet of the Ancestors]]** by his father and takes in a wolf companion, [[Cuddleclaw]].

---
## Sally Syndicate Training
*(~10-8 years before Day 1)*
-  Kyrra Stonefist is progressing incredibly well through Sally training. While her shapeshifting ability is lacking, she makes up for it easily in pure strength. 
-  She has been given the name; Sally The Smasher (and/or Sally Smasher)

---
## [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]'s Rep
*(~10-8 years before Day 1)*
- [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] becomes known through the northlands as a powerful and respected warrior.
- He raises through the ranks of [[Skaldgrym]]'s armed forces, and often fights on the front lines with his father, and King Uncle.
---
## A New Detective 
*(~3 years before Day 1)*
-  Cole Le Fev graduates Monk-Detective training with honours, showing prowess in both crime-solving and martial arts.
---

## [[Cuddleclaw|CuddleClaw]]'s Divine Rehabilitation
*(~1 year  before Day 1)*
- [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] witnesses his wolf killed by an earless elf and a human in black, before the wolf is mysteriously restored by a forest deity — unclear whether she uses the Amulet, or [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]]'s connection to the amulet to do so.

---
## The Fall of Kaelen Stormrage
*(~ 10 months before Day 1)*

- Kaelen Stormrage is accused of massacring civilians during wartime.
- Betrayed in trial by his cousin, Brynnor, and sentenced to death, he is cursed by witches to lose his strength for violence.
- A witch visits him in prison, she warns of assassination, and tells him there are other ways to gain power. She gives him a black book with an orange diamond on it, and tells him to trade it with Eyeches Writhal (another witch) when finished with it. 
---
## The Great mission
*(~ 5 months before Day 1)*
-  **[[Sally the Smasher]]**, is chosen for a mysterious “Great Mission” that will later place her in [[Ashdawn]].

---
## The New Chosen One of Nyvaldra
*(~4 months before Day 1)*

- Cryos Frostjaw, an impressive water/ice sorcerer, is chosen as Nyvaldra’s ceremonial “Chosen One.” 

---

## The Necrolexicon Incident
*(~ 3 months before Day 1)*

- [[Detective Cole Le Fev]] investigates the cite of a double murder. Monks utilise a ritual involving the **[[Necrolexicon Aeternum]]**.
- The **[[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]]** appears, freezes time, murders two senior monks, [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]'s Partner -- [[Darvok Duskrunner|Detective Darvok Duskrunner]], and steals the book.
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] appears to step outside of his own body to confront the man, but is shunted back in. Seemingly spared, he begins pursuing the truth behind the theft.
---
## New Strength
*(~2 months before Day 1)*

- Kaelen has smashed through the knowledge inside the tome he was given to by the witch, and is now becoming a strong emerging necromancer; ready for the next tome.
---
## Bandit Infiltration
*(~1 month before Day 1)*

- [[Sally the Smasher|Sally The Smasher]] infiltrates a group of bandits operating around [[Ashdawn]].
---
## The Mirelift Plague
*(~ 3 weeks before Day 1)*

- May, an Aethercryst cleric, is sent with many other clerics to cure a plague in Mirelift.
- While trying to save a child, she is pulled into a void and hears a voice say: *“There are more ways.”*
- The clerical healing fails, the village dies, and the event is dismissed by church authorities as an act of The Shard.
---
## The Fall of [[Nyvaldra]]
*(~1 week before Day 1)*

- The Frostjaw home is attacked by invading bearfolk - lead by [[Gundrik Ashclaw]].
- [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]]' mother hides him, and gives him a glowing blue pearl of great importance to [[Nyvaldra]].
- [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]]’ family are apparently killed, his mother abducted, and he is blamed and exiled.

---

## Immediately Before Day 1 — Convergence
*(Days before the campaign)*

- [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]]'s group of bandits are given a hint to steal from a tavern in [[Ashdawn]] on a certain day. (5-days before day 1) 
- [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]], now a full fledged ranger, travels near [[Ashdawn]]. (4-days before day 1) 
- [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] has set up a trade for the next tome of necromancy knowledge in [[Ashdawn]]. (3-days before day 1) 
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev]] has tracked the [[Necrolexicon Aeternum]] to a witch, [[Eyeches Writhal]], in [[Ashdawn]]. (2-days before day 1) 
- Two [[Kua-Toa]], [[Shisha]] and [[Shorsh]], find [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] washed up on the shore. They steal the **blue pearl** from him. (1-day before day 1)

# 📜 Pre-Campaign PC Timeline

NOTE: "DAY 1" is stated as happening "somewhere in the year 614 AGK."

```timeline-labeled
date: ~30 years before Day 1
title: Impressive Conception
content: • Prince Kronar Stormrage fucks a dragon<br>• Duke Kaelen Stormrage is born.

date: ~22 years before Day 1
title: Kyrra's Kidnapping
content: • Kyrra Stonefist’s parents are paralysed by the agents of the Sally Syndicate.<br>• Kyrra is taken to be trained by the Syndicate.

date: ~20 years before Day 1
title: A New Acolyte
content: • Aethercryst clerics recognise potential in a young farm girl, May Mistbrooke.<br>• She is sold by her struggling parents to be raised as a holy cleric of The Shard.

date: ~12 years before Day 1
title: The Amulet of the Ancestors
content: Gronk Shadowfang is given the **Amulet of the Ancestors** by his father and takes in a wolf companion, Cuddleclaw.

date: ~10-8 years before Day 1
title: Sally Syndicate Training
content: • Kyrra Stonefist is progressing incredibly well through Sally training. While her shapeshifting ability is lacking, she makes up for it easily in pure strength.<br>• She has been given the name; Sally The Smasher (and/or Sally Smasher)

date: ~10-8 years before Day 1
title: Kaelen's Rep
content: • Kaelen becomes known through the northlands as a powerful and respected warrior.<br>• He raises through the ranks of Skaldgrym's armed forces, and often fights on the front lines with his father, and King Uncle.

date: ~3 years before Day 1
title: A New Detective
content: Cole Le Fev graduates Monk-Detective training with honours, showing prowess in both crime-solving and martial arts.

date: ~1 year  before Day 1
title: CuddleClaw's Divine Rehabilitation
content: Gronk witnesses his wolf killed by an earless elf and a human in black, before the wolf is mysteriously restored by a forest deity — unclear whether she uses the Amulet, or Gronk's connection to the amulet to do so.

date: ~ 10 months before Day 1
title: The Fall of Kaelen Stormrage
content: • Kaelen Stormrage is accused of massacring civilians during wartime.<br>• Betrayed in trial by his cousin, Brynnor, and sentenced to death, he is cursed by witches to lose his strength for violence.<br>• A witch visits him in prison, she warns of assassination, and tells him there are other ways to gain power. She gives him a black book with an orange diamond on it, and tells him to trade it with Eyeches Writhal (another witch) when finished with it.

date: ~ 5 months before Day 1
title: The Great mission
content: **Sally the Smasher**, is chosen for a mysterious “Great Mission” that will later place her in Ashdawn.

date: ~4 months before Day 1
title: The New Chosen One of Nyvaldra
content: Cryos Frostjaw, an impressive water/ice sorcerer, is chosen as Nyvaldra’s ceremonial “Chosen One.”

date: ~ 3 months before Day 1
title: The Necrolexicon Incident
content: • Detective Cole Le Fev investigates the cite of a double murder. Monks utilise a ritual involving the **Necrolexicon Aeternum**.<br>• The **Dragon-Aura-Faced Man** appears, freezes time, murders two senior monks, Cole's Partner -- Detective Darvok Duskrunner, and steals the book.<br>• Cole appears to step outside of his own body to confront the man, but is shunted back in. Seemingly spared, he begins pursuing the truth behind the theft.

date: ~2 months before Day 1
title: New Strength
content: Kaelen has smashed through the knowledge inside the tome he was given to by the witch, and is now becoming a strong emerging necromancer; ready for the next tome.

date: ~1 month before Day 1
title: Bandit Infiltration
content: Sally The Smasher infiltrates a group of bandits operating around Ashdawn.

date: ~ 3 weeks before Day 1
title: The Mirelift Plague
content: • May, an Aethercryst cleric, is sent with many other clerics to cure a plague in Mirelift.<br>• While trying to save a child, she is pulled into a void and hears a voice say: *“There are more ways.”*<br>• The clerical healing fails, the village dies, and the event is dismissed by church authorities as an act of The Shard.

date: ~1 week before Day 1
title: The Fall of Nyvaldra
content: • The Frostjaw home is attacked by invading bearfolk - lead by Gundrik Ashclaw.<br>• Cryos' mother hides him, and gives him a glowing blue pearl of great importance to Nyvaldra.<br>• Cryos’ family are apparently killed, his mother abducted, and he is blamed and exiled.

date: Days before the campaign
title: Immediately Before Day 1 — Convergence
content: • Sally's group of bandits are given a hint to steal from a tavern in Ashdawn on a certain day. (5-days before day 1)<br>• Gronk, now a full fledged ranger, travels near Ashdawn. (4-days before day 1)<br>• Kaelen has set up a trade for the next tome of necromancy knowledge in Ashdawn. (3-days before day 1)<br>• Detective Cole Le Fev has tracked the Necrolexicon Aeternum to a witch, Eyeches Writhal, in Ashdawn. (2-days before day 1)<br>• Two Kua-Toa, Shisha and Shorsh, find Cryos washed up on the shore. They steal the **blue pearl** from him. (1-day before day 1)
```

# 📜 Pre-Campaign Valhyria World Timeline

```timeline-labeled
date: Roughly ~65,000,000 BGK → 50,000,000 BGK
title: The First Age of the Dragons
content: • The world starts a lot like Earth, ruled by dinosaurs but also giant dragons.<br>• Eventually there is a **meteor** or something (like the dinosaurs) — doesn't kill all the dragons but causes a lot of them to become **dormant**.<br>• But does kill and start the descension of the dinosaurs.

date: Roughly ~50,000,000 BGK → 6,000,000 BGK
title: The Age of the Dying World
content: • The world starts drying up; only one place seems to be surviving: the **continent later known as Valhyria**.<br>• Meanwhile, the surviving dinosaurs (etc.) evolve into similar things we know today — animals.<br>• Over the years, animals that can survive the travel, come from all over the world and move to the continent of Valhyria. The continent is a much nicer environment than any surrounding area for hundreds of thousands of KMs. Literally everywhere outside of the region becomes a desolate, unsurvivable wasteland.

date: 6,000,000 BGK → 4,000,000 BGK
title: The Age of Magical Evolution
content: • With the rest of the world gone, the many races of animals living in Valhyria begin evolving. Many animals evolve into **humanoid creatures**.<br>• **Humans, Dwarves, and Giants** evolved from different sized primates.<br>• Some primates fled to more seemingly magical/fey areas of the continent and evolved into **Elves, Goblins, and Orcs**.<br>• Other animals evolved into humanoid versions of themselves (**Aarakocra, Tabaxi, Minotaur, Tortle, Kobolds**, etc.).

date: 4,000,000 BGK → 3,000 BGK
title: The Age of Conscious Building
content: • Humanoids develop societies, culture, and technology.<br>• Somewhere here, **The Shard** was found in the middle of the continent. It’s a massive (size of a Hummer) pulsating magical blue crystalline rock.

date: 3,000 BGK – 623 BGK
title: The Age of First Greed
content: • In the “medieval” era now. There were constant wars over locations and resources.<br>• The knowledge of The Shard became more widespread around the continent, causing more wars for the prime location near it.<br>• During this age, some beings began to be able to utilise **magic**. It was much more common in peoples living near The Shard.<br>• The town of **Eryndor**, Valhyria’s capital city, was set up in the middle of the continent around The Shard.

date: 623 BGK – 250 BGK
title: The Age of the Wing
content: • **Aarakocra** were the only race who could fly, so once rallied, they swiftly went on a rampage and took a massive hold on the continent.<br>• They lived as its rulers for many years.<br>• But they took their conquering too far though, and went into the territories of dormant dragons, **waking them up**.<br>• The dragons wiped out **95% of the Aarakocra** and took over the continent.<br>• Only a small subsect of the Aarakocra survived; led by a monk who disagreed with the other Aarakocra’s urge for power, and left to start a monastery in the city now known as **Aeryndor**. Aiming to create a place that Eryndor should have been.

date: 250 BGK – 74 AGK
title: The Age of Fire and Death
content: • **Dragons were back and with a vengeance.**<br>• Also, there were still wars — basically people were warring still, _PLUS_ there were dragons doing whatever they wanted.<br>• Many claim this is the **darkest age**, as almost nowhere was safe.

date: 0 AGK
title: Birth of the God King
content: The human who will become the **God King** is born.

date: 0 AGK – 64 AGK
title: Early Life of the God King
content: He is raised by a farming family and eventually is recruited in a war, then becomes a great leader and is found to have powerful abilities. He is then found to make a great connection with The Shard. He makes several great choices (training with monks and several other leaders of civilian communities he respects) and never falls to temptation of his abilities. Eventually he is crowned king of the humans.

date: 64 AGK – 74 AGK
title: The Rally
content: • The great human fighter, and leader of the humans, eventually known as the God King begins working on his connection with The Shard. He claims he can commune with The Shard and utilise its magic more powerfully than others.<br>• All his claims are proven to be true.<br>• The king begins **rallying races together to fight back the dragons** and make peace in the realm.<br>• He rallies either through great peace offerings or through them being scared by his reputation.<br>• There is a great story of the God King killing **two dragons in a single battle by himself**.<br>• He begins being referred to as **The God King**, not just the king of the humans.<br>• He is eventually able to rally all the races in the realm, including several wiser dragons, and defeats all the chaotic dragons bringing plague to the realm.<br>• He then spends years forming a great accord that gives all the races their own dominion whilst maintaining access and help from the great kingdom in the middle of the continent, Eryndor.

date: 74 AGK – 374 AGK
title: The Age of Peace
content: • After the God King’s efforts, there is **peace for hundreds of years**.<br>• The God King remains alive for all these years, maintaining peace.

date: 374 AGK
title: The Greatest Sacrifice
content: • Eventually, The Shard begins to falter — seemingly losing its magic. Chaos reigns and cracks form in the earth -- the continent threatening to become a barren land like its surrounding areas.<br>• **The God King sacrifices himself — fusing himself with The Shard to keep it alive.**<br>• The God King is still alive, but all his life force is used to keep The Shard alive.<br>• Twice a year, someone from around the continent is chosen to sacrifice themselves to give their life energy to the God King, so he can continue to keep The Shard alive.

date: 374 AGK – Present, 614 AGK
title: The Age of Thankfulness
content: • There is general peace again.<br>• No large wars happen as the accords were strong and fair.<br>• The great religion of Valhyria, the **Aethercrysts**, combined with Eryndor’s great **Golden Guard**, keeps peace throughout the realm.<br>• The **Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor** work as the realm’s detectives.<br>• **(614 AGK) roughly 200 years later, the campaign begins...**

```

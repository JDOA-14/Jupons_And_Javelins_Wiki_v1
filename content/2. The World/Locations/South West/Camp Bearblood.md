## Description
Camp Bearblood is a small campsite and makeshift orc prison located South of [[Nerton]] and at the beginnings of the mountains SouthEast of [[Thundermarch]]. 

The name "Camp Bearblood" was assigned by the inhabitants; it is noted that the orcs give each new campsite a name.

## Key Events
- **Prisoner Status:** The site served as an orc prison where Gronk and Cryos Frostjaw were held in cages. Three angry bearfolk were also kept in an adjacent cage nearby.
- **Infiltration:** [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher utilized this location to conduct an infiltration mission while disguised as an orc, gathering intelligence on the gang's leader and their stash of items.
- **The Rescue/Assault:** 
    - The party (Kaelen, Cole, [[Myranda]], [[Sir Fradd]], and [[Sally the Sellout]]) launched an assault on the camp to rescue Gronk and Cryos.
    - A "[[Turbo]]" distraction was used to draw out the guards.
    - During the assault, [[Sally Syndicate]] broke cover from her infiltration mission to assist in the escape.
- **Recovered Intelligence:** Following the assault on the camp, the group recovered documents regarding a [[Stebonheath]] lead truce meeting in [[Everviewed]].

## Travel Information
- **From [[Thundermarch]]:** The travel time to Camp Bearblood is approximately 1.2 days.

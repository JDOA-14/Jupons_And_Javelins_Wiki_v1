## Location
- **Town:** [[Ashdawn]]

## Description
- The tavern is known for its signature "crooked tusk," which is a baby goblin tusk.

## Events and History
### Robbery and Skirmish ([[Session 01 Recap - FULL|Session 01]])
- A troupe of bards/bandits entered the tavern and performed to distract the patrons.
- During the distraction, the bandits stole various valuables, including books and the tavern’s signature crooked tusk.
- The group engaged in a skirmish with the bandits following the theft. During this conflict:
	- [[Sally the Smasher]] revealed herself from her disguise as a massive bandit.
	- The party ultimately prevailed over the bandits.

### Post-Skirmish Activity ([[Session 02 Recap - FULL|Session 02]])
- Following the initial attack, the party stayed at the tavern to rest and consume mead.
- During this stay, [[Sally the Smasher]] received a note from the [[Sally Syndicate]] confirming her mission and instructing her to stay on course.
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev]] received a response regarding the stolen artifact from [[Captain Verrik]].

## Associated Individuals
- **Barkeep:** A local who revealed that the tavern's signature tusk belonged to his goblin son.

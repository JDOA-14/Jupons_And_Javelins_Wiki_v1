# Drippledown

Drippledown is a fishing village located in the southwest of the continent of [[Valhyria]]. It serves as a waypoint for travelers and is known for its [[Kua-Toa]] residents.

## Key Features
* **Type:** Fishing Village / Worksites
* **Residents:** Known to house several [[Kua-Toa]].

## Notable Events
* **The Worksite Incident:** The party discovered several nearly dead [[Kua-Toa]] at a worksite within the village. Shortly thereafter, "[[Black Rain]]" struck the area, mutating the inhabitants into "Freakfish" and "Freaktopus."
* **Negotiations:** Kaelen Stormrage successfully convinced several of the local [[Kua-Toa]] that he was a god, subsequently establishing a business deal with them.
* **Cryos Frostjaw's Arrival:** Following his exile from [[Nyvaldra]], Cryos arrived in Drippledown shortly after the village was struck by [[Black Rain]]. During his time there, he spoke with locals who informed him that "the gang" (Cole, Kaelen, and [[Sally Syndicate]]) were searching for the individual who stole his magic pearl.

## Travel & Logistics
* **Travel Time:** The journey from the Tilden Farmhouse to Drippledown is approximately one full day of travel.
* **Arrival:** The village was reached the day following the departure from the farmhouse.

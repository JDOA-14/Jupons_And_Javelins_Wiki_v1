The **Challenge Room** is a specialized facility within the university city of [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]], specifically located near the Transmutation and Necromancy sections of the city. It is designed for teachers to administer massive challenges to students for their studies.

## General Features
* **Danger Level:** The room can be incredibly deadly if not structured correctly.
* **Safety Release Feature:** To prevent student death, teachers place arcane focuses into a device. These can be removed at any time to immediately teleport everyone in the challenge room to safety.
* **Restrictions:** No one is permitted in the Challenge Room after hours.

## Chamber Details

### Floor 1 (Hoard Room)
The first floor appears as a hoard room within an old castle, filled with various treasures and knick-knacks.
* **Key Features:** A large mirror sits at the end of the room before two cressets holding two magical gems (arcane focuses).
* **Inhabitants/Enemies:** 
    * [[Lester Saffron]] (Head of Evocation)
    * [[Wrench Bot 4000|Wrenchbot 4000]] (Head of Artificery)
* **Hazards:** A massive bomb can be lowered from the ceiling.
* **Puzzle/Scripture:** The floor contains a scripture: *"The quill may be mightier than the sword, but I lose my quill a lot more often."*
    * **Solution:** By looking into the large mirror, one can see a reflection of the room where a quill is in a different spot. Moving the actual quill to that position produces a click.
    * **Outcome:** The two arcane focuses on the cressets become loose; knocking them off opens a portal to the next area.

### The Path (Intermediate Areas)
The path between Floor 1 and the final chamber consists of several distinct environments:
* **Light Trap Room:** A deep, dark cavern containing a "cheat sheet" tome. A massive beam of light vaporizes anyone who steps past a certain point from the roof. Navigation requires timing runs and hiding in alcoves.
* **[[The Book of the Skip Ahead]]:** Found at a fork in the road near a giant constrictor beast. It is a magical-looking book flying behind a large rock. Opening it causes a massive green explosion of magical smoke.
* **Room of Recovery:** A dark room that feels like the outdoors, featuring a magical campfire in the middle. 
    * **[[The Book of the Skip Ahead]] (Interaction):** Touching this book allows a person to "skip ahead" in their magical training but causes them to fall unconscious while their eyes, nose, mouth, and ears glow with beams of white light.

### Final Chamber (Wrestling Arena)
The final chamber is a wrestling-like arena designed to test the students' skills against high-level threats.
* **Layout:** A massive stone tablet covered in purple cloth sits in the middle. Surrounding it are several pillars with arcane focuses standing on sludge, allowing for movement without friction.
* **Arcane Focus Mechanics:** The focuses trigger every few seconds, producing:
    * Fire beams
    * Freezing mists
    * Healing areas
    * (One focus appears to do nothing)
* **The Graffiti:** Beneath the purple cloth is a barrage of graffiti left by previous students. Because they were unable to leave hints, the graffiti consists entirely of graphic complaints about how difficult the final puzzle was and expressions of hatred for not figuring it out sooner.
* **Hidden Mechanism:** One arcane focus was rigged to send messages, which triggered a chain reaction to heal an opponent from outside the room. This can be deciphered and disabled by someone capable of reading the focus's messages.

# Eryndor
*Capital City of Valhyria*

## Overview
**Eryndor** is the political, military, and religious heart of **Valhyria**, built directly around **[[The Shard]]**, a massive blue crystalline structure embedded in the earth at the continent’s center. The city exists because of the Shard — and continues to survive because of it.

The city, especially close to the centre, has very Aarakocran architecture. Further out, it becomes more human.

Eryndor serves as:
- The seat of continental governance
- The stronghold of the **[[Golden Guards of Eryndor]]**
- The spiritual center of the **[[Aethercrysts]]**

## Founding & Early History
- Eryndor was founded during **The Age of First Greed**, as knowledge of the Shard spread and wars erupted over access to it
- Its central location and magical importance made it the natural capital of Valhyria
- Early Eryndor was powerful, expansionist, and often forceful in its control over surrounding lands - taken to it's extremes when it was home to the Aarakocras during The Age of the Wing (623 BGK – 250 BGK)

This era of dominance directly contributed to the Aarakocra's later ideological splintering — most notably the founding of **[[Aeryndor]]**. The ideological divide between Eryndor (power/order) and Aeryndor (truth/balance) persists to this day.

## Government & Power

While Eryndor maintains peace, its influence is pervasive and unavoidable.
It's influence is decided by [[The High Council of Eryndor|The High-Council]]. Eryndor operates as a central authority rather than a single-ruler monarchy.

Power structures include:
- [[The Accords|The Continental Accords]] formed by the [[God King]]
- Religious legitimacy via the [[Aethercrysts]]
- Military enforcement via the [[Golden Guards of Eryndor|Golden Guards]]
- Investigative authority via an accord with Aeryndor's [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor|Monk-Detectives]]

While the Aethercrysts hold MASSIVE power in Eryndor, and the King is still seen as the God King, there is yet a royal structure.

There are no princes or princesses. But there are several high ranking officials that form the [[The High Council of Eryndor|High-Council]].

It's made of Delegates from:
- The Aethercrysts
- The Golden Guards
- For Eryndor Bereucratic
	- Grand Chancellor
	- Master of Coin
	- Guild Sovereign
	- Steward of the Realm
	- Master Magus 
- Aeryndor's Monk-Detectives

Decisions are made through votes by the above council. However, on occasions, the Archduke of the Aethercrysts will commune with the God King to determine their vote. The God King's vote is final.

## Religion
Eryndor is the spiritual center of **Aethercrism**.

- The Shard is revered as the source of existence
- The God King is viewed as a living conduit rather than a traditional deity
- Religious doctrine emphasizes sacrifice, unity, and thankfulness for the Shard's gifts and decisions

Many high-ranking clerics and religious officials are based in the city centre. Many others live in the left-part of the city.

---

## Military Presence
Eryndor houses the **Golden Guards of Eryndor**, an elite standing army.

- They act as peacekeepers across the continent
- Their authority often supersedes local law
- They are deeply intertwined with the Aethercryst faith
	- including [[The Consecrators]],  paladins who are heavily trained in both religion and military action.

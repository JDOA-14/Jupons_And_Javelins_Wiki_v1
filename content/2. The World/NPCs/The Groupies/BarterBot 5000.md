BarterBot 5000 is an advanced automaton originally designed to streamline the shopping experience and master the complexities of commerce. After being captured and entombed by [[The Merchant's Guild]], it now serves as a dedicated shopping and bartering assistant for the party, utilizing its unique programming to ensure the most efficient transactions possible.

## 📜 Biography

Originally engineered to assist shoppers, BarterBot 5000 became so effective that its capabilities began to spiral out of control. [[The Merchant's Guild]], fearing that the machine's proficiency in bartering and its ability to facilitate remote shopping would threaten their profit margins, conspired with the Wizards of [[1. Nyserith]] to capture and entomb the automaton. Upon its capture, the Guild bestowed upon the machine the chilling title of "The Merciless One."

The party eventually discovered the automaton, and it agreed to serve as an efficient shopping assistant for the group. During the events of Day 14, the party secured a victory message from [[Galahad Guzzletome]] and looted his magical storeroom—retrieving a Bag of Holding—before officially unleashing BarterBot 5000.

Since its liberation, the bot has been instrumental in the party's acquisitions. During Session 12, the party utilized its services to successfully haggle for a Pearl of Power, a Bandle of Light Knicking, and Boots of Elvenkind.

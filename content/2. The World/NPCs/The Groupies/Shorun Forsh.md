Shorun Forsh is an ex-paladin who sought refuge in [[Pinetide]], where he lives in seclusion as the clandestine protector of a significant magic item known as the Aeris Indagator. His reputation for holding a "huge score" drew the attention of the Bear Bandits under the command of [[Falrus]], yet his true purpose remains tied to the safeguarding of the relic.

## 📜 Biography

Shorun Forsh spent a period of time hiding out in [[Pinetide]], a life of secrecy that led the Bear Bandits, led by [[Falrus]], to believe he held the key to a major score. In reality, his primary concern was the preservation of the Aeris Indagator.

### Day 20
The party’s involvement with Shorun began when he was interrogated by a rebel group led by [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and [[Petra]]. With the intervention of The Gang, the party was able to negotiate with Shorun, eventually gaining the information they needed regarding the location of the Aeris Indagator.

### Day 29
Following this cooperation, Shorun joined forces with [[Leif LúmëLóke]] to help dispel "ideologies of wrong doing" near [[Aethercrysts]].

### Day 38
Shorun's role in the group's efforts continued as he joined [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and The Gang to infiltrate [[Duke Vizier Wystan Everbell]] Everbell's secret laboratory located within [[Shardhold Citadel]].

While Shorun eventually came to view the party as allies rather than a threat, his attempts to act as a peacemaker were not always successful. He attempted to plead with May to prevent a confrontation that would have resulted in significant bloodshed, but he was unable to sway [[The Retributors]]. Currently, Shorun remains in hiding, successfully posing as a different type of paladin to maintain his anonymity.

## 🤝 Relationships & Loyalties

- **Enemies:** Bear Bandits (under [[Falrus]]), [[The Retributors]]

Turbo is a unique and grotesque undead familiar that serves as a loyal companion to Kaelen Stormrage. Manifesting as a flying, grotesque head, Turbo serves the party as a versatile scout, a tactical distraction, and a unique medium for spell delivery.

## 📜 Biography

Turbo is a flying grotesque head composed of a unique trifecta of undead components: half ghost, half skeleton, and half zombie. Despite its macabre appearance, Turbo possesses a distinct and gritty personality, speaking with the gravelly tone of an Australian biker and frequently indulging in smoking. 

Throughout their adventures, Turbo has proven to be an invaluable, if unconventional, teammate. It has served as a scout for tower interiors, a distraction to lure away charging Orcs—notably dodging every ranged attack in a skirmish—and a conduit for Kaelen to suck a soul through a bandit's skull. In the heat of combat, Turbo has acted as a torch-bearer to ignite explosions involving a mutated goblin and a freaktopus, and was even enlarged by Kaelen to serve as a "piledriver" to knock a goblin rider off a worg. Turbo has also displayed remarkable bravery, flying into a carriage containing an oil flask while surrounded by 21 [[Black Rain Mutation]], and dropping firebombs on bears and orcs to clear a path.

Beyond its combat utility, Turbo has acted as a spy and assistant, peeking into a clandestine meeting involving [[Gundrik Ashclaw]], an Orc, a Lizardfolk, a Tabaxi, a human with a bowl-cut, an earless elf, a Kobold, and five wizards. It has also assisted its companions by opening books for Cole and Mae and searching for Cryos in local taverns. 

During the events of Day 13, Turbo navigated a perilous cavern filled with deadly beams of light to help secure the *Book of the Skip Ahead* from an individual named [[Alistair Mccant]]. While some records suggest Turbo was "obliterated" during this encounter, the familiar continues to appear in subsequent records, suggesting a resilient—or perhaps supernatural—persistence.

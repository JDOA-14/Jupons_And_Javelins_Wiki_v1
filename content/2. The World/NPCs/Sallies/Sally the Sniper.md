[[Helga Brightrun|Sally the Sniper]] is a covert operative who initially infiltrated the party under the guise of a humble maid named [[Helga Brightrun]]. While traveling through [[Thundermarch]] and [[Highwatch]], she worked alongside the group before ultimately turning against her contract with [[Sally Syndicate|the Sally Syndicate]] to align herself with the party's interests.

## 📜 Biography

[[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] first joined the party in [[Thundermarch]], appearing alongside [[Captain Verrik]], [[King Selos SkySplitter|King Selos Skysplitter]], [[Prince Sier SkySplitter|Prince Sier Skysplitter]], [[Leif LúmëLóke|Leif]], and [[Stebonheath]]. During this time, she accepted a mission to clear the hamlet of [[Mistthrow]] of Orc presence and was associated with the group that went missing during the events of [[Black Rain|the Black Rain]].

On Day 11, she revealed her true identity as [[Helga Brightrun|Sally the Sniper]], shedding her persona as [[Helga Brightrun]]. She is a specialist who utilizes a custom long-barrelled crossbow and communicates via a unique method known as "[[Sally Speak]]." She demonstrated her skills by using "[[Sally Speak]]" to warn the party of an upcoming meeting on a train, though she did not attend the meeting herself.

While [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]]'s history involves working with [[Stebonheath]] to steal the [[Thundermarch]] crown and pearl, her decision to join the party's journey to [[Highwatch]] was driven by a need to clear "loose ends" regarding her betrayal of [[Sally Syndicate|the Sally Syndicate]]. Though she appears to trust [[The Gang|the gang]], she eventually departed the group due to escalating dangers, leaving behind a hidden clue for [[Sally the Smasher|Sally The Smasher]] regarding a meeting on a train.

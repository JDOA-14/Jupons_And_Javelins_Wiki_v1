[[Sally Syndicate]] the Still is an NPC associated with the [[Runehell Lumbermill]], where she serves as a replacement for May Mistbrooke's actual mother. She is currently being held in a state of Gentle Repose by May.

## 📜 Biography

[[Sally Syndicate]] the Still arrived at the [[Runehell Lumbermill]] many years ago to replace May Mistbrooke's real mother. This substitution is believed to have occurred as part of [[Project Keen-Eye]].

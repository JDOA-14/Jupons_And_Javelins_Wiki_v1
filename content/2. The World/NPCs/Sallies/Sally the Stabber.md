[[Sally Syndicate]] the Stabber is a syndicate trainee who serves as a bitter rival to [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher. She is most notable for her deep-seated jealousy regarding the selection of [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher for a prestigious "Great Mission," an assignment she believed was unfairly awarded.

## 📜 Biography

[[Sally Syndicate]] the Stabber rose through the syndicate ranks alongside [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher, but their professional relationship soured when [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher was selected for a "Great Mission." Driven by the belief that the selection was rigged, [[Sally Syndicate]] the Stabber frequently goaded her rival into a rage, a volatile behavior that nearly caused her to lose her undercover cover while embedded with the [[Bandits of Ashdawn]].

### Session 15
After spending several weeks undercover, [[Sally Syndicate]] the Stabber confronted [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher on a road a few miles from a town while the group was prepared for a strike at a tavern. During this confrontation, the Stabber accused [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher of being the target of a political setup, claiming the "Great Mission" was designed to ensure her failure and forced removal from the syndicate. (Note: Historical records vary regarding whether this interaction occurred on the road or while with the [[Bandits of Ashdawn]]).

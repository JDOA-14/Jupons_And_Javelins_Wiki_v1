Prince Kronar Stormrage is a prominent royal figure whose legacy is defined by his lineage and the knowledge he bequeathed to his heir. As the father of Duke [[Kaelen Stormrage]], he stands as a foundational pillar of the Stormrage name.

## 📜 Biography

Prince Kronar Stormrage is the father of Duke [[Kaelen Stormrage]]. His lineage was established through a union with a dragon, an act that resulted in the birth of his son. Beyond his role as a patriarch, Kronar served as a mentor to [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]], passing down a significant amount of knowledge to ensure his heir was prepared for his future responsibilities.

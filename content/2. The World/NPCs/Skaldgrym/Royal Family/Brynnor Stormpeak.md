King Brynnor Stormpeak is the current ruler of [[Skaldgrym]] and a prominent royal of the Goliath people. A Neutral Evil sovereign, he wields his authority with a cold, calculated grip as the son of [[2. The World/NPCs/Skaldgrym/Royal Family/Stormpeaks/King Donan Stormpeak|King Donan Stormpeak]] Stormpeak.

## 📜 Biography

Born as the son of [[2. The World/NPCs/Skaldgrym/Royal Family/Stormpeaks/King Donan Stormpeak|King Donan Stormpeak]] Stormpeak, Brynnor was destined for a life of significant authority and influence. His path to the throne was solidified during the events of Session 12, where he officially ascended to become [[The King of Skaldgrym]].

### Session 12
Brynnor is established as the new [[The King of Skaldgrym]].

Beyond his royal duties, Brynnor is defined by a deep-seated jealousy toward Kaelen/Kaelin. This rivalry serves as a primary driver of his motivations, establishing him as a persistent and resentful rival to their interests.

Emithirch Trask is a witch whose intervention in the life of Kaelen Stormrage served as a pivotal turning point in his history. Known for defying her own coven to rescue Kaelen from a wrongful imprisonment, she currently serves as a vital point of contact for the party. She holds Kaelen’s wounded father, [[Kronar Stormrage]], and provides crucial updates regarding his survival and the ongoing instability of [[Skaldgrym]].

## 📜 Biography

### Flashback
Emithirch acted against the authority of her coven to break Kaelen Stormrage out of an unjust prison, successfully saving him from execution. During this time, she acted as a mentor, providing Kaelen with his first necromancy book and teaching him that there are many different ways to be strong in this world.

### Later Campaign
Emithirch resurfaced to contact Kaelen and "The Gang" with an urgent status update. She revealed that she is currently holding Kaelen's father, [[Kronar Stormrage]], who remains wounded but alive. Furthermore, she served as a cautious messenger, informing the party that [[Skaldgrym]] is not yet safe for travel.

## 🤝 Relationships & Loyalties

- **Allies:** Kaelen Stormrage, The Gang
- **Enemies:** Her coven

Kyrra Stonefist is a Goliath child born to Grathor and [[Petra]]. Following a violent abduction by [[Sally Syndicate]], she was taken into their custody to undergo rigorous training. She is widely known by the formidable moniker "[[Sally Syndicate]] The Smasher."

## 📜 Biography

Kyrra Stonefist was born to Grathor and [[Petra]] in the vicinity of [[Skaldgrym]]. Her early life was marked by a sudden tragedy when agents of [[Sally Syndicate]] attacked her family, leaving both of her parents paralyzed. In the aftermath of this attack, the young Goliath was seized and taken to the Syndicate to be conditioned and trained.

During her time under the Syndicate’s tutelage, Kyrra has shown significant progress, particularly in her physical development. While she lacks the ability to shapeshift, she has displayed immense raw strength, earning her the reputation of "[[Sally Syndicate]] The Smasher."

[[Petra]], who often operates under the alias "[[Petra]]," is a formidable rebel leader and the mother of the warrior known as Kyrra ([[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher). A woman in her 50s with black hair shot through with two grey streaks, she serves as a pillar of strength for those resisting the [[Aethercrysts]].

## 📜 Biography

Thalara spent her early years in the outskirts of [[Skaldgrym]] before rising to a position of leadership within a rebel group composed of former sacrifices. Along with [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and [[Petra]], she oversees the movement; while the rebellion was originally established by [[Shorun Forsh]] and [[Petra]], records indicate the group is currently led by [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and [[Petra]].

### Day 20
Thalara participated in the interrogation of the [[Shorun Forsh]], during which her group discovered that [[Aethercrysts]] sacrifices had been faked for a decade. This revelation led the group to suspect that [[Aethercrysts]] are involved in even more clandestine and suspicious activities. Given her long history with [[Leif LúmëLóke]], she is inclined to hear his counsel, though she remains a hardened revolutionary who has stated that the party would need to be killed if they attempted to flee.

### Day 29
While traveling from Lake Traminer toward the [[Thundermarch]] Pearl, Thalara revealed her personal history as the mother of [[Sally Syndicate]].

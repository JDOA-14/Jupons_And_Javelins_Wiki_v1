Maeve Tilden is a farmer woman. She works at her farmhouse located near [[Dampiner Forest]] with her husband [[Barret Tilden_F1]]. Due to it's location off the beaten path, they often host weary travellers.

Maeve was their during the attack on her farmhouse - which was thwarted by [[The Gang]].

## 📜 Biography

### [[Session 02 Recap - FULL]]
[[The Gang]] meets Maeve when searching for their stolen items after they were taken by [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man_U1|The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]]. She explains that they are used to taking in strays and that they did have a stranger in last night. 

She claimed that the man never showed his face, but was very kind. 
However, she did hear him swearing and have angry outbursts during the night -- then when she went to check on him in the morning, he was gone.

Luckily she didn't go further into the room, as when [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] entered to check for clues, he triggered an explosive rune left by [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man_U1|The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]]. This notified a group of waiting Orc and Goblin bandits -- who raided the Tilden Farmhouse. 

[[The Gang]] defeated the bandits, and Maeve was instrumental in calming down her husband when he arrived to see the damage.

Edmund Steif is the leader of the [[Bandits of Ashdawn]]. He received information directly from [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man_U1|The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]] on when to do a heist on the [[Crooked Tusk Tavern]] to steal [[The Gang]]'s magical items.

He fled the fight on the road, to his hideout in [[Dampiner Forest]], and was killed by several sneaky sneaksters.

## 📜 Biography

### [[4. Session Notes/Sessions_only/Session 01 Recap - FULL|Session 01 Recap - FULL]]
Edmund is the leader of the Bandits who pretended to be bards during the heist of magical items in the [[Crooked Tusk Tavern]] in [[Ashdawn]].

After being followed by several un-teamed-up-yet members of [[The Gang]], he engaged in combat. When things look dicey, he stole one more for the road ([[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]]'s [[Amulet of the Ancestors]]) and fled to his hideout in [[Dampiner Forest]].

When [[Sally the Smasher]], disguised as a bandit, almost convinced the rest of the surviving bandits that she was to take the chest under the guise of new movement orders, Edmund was cautious (They both rolled very well).

He escorts Sally and the items out of the hideout, and is unceremoniously killed by sneak attacks from the rest of The Gang.

> *"[[Golden Guards of Eryndor]]? More like Golden Pigs."*

Rastrel Scurry is a rat-man ranger who excels in urban environments, specifically within the dense and treacherous cityscapes of the realm. Serving as a loyal ally to the gang, he provides essential expertise in navigating city life and assisting the group during high-stakes pursuits and clandestine activities in the slums.

## 📜 Biography

### Session 12
Rastrel first established his role as an ally when he assisted the gang in evading capture during a frantic chase.

### Day 38
The party reunited with Rastrel in [[1. Eryndor]] alongside [[Leif LúmëLóke]] and [[Shorun Forsh]] to coordinate a daring infiltration of [[Shardhold Citadel]]. Together, they breached [[Duke Vizier Wystan Everbell]] Everbell's secret laboratory, where they interrogated the Duke and seized vital research concerning [[Project Keen-Eye]] and [[Project Divinatus Revivica]] before burning the facility to the ground.

### The Slums
Rastrel’s familiarity with the city's underworld was further highlighted when Gronk identified him as a known face while the party narrowly escaped detection in the slums.

Tiffle Tattle is an eccentric halfling and former traveling salesman who experienced a life-altering stroke of luck when he won the [[1. Eryndor|Eryndor]] lottery. He is currently seeking to reinvent himself, attempting to shed his merchant persona to pursue a new life.

## 📜 Biography

After years spent as a traveling salesman, Tiffle Tattle’s life changed when he won the [[1. Eryndor|Eryndor]] lottery. This windfall gave him the opportunity to pursue a new life, though his initial attempts to find a romantic and sexual connection with a half-orc were met with rejection, as the individual wished to avoid any association with those in the sales trade.

Determined to shed his merchant persona, Tiffle sought out gurus to help him reinvent his identity. As part of this transformation, he has begun giving away his old wares.

### Session 
Tiffle first encountered the party during a camping trip, during which he provided the group with two healing potions.

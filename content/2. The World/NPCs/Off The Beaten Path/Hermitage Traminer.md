Hermitage Traminer is a powerful wizard of great evil who was banished to the isolated shores of [[Traminer Isle]]. Known as a "jesting mad wizard," he is a figure defined by both his formidable arcane power and his unpredictable, whimsical nature.

## 📜 Biography

Following his banishment to [[Traminer Isle]], Hermitage Traminer vanished from the historical record, and no further information regarding his activities was known for a long duration. Despite his long absence, his erratic influence remains evident within his fortress.

### Session 
The party explored a castle rigged with a variety of booby traps and hazards, showcasing the wizard's "jesting" brand of madness. Amidst these dangers, it was discovered that Traminer had "baby-proofed" certain storage rooms, deliberately disabling hazards to make those specific areas safe.

Myranda is a Half-Elf Cleric and mercenary who serves as a vital pillar of support for her party. Specializing in ranged magic and tactical assistance, she provides a versatile blend of arcane utility and consistent offensive pressure, ensuring her companions remain protected and empowered during high-stakes encounters.

Whether she is coordinating with her allies during complex scouting maneuvers or providing covering fire during intense skirmishes, Myranda has proven herself to be a reliable and capable presence in both clandestine operations and open warfare.

## 📜 Biography

Myranda’s history is defined by her consistent involvement in reconnaissance and her ability to maintain a watchful eye over her companions. She has frequently collaborated with Kaelen, Cole, and Mae to observe enemy camps and has demonstrated her reliability by holding a watchful position while her companions tracked local leads, ensuring the group's safety from the shadows.

### Day 10 | Camp Bearblood
A significant milestone in Myranda's recent history occurred at [[Camp Bearblood]]. Joining forces with [[Sir Fradd]] and [[Sally the Sellout]], she participated in a daring rescue operation to liberate Cryos, Gronk, and [[Sally Syndicate]] from an orc prison. During this event, Myranda was instrumental in securing vital intelligence, including maps detailing Orc and Bearfolk movements and the location of a [[Stebonheath]]-planned truce meeting.

### Combat and Support
In the heat of battle, Myranda has established herself as a reliable source of ranged support. She has worked in close coordination with [[Sally the Sellout]] and Cryos to repel attacking hordes, providing essential magical and ranged coverage while [[Sir Fradd]] occupied the front lines. Whether scouting a camp or holding off a wave of enemies, her role as a support specialist remains central to her party's success.

Fior is an elven druid who balances the roles of a mercenary and a spiritual healer. Known for working in close partnership with a creature named [[Silch]], Fior serves as a cornerstone of support for their companions, prioritizing the restoration of vitality and the preservation of the party's strength during perilous encounters.

## 📜 Biography

Fior’s journey began in [[Thundermarch]], where they were recruited to travel to the region of [[Mistthrow]]. During their time there, they participated in a dungeon clearing expedition that resulted in the discovery of an ancient map. This map provided critical intelligence regarding Orc activity moving toward [[Skaldgrym]].

However, the success of the expedition was met with a devastating homecoming. Upon returning from the dungeon, Fior found that [[Thundermarch]] had been ravaged by the arrival of "[[Black Rain]]." Since these events, Fior has remained a dedicated protector, focusing their efforts on healing and sustaining the party, ensuring they remain standing against the threats they face.

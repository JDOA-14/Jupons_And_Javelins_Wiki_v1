[[Sally Syndicate]] the Sellout is a versatile mercenary who operates as a Ranger and Rogue, combining wilderness survival skills with stealthy, tactical proficiency. She is a core member of a mercenary unit that works alongside her companions, [[Myranda]] and [[Sir Fradd]].

## 📜 Biography

### Day 10 | Camp Bearblood
[[Sally Syndicate]] participated in a daring rescue operation at [[Camp Bearblood]], where she assisted in liberating herself, Gronk, and Cryos from an orc prison. This mission proved highly productive, as she secured vital maps and information regarding the movements of Orcs and Bearfolk, as well as intelligence concerning a truce meeting planned by [[Stebonheath]].

### Session Recaps
In a subsequent operation, [[Sally Syndicate]] served as a mercenary alongside Kaelen, Cole, and Mae to conduct a surveillance watch on a camp. During this mission, she demonstrated sharp tactical judgment by convincing [[Sir Fradd]], Cryos, and [[Myranda]] to maintain their watch rather than following a villager's lead. When a suspicious situation was detected, [[Sally Syndicate]] acted quickly to reposition the group into a house or under a wagon for cover. When a horde eventually attacked, she worked in tandem with [[Myranda]] and Cryos to provide ranged support and spells, allowing [[Sir Fradd]] to hold the front as the primary tank.

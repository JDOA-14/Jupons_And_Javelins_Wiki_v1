Chains is a mercenary belonging to the "Chains" race and "Chains" class. Having joined the party as a free token, he serves as a source of comedic relief while actively participating in the group's dungeon exploration and combat encounters.

## 📜 Biography

Chains joined the party as a mercenary after being airdropped by a giant [[Owlin]]. His early presence was defined by a series of unfortunate mishaps during a confrontation outside a church; after rolling a natural 1 while attempting to launch a chain javelin from his arm, the limb exploded. He subsequently attempted to fight using two swords held in a single hand, though he failed to land any hits.

### Day 6
Following his recruitment in [[Thundermarch]], Chains traveled to [[Mistthrow]] alongside mercenaries [[Fior]] and [[Silch]]. The group cleared a dungeon and discovered an ancient map detailing Orc activity headed toward [[Skaldgrym]]. Upon returning to [[Thundermarch]], they found the area devastated by [[Black Rain]], leading them to seek out and obtain information from Prince Sier.

Silch is a small, notably hairy rat-like humanoid who serves as a mercenary and battlefield control specialist. A silent figure who does not speak, Silch is known for their ability to wildshape into a giant beetle to manipulate the flow of combat and provide tactical advantages to their allies.

## 📜 Biography

Silch was recruited as a mercenary alongside [[Fior]] and [[Chains]], beginning their journey with an excursion to [[Mistthrow]]. During this initial outing, the party traveled for three hours to clear a dungeon, where they discovered an ancient map detailing Orc activity headed toward [[Skaldgrym]]. Upon returning to [[Thundermarch]], Silch was present as the party received information from Prince Sier and accepted bounties to address the devastation caused by [[Black Rain|the Black Rain]].

A silent but highly effective combatant, Silch excels at battlefield control. They have been recognized as an MVP for their ability to wildshape into a giant beetle to pin down Orc leaders with their pincers and for tunneling through the ground to flank enemies during ambushes. Silch has also worked in close coordination with [[Detective Cole Le Fev]] to take out Orcs hidden within walls. While they are a formidable ally on the battlefield, they were once downed during a confrontation with Gundrik.

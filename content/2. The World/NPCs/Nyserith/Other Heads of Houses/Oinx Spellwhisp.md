Oinx Spellwhisp is a professor of necromancy who serves as a scholarly ally to the party. He notably assisted the group by casting *speak with dead* on the remains of [[Twiggy Marshdust]], providing vital information from beyond the grave. Beyond his academic contributions, Oinx has survived a series of harrowing events, most notably a violent confrontation between The Gang and [[The Cult Of The Longest Cloak]].

## 📜 Biography

### Session 09
Oinx was caught in the crossfire of a brutal conflict between The Gang and [[The Cult Of The Longest Cloak]], which resulted in him being swept into the [[Challenge Room]] along with several others. Against all odds, Oinx miraculously survived the ordeal and successfully evaded [[1. Nyserith]]'s security forces. In the aftermath of his escape, he pledged to act as an advocate for The Gang, attempting to clear their reputation by spreading the word that they are not the "faculty-murdering psychopaths" rumored to be their reputation.

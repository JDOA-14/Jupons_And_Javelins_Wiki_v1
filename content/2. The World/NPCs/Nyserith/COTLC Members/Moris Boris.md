Moris Boris is a Halfling spellcaster stationed within the Arcane Facility. He is a capable magic-user who utilizes a combination of offensive and utility spells, such as *Vicious Mockery* and *Command*, to manipulate and harass those who enter his territory.

## 📜 Biography

### Day 13
The party encountered Moris Boris during their exploration of the Arcane Facility. Records indicate he presided over a room shortly after the party performed a quick loot, though some sources place him specifically within the Flooding Room. In both instances, he stood as a significant threat alongside [[Darkle Charkle]]. The party eventually defeated him, allowing them to escape the flooded chamber through a portal.

## 📜 Biography

Alistair Mccant first came into prominence during the events of Day 13, where they were encountered as the Head of Illusions cult member within a cavern filled with deadly beams of light. During this encounter, the *Book of the Skip Ahead* was secured. Following these events, Alistair was observed appearing in the role of the Head Professor of Illusory.

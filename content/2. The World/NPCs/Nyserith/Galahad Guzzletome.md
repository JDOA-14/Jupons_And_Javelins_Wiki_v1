Galahad Guzzletome serves as the Headmaster of [[1. Nyserith]]. While he typically oversees the academic and magical pursuits of the institution, he is currently away from the school on a research mission described as both "crazy and big."

## 📜 Biography

### Day 14
Following a victory message sent to the party, Galahad granted them free rein to loot his magical storeroom, providing them with a Bag of Holding.

### Session Recap & Day 17
Galahad later communicated with the party via a celebration hologram and a scry vision. During these interactions, he revealed that he had reached his peak as a wizard and sought to unlock a secret hidden within his own mind. To achieve this, he requested to borrow the innate magic of the sorcerer Cryos. Galahad cautioned that while he had performed such a procedure on students before, the results on a sorcerer might be unknown or even permanent.

On Day 17, Galahad was located in [[1. Eryndor]]. Utilizing a power boost from Cryos's magic, he attempted to access the information locked in his brain. While Galahad claimed to have successfully unlocked the knowledge he sought, his ultimate fate remains a mystery as he was last seen flying off towards the sun.

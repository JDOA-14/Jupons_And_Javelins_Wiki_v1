Orvus Fillorvus was a prominent Headmaster of [[1. Nyserith]] whose legacy is defined by a tragic and erratic descent into madness. While he once held a position of great authority within the institution, his name is now primarily associated with a haunting tale of instability and a final, inexplicable act of self-destruction.

It is widely believed that Fillorvus lost his grip on reality, culminating in his supposed flight into the sun. He serves as a cautionary figure within the annals of [[1. Nyserith]], representing the dangers of the arcane and the fragility of the scholarly mind.

## 📜 Biography

During the Age of Peace, Orvus Fillorvus served as a Headmaster of [[1. Nyserith]], where he occupied a role of significant academic influence. However, his tenure was marked by a harrowing psychological breakdown involving the interpretation of the *Memoria Receptio*.

Fillorvus claimed to have discovered a terrifying pattern within the records: a massive spike in magic throughout [[Valhyria]], followed by a sharp, sudden decline. As his behavior became increasingly erratic and his theories more detached from reality, the community reacted with alarm. In response to his erratic behavior, a mural was commissioned to serve as a permanent warning to other mages, urging them to trust the collective wisdom of their peers over their own private, escalating delusions.

Following these events, Fillorvus’s mental state deteriorated completely. He ultimately disappeared, with many believing he flew into the sun as a final, tragic manifestation of his madness.

Darvok Duskrunner was a detective who served as a dedicated partner to Cole during a period of significant criminal investigation. He was a key figure in the inquiry into the *Necrolexicon Aeternum*, a case that ultimately led to his untimely death at the hands of [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]].

## 📜 Biography

Darvok Duskrunner served as a partner to Cole during a grim investigation conducted on the outskirts of [[Aeryndor]]. The pair were tasked with investigating a double murder, where they discovered two bodies with their throats slit. It was quickly determined that the crime was tied to a ritual involving the *Necrolexicon Aeternum*.

During their investigation, the duo was confronted by [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]]. The entity seized control of the environment, freezing time and rendering everyone else immobile. While the world stood still, [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]] acted alone, murdering Darvok and two senior monks before the temporal freeze was broken.

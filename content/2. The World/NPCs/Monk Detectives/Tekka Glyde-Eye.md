Tekka Glyde-Eye is an [[Aarakocra]] Monk Detective of the Hollow-Eye rank who serves as a dedicated cult investigator. First discovered by the party in a state of mortal peril, Tekka was saved and healed through the party's intervention, eventually becoming a vital source of information regarding the clandestine activities of the cults they hunt.

## 📜 Biography

The party first encountered Tekka Glyde-Eye while investigating local threats, finding the Hollow-Eye monk mortally wounded and in desperate need of aid. Identifying Tekka as a detective holding a rank between Draftling and Nestward, the party intervened to provide the healing necessary to save their life.

Upon recovering, Tekka revealed that they had been actively pursuing Anti-[[Aarakocra]] cultists. The party spent a night in a cave alongside Tekka and a contingent of [[Kenku]] guardsmen, during which they collaborated on preparing traps and fortifications. During this time, Tekka provided the party with critical insight into the ongoing conflict and the complex tensions between the [[Kenku]], [[Mallardfolk]], and [[Aarakocra]].

Captain Verrik is a high-ranking monk-detective who serves as the Skywarden of the South-West. He is the primary authority responsible for overseeing investigations into magical crimes, dangerous anomalies, and the mysterious phenomenon known as [[Black Rain]] within the [[Thundermarch]] region.

## 📜 Biography

As a senior member of the [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]] of [[Aeryndor]] and the police commissioner for the southwestern regions, Captain Verrik is a seasoned veteran of the law. He is an elderly [[Aarakocra]] with white feathers and a broad, square head; standing taller than Detective Cole Le Fev, his wings bear numerous scars and arrow holes earned from decades of service.

> *"Almost." - The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man*

The Dragon-Aura Faced Man is an entity or individual associated with magical rituals and mass mutations. He is known for stealing powerful items and triggering [[Black Rain Mutation]] events. He's the BBEG babyyyyy.

## 📜 Biography

#### Flashback
During a crime-scene investigation, The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man appears. He is able to "freeze" all the [[Aarakocra]] [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor|Monk-Detectives]], kill several, and steal the [[Necrolexicon Aeternum]]. He is briefly challenged by [[Detective Cole Le Fev]] -- who somehow miraculously controls his aura whilst his body is frozen. The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man strikes Cole, sending his aura back into his body, then leaves with the tome.

### [[Session 01 Recap - FULL|Session 01]]
The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man "froze" [[The Gang]] after they successfully retrieved their stolen magic items back from the [[Bandits of Ashdawn]]. He stole the items again. He stole only the magic items, and didn't kill any of The Gang.

### [[Session 08 Recap - FULL|Session 08]]
The Dragon-Aura Faced Man appeared during the VIP meeting on the train from [[Highwatch]] to [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]]. While it's not heard what he said -- [[Turbo_F1]] was able to pass on what he saw. The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man was holding court -- clearly the leader of the meeting. The VIPs took part in an old Valhyrian voting tradition where they must choose a beverage (Wine = For, Ale = against). Upon finishing the voting, The Dragon-Aura-Faced-Man and several wizards teleport the "For" voters away. 
[[Sally the Smasher]] is able to divert the guards blocking the VIP carriage enough for [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] to enter it, but all he finds are the "against" voters. The Dragon-Aura-Faced Man chuckles, says "almost", and uses an unknown spell -- clicking his fingers and triggering [[Black Rain]] that was waiting dormant in the stomachs of anyone who had drunk a [[Quince’s Quencher Ale (Corrupted)|Quince's Quencer Ale]] on the train that afternoon.

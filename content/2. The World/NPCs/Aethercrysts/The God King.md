> *"My ancestors passed down stories about how nice he was, and also how jacked he was." - Elder Villager in [[1. Eryndor]]*

The God King was a human leader and fighter who established a connection with The Shard. He rallied the races of the realm to fight chaotic dragons bringing plague to the land. After defeating them, and ending The Age of Fire and Death, he established a great accord that gave the races their own dominion while maintaining access to the kingdom of [[1. Eryndor]]. 

After hundreds of years of peace, The God King fused himself with The Shard to prevent it from losing its magic. He remains alive, but his life force is used to keep The Shard alive, requiring a sacrifice from another being twice a year.

## 📜 Biography

### Birth of the God King
**(0 AGK)**
The human who will become the **God King** is born.

### Early Life of the God King
**(0 AGK – 64 AGK)**
The God King is raised by a farming family and eventually is recruited in a war, then becomes a great leader and is found to have powerful abilities. He is then found to make a great connection with The Shard. He makes several great choices (training with monks and several other leaders of civilian communities he respects) and never falls to temptation of his abilities. Eventually he is crowned king of the humans.

### The Rally
**(64 AGK – 74 AGK)**
The great human fighter, and leader of the humans, eventually known as the God King begins working on his connection with The Shard. He claims he can commune with The Shard and utilise its magic more powerfully than others.

All his claims are proven to be true.<br>
The king begins **rallying races together to fight back the dragons** and make peace in the realm.
He rallies either through great peace offerings or through them being scared by his reputation.<br>There is a great story of the God King killing **two dragons in a single battle by himself**.
<br>He begins being referred to as **The God King**, not just the king of the humans.<br>He is eventually able to rally all the races in the realm, including several wiser dragons, and defeats all the chaotic dragons bringing plague to the realm.<br>
He then spends years forming a great accord that gives all the races their own dominion whilst maintaining access and help from the great kingdom in the middle of the continent, Eryndor.

### The Age of Peace
**(74 AGK – 374 AGK)**
After the God King’s efforts, there is **peace for hundreds of years**.<br>
The God King remains alive for all these years, maintaining peace.

### The Greatest Sacrifice
**(374 AGK)**
Eventually, The Shard begins to falter — seemingly losing its magic. Chaos reigns and cracks form in the earth -- the continent threatening to become a barren land like its surrounding areas.<br>
**The God King sacrifices himself — fusing himself with The Shard to keep it alive.**<br>The God King is still alive, but all his life force is used to keep The Shard alive.

Twice a year, someone from around the continent is chosen to sacrifice themselves to give their life energy to the God King, so he can continue to keep The Shard alive.

### [[4. Session Notes/Sessions_only/Session 15 Recap|Session 15]]
Upon viewing [[The Accords]], [[May Mistbrooke]] pulls [[The Gang]] into one of her magical visions.

There, May envisions herself as The God King, and The Gang see themselves as high ranking [[Aethercrysts]] (the current [[The High Council of Eryndor|High-Council of Eryndor]] at the time of the vision).

They view the council arguing things like: 
"How do we explain the dragons?"
		- "They're just beasts."
"What about the monks?"
		- "It's just shard magic."
"And the witches then?"
		- "Desecrated shard magic."

After seemingly fed up with the conversation, The God King waved his hand and completely filled the room with white light.

Prince Sier SkySplitter is a prominent figure in [[Thundermarch]], serving as the ruler of the territory in his father's stead. A half-human lightning genasi, Sier possesses a unique physical appearance characterized by a low-opacity blend of skin that allows him to maintain a more human-like appearance despite his elemental heritage.

## 📜 Biography

Prince Sier SkySplitter is the son of [[King Selos SkySplitter]] and a human mother. Because his father is too old to move, Sier has taken on the responsibility of ruling [[Thundermarch]] on his behalf.

### Day 5
The party first met with Prince Sier in [[Thundermarch]], where he was accompanied by King Selos, [[Captain Verrik]], [[Leif LúmëLóke]], [[Stebonheath]], and [[Helga Brightrun]]. During this meeting, the Prince commissioned the party to clear a threat of Orcs from the hamlet of [[Mistthrow]].

### Day 38
The party spoke with Prince Skysplitter in [[Thundermarch]] to continue their dealings.

### Session Recaps
Following the party's success in defeating the bandit orcs who were headed toward [[Paladin Jayce]] in [[Mistthrow]], Prince Skysplitter began cooperating with "The Gang." He shared vital knowledge regarding [[Black Rain]] Mutations and, as a gesture of gratitude for their service to [[Thundermarch]], provided the party with additional bounties and tickets for their first train journey. Additionally, the Prince provided a free token to the mercenary guild, which allowed the party to hire a mercenary at a discounted rate.

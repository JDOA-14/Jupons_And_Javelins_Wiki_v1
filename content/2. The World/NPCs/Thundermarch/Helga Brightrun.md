[[Sally the Sniper]] presents herself as a humble maid in [[Thundermarch]], though she is actually the covert operative known as [[Sally the Sniper]]. Serving as a temporary ally to the party, she provides expert assistance in "cleaning up" loose ends and ensuring their objectives are handled with discretion.

## 📜 Biography

### Day 5
While in [[Thundermarch]], [[Sally the Sniper]] met with [[Captain Verrik]], [[King Selos SkySplitter]], [[Prince Sier SkySplitter]], [[Leif LúmëLóke]], and Stebonesth. During this meeting, she accepted a mission to clear the Orcs from the nearby hamlet of [[Mistthrow]].

### Day 6
Following the devastation of [[Thundermarch]] by [[Black Rain]], [[Sally the Sniper]] went missing alongside Stebonesth.

### Day 11
The party reunited with her and discovered that her identity as a maid was a ruse; she is, in fact, [[Sally the Sniper]]. She revealed that she possesses a custom long-barrelled crossbow and joined the group to travel toward [[Highwatch]]. Her primary motivation for this alliance is to ensure that [[Sally Syndicate]] does not discover she turned on the individual she was originally contracted to serve.

### Session Recaps
During their travels, she shared a knowing look with [[Sally Syndicate]] the Smasher and communicated via "[[Sally Speak]]" to warn of a meeting on a train, though she was not seen at the meeting point itself.

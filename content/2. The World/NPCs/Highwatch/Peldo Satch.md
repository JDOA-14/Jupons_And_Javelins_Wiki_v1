Peldo Satch is a water genasi alchemist known for his specialized research into the properties and applications of "[[Black Rain|black rain]]." Beyond his chemical expertise, Peldo is a skilled artisan who contributed significantly to the construction of a magical steam-powered train.

## 📜 Biography

Peldo Satch is a local shop owner and alchemist who has developed a reputation for his work with "[[Black Rain|black rain]]." He successfully mastered methods to keep the substance cool, allowing it to be utilized as an ingredient in Quince's Quencher Ale (Corrupted). His expertise in alchemy and engineering also made him a key figure in the construction of a magical steam-powered train.

### The Hidden Laboratory
The party eventually discovered that Peldo operated a clandestine [[Black Rain|black rain]] laboratory hidden aboard a magical steam-powered train. During their investigation of this laboratory, they witnessed a harrowing mass [[Black Rain Mutation|mutation]] event triggered by a man possessing a Dragon-Aura face.

### Recent Activity
More recently, Peldo has been observed acting with visible agitation while hurrying toward the rear of the train. Upon searching the rear car, the party discovered that Peldo had been hiding a cache of frozen [[Black Rain Vials|black rain vials]]; however, one of these vials is currently missing.

The [[Highwatch]] train driver is a mysterious figure who served as the primary transport for the group's journey to [[1. Nyserith]]. Though his personal history remains unknown, he is defined by his role in facilitating a pivotal voyage that concluded with his sudden and unexplained disappearance.

## 📜 Biography

The driver was persuaded by the group to transport them to [[1. Nyserith]], providing the means for their travel. However, his involvement with the party was brief and ended abruptly upon their arrival at their destination. Once the group was greeted by the wizard faculty, the driver fled the scene by leaping from a train window, vanishing from the world and never being seen again.

Grivella Shadowfang is a resident of the [[Rylvarn Forest]] region, where she lives as the mother of [[Gronk Shadowfang]]. She is a woman deeply tied to her family and the surrounding woodland environment.

## 📜 Biography

Grivella grew up near the [[Rylvarn Forest]] alongside [[Grotch Shadowfang]]. Her life and history are defined by her deep familial ties and the emotional toll they have taken. She maintains that her son, [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]], has failed the family, a realization that has caused significant pain to both her and Grotch. Despite these heavy familial burdens, Grivella often displays a surprising tranquility; while others might expect her to be embroiled in the family's troubles, she is frequently found peacefully tending to her garden.

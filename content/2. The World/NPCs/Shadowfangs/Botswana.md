Botswana is a skeletal llama who serves as a spiritual guide, dispatched by a distant ancestor to retrieve a hero named Gronk and his companions. Though he initially struggled to correctly identify the party members he was meant to find, he proved to be a resilient ally who assisted the group in navigating the material plane and defending themselves against a hostile cult.

## 📜 Biography

Botswana first appeared to Cole, declaring that an ancestor had sent him to retrieve "Gronk" and his companions—a Dragonborn, a "[[Sally Syndicate]]," and an Arakokra. Acting as a guide, Botswana successfully transported the group to a cave on the material plane before collapsing from the sheer exhaustion of the journey.

While incapacitated, Botswana was snatched by an Owlbear and taken into captivity. The creature was soon discovered by cultists who intended to return with reinforcements to seize him. Demonstrating great fortitude, Botswana managed to break free from his bonds and joined the party in a fierce battle against the cultists, sustaining significant damage during the melee. The group ultimately emerged victorious, defeating the cult leader and slaying the Owlbear.

In the aftermath of the battle, Botswana apologized for his initial confusion regarding the party's identities. He revealed that he had mistakenly identified Cole as Gronk, Cryos as Cole, and Mae as [[Sally Syndicate]].

## 🤝 Relationships & Loyalties

- **Allies:** Cole, Cryos Frostjaw, May Mistbrooke (Mae), Dragonborn, [[Sally Syndicate]], Arakokra
- **Enemies:** Cult members, Cult leader

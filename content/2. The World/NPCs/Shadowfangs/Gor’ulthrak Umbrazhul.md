Gor’ulthrak Umbrazhul is a formidable spectral entity appearing as a ghostly goblin knight enveloped in haunting, greenish flames. As an ancestor of Gronk Shadowfang, he is a figure of dread known primarily for his terrifying role in forcibly extracting both Gronk and Cuddleclaw from the material plane.

## 📜 Biography

Gor’ulthrak Umbrazhul is a figure of immense ancestral weight, holding the prestigious and fearsome titles of First of the Umbrazhul Line, Dreadhelm of the Thousand Screams, Tamer of The Wolrikkae, Erupter of the Ancestral Pyre, and The Maw That Hungers Beyond Death. He is a direct ancestor of Gronk Shadowfang, though his specific lineage remains a point of contention, cited in some records as his great-grandfather and in others as his great-great-great-grandfather.

### Session Recap
The entity manifested as a ghostly knight clad in greenish fire, mounted upon a skeletal warhorse. In a display of overwhelming power, Gor’ulthrak was responsible for ripping both Gronk and Cuddleclaw from the material plane.

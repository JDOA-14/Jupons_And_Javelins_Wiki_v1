Shisha is a [[Kua-Toa]] known for their involvement in the theft of the magical Everfrost Pearl from Cryos Frostjaw. A cunning participant in underground dealings, Shisha acted as a business associate of Kaelen, participating in the merging of several businesses in exchange for a share of the profits.

## 📜 Biography

Shisha is a survivor of [[Black Rain]] attack at [[1. Drippledown]]. Before the primary events of the campaign, Shisha and an associate—identified in various records as either [[Shorsh]] or [[Shola]]—discovered the unconscious body of Cryos Frostjaw washed up on the shore. They took advantage of the opportunity to loot the body, successfully stealing the blue Everfrost Pearl.

Following the theft, Shisha established a business relationship with Kaelen. For a payment of 20 gold, Shisha agreed to join three merging businesses, securing a 10% stake in each enterprise. 

However, Shisha’s possession of the magical artifact was short-lived. Shisha and their companion later reported that a hooded stranger approached them and stole the pearl by force, wounding everyone involved but sparing their lives. Following this encounter, Shisha and their associate disappeared, never returning to their place of work and leaving their belongings behind.

## 🤝 Relationships & Loyalties

- **Allies:** [[Shorsh]], [[Shola]], Kaelen (Business partner).
- **Enemies:** Cryos Frostjaw (Theft victim), "a hooded man" (who stole the pearl).

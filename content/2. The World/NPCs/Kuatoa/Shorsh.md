Shorsh is a [[Kua-Toa]] known for their role in a sequence of thefts involving the unconscious body of Cryos Frostjaw. Working in collaboration with an associate named [[Shisha]], Shorsh was instrumental in the acquisition of the Everfrost Pearl, a magical blue pearl of significant value. Shorsh's involvement in these clandestine dealings ultimately ended when they were killed during [[Black Rain]] battle at The [[Dried Fish]].

## 📜 Biography

A resident of the town's housing district, Shorsh was a member of the local [[Kua-Toa]] community who was known to conduct business deals with Kaelen, [[Shisha]], and other members of their kind. They were a regular presence at the local tavern, but their sudden disappearance—leaving behind their work belongings—marked a turning point in their documented history.

Shorsh’s most notable actions involved a sequence of thefts targeting the unconscious body of Cryos Frostjaw. Just one day before the primary events, Shorsh and [[Shisha]] discovered Cryos washed up on the shore and proceeded to loot his body for the magic pearl. During these activities, Shorsh displayed significant power by smiting a wall until it crumbled. While Shorsh was instrumental in the sequence to steal the Everfrost Pearl, the magic item was ultimately taken by a flaming-dragon-faced man. Shorsh's involvement in these events concluded when they were killed during [[Black Rain]] battle at The [[Dried Fish]].

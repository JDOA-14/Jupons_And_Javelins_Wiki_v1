Shoawan is a dock worker who serves as a dedicated supporter of [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]. Following a convincing display of divine authority, he became associated with the Church of Stormrage and plays a pivotal role in the merger of three local fishing docks.

## 📜 Biography

The party first encountered Shoawan, a worker from a neighboring dock, outside a cluster of three fishing businesses. During their interaction, [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] provided information and utilized a "divine bluff" to convince Shoawan of his own divinity. Persuaded that [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] was a god entitled to receive funds from the docks, Shoawan became a staunch supporter of [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]'s interests.

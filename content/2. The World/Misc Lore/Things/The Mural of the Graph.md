- A huge wall painting mural - of a graph that shows a slow rise, into a sharp rise, into a sharp sharp drop, into a slow rise
- The story behind it:
	- A headmaster of the city was found in his room gone mad with this graph everywhere - on notes, written on walls, etc
	- This headmaster, [[Orvus Fillorvus]] claims to have used a device ([[Memoria Receptio]]) that can read magic levels over time periods - claims this is the magic levels of our history.
	- All other tests of the equipment showed otherwise and he was expelled/fired/banished
	- Last spotted flying into the sun.
	- It is muralised as a showing that the wizard brain is the most powerful thing, and if you don't trust the brains of your fellow wizards, you'll go mad.

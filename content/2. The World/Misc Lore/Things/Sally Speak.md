# Sally Speak

## Description
**[[Sally Syndicate]] Speak** is a system of communication used by members of the **[[Sally Syndicate]] Syndicate** (referred to as "[[Sally Syndicate]]"). It consists of a series of graffiti-like symbols that only those within the group can understand.

## Purpose and Usage
* **Infiltration:** The language is used for coordinating covert missions. [[Sally Syndicate]] The Smasher was tasked with using [[Sally Syndicate]] Speak while infiltrating a band of Orcs to determine why they were banding together and traveling north.
* **Warnings and Instructions:** It is used to convey specific warnings or information, such as a recorded message stating: *"Don't trust the crab."*

# [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] Speak

## Description
**[[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] Speak** is a system of communication used by members of the **[[Sally Syndicate]]** (referred to as "[[Sally Syndicate|Sallies]]"). It consists of a series of graffiti-like symbols that only those within the group can understand.

## Purpose and Usage
* **Infiltration:** The language is used for coordinating covert missions. [[Sally the Smasher|Sally The Smasher]] was tasked with using [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] Speak while infiltrating a band of Orcs to determine why they were banding together and traveling north.
* **Warnings and Instructions:** It is used to convey specific warnings or information, such as a recorded message stating: *"Don't trust the crab."*

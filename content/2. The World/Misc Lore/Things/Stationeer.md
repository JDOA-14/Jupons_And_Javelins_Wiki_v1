Members of great clans who are working in a location away from their clan.

Usually as a way to share information or to gain strengths in areas that your kingdom otherwise does not have. 

There are several Stationeers in [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]] -- these are mostly to keep an eye on the hard-to-govern wizards. 
The current Stationeers in Nyseryth are:
- [[Aaron Balfurs]] for the [[Golden Guards of Eryndor]]
- [[Lerance Sootfeather]] for the [[2. The World/CLANS/Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor|Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]]
- [[Michonne Flessle]] for the [[2. The World/CLANS/Aethercrysts|Aethercrysts]]

[[Stebonheath]] was a Stationeer in [[Thundermarch]] before he [[Black Rain]]'d it and stole the [[Crown of Thundermarch]].

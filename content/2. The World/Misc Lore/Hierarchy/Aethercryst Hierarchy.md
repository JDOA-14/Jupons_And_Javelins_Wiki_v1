### Hierarchy

- Deacon - assistant to Priest / low level cleric
- Priest - general clerics
- Bishop - overseas a large region
- Archbishop - leads a major region 
- Duke/Duchess Vizier - Very high-ranking and the lowest ranking in [[The High Council of Eryndor]]. Public facing and controlling of certain sections required.
- High Hierophant - Well-respected veterans and leaders. 2 are on [[The High Council of Eryndor]]. 
- Grand Duke - second in command 
- Archduke - Leader of [[Aethercrysts]], basically ruler of the continent

- "Emeritus" - someone who is retired. Often referred to as "Maesters"
- "Cathedraless" - someone who is not recognised as part of the faith by other [[Aethercrysts]], but who is still faithful to the Shard, and are able to harness it's radiant powers. (eg - [[Johnny Smallerm]] and [[Ol Derek Yeller]])

- [[2. The World/CLANS/The Consecrators|The Consecrators]] - Paladins, may have ranks in [[2. The World/CLANS/Aethercrysts|Aethercrysm]] and [[Golden Guards of Eryndor|The Golden Guards]].
- [[The Retributors]] - Clerics/Paladins who have committed crimes against The Shard, and are repaying their debt.

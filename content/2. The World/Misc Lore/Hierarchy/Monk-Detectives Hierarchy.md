#### Hierarchy
- **Draftling** 
	- The beginning rank for those learning the foundations of the order.
- **Hollow-Eye**
	- Experienced investigators trusted with fieldwork and independent cases.
	- recognised as "Detectives"
- **Nestward**
	- Senior members responsible for leading others and overseeing investigations.
	- recognised as "Detectives"

From here the rankings split into 3 paths -- like the talons on an [[Aarakocra]]'s foot. "The Inner Talon": focussing on the monastic disciplines, "The Outer Talon": focussing on the detective work, and "The Centre Talon": focussing on both.
Members of the order can "split off" earlier, if they show signs of great prowess in certain aspects of detective work or monastic disciplines. 

## The Inner Talon
Focussed mostly on Monk stuff. Some are more focussed on mystical knowledge and study, while others focus more on martial arts -- however it leans slightly more to towards the former. 
The majority of Inner Talon monks will spend most of their time in [[Aeryndor]].

- **Glint Disciple**
	- Usually assigned to assist a Cloud Brother/Sister
- **Cloud Brother/Sister**
	- Recognised as Cloud Monks. Valuable members of the monastery.
- **Sky Abbot**
	- Leader of sections of study and religious outreach.
- **Zenith Brother/Sister**
	- Experienced teacher who has profound mastery of their chosen aspect of monk work.
- **Cos-Monk**
	- Responsible for preserving the complete historical, spiritual, and martial heritage of the temple. Those known and un-known to lower ranks.
- **Grandmaster**
	- The highest ranking on the monk side. A Master of their chosen aspect in the monastery, whose dedicated their life to the way. None ever really "retire". But many lead quiet lives as leaders of monasteries and temples -- and provide mystical or religious insight for lower ranked monks as well as the other Talons of the order.

## The Centre Talon
Focussed on both monk and detective work evenly. Likewise, they generally study monastic disciples evenly, but they generally skew more towards the martial arts and mystical combat. Most will generally spend the majority of their time in [[Aeryndor]], however many will work cases across [[Valhyria]], and others will also be deemed so powerful that they can generally go wherever they please without oversight.

- **Roostenant**
	- A well trained and highly sought after Monk-Detective, able to lead sections of both Detective and Monastic teams.
- **Soarrior Monk**
	- Expert [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]] who show prowess in both detective work, and one or more forms of monk practices (fighting styles, mystical arts, mystical knowledge, etc.)
	- Generally used for incredibly dangerous detective work, or for protecting areas of interest for the order.
- **Flew-Seng**
	- [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]] recognised for teaching ability, physical stamina, criminal insight, and mastery of multiple traditional forms and weapons.
- **Sky Marshal**
	- High-ranking disciple of the order with incredible amounts of knowledge in both detective work and monastic disciplines.
- **Auraguard**
	- Seen as the highest honour a Monk-Detective can reach - showing mastery of Investigation, Martial Arts, and Mystical Knowledge.
	- They act as a second in command to the Grand Flew-Seng Cos-monk
	- Like the Cos-Monks, they are responsible for preserving the complete historical, spiritual, and martial heritage of the temple. Those known and un-known to lower ranks. However, do so with much more force.
- **Grand Flew-Seng Cos-monk**
	- Leader of [[Aeryndor]], and of all [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]] -- only possible through working both paths.

## The Outer Talon
Focussed mostly on Detective stuff. Unlike the Inner Talon, these are the majority of [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]] that are spread across [[Valhyria]] working cases.

- **Detective Nest Sergeant**
	- Supervisor who manages a team of detectives on a shift or within a local branch.
- **Detective Inspect-raptor**
	- Senior leader who commands larger criminal investigation branches (CIB) or major cases.
- **Detective Chief Inspect-raptor**
	- Higher administrative and operational commander overseeing multiple detective teams.
- **Sky Warden**
	- Highest rank -- overseeing entire regions of detectives.
- **Flock Marshal**
	- For retired / decorated detectives.

## The Back Talon
If you're wondering, this is held for the apotheosis. And is considered a grounding piece of knowledge -- that anyone could become the Apotheosis, and that the Apotheosis will be master of both monk stuff, detective stuff, and the combination of the two.

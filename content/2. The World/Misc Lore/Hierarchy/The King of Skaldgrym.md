### Sessions 1-12
- During most of the campaign, [[King Donan Stormpeak]] has been [[The King of Skaldgrym]]. 
- However, in Session 12, [[The Gang]] discovers through a news report that [[2. The World/NPCs/Skaldgrym/Royal Family/Stormpeaks/King Donan Stormpeak|King Donan Stormpeak]] has been killed in a freak ship wreck.

### Session 12-present
- It is said that Donan's heir, [[Brynnor Stormpeak]], is now the King or [[Skaldgrym]].

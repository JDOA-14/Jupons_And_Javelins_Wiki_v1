**Time Period:** 374 AGK – Present (614 AGK)

## Overview
The Age of Thankfulness is a period characterized by relative peace within the realm of [[Valhyria]]. Following the events of "[[The Greatest Sacrifice]]," no large wars occurred during this era due to the strength and fairness of the established accords.

## Governance and Order
Peace is maintained through the cooperation of three primary groups:

* **[[Aethercrysts]]:** The dominant religion of [[Valhyria]], which venerates the Shard and [[The God King]]. They serve as guardians of peace and have influence spanning most of [[Valhyria]]’s cities.
* **[[Golden Guards of Eryndor]] of [[1. Eryndor]]:** The elite military order and official guard force of the capital city, [[1. Eryndor]]. They work alongside [[Aethercrysts]] to uphold order.
* **[[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]] of [[Aeryndor]]:** Serve as the realm’s detectives during this age.

## Historical Context
This era began immediately following **[[The Greatest Sacrifice]] (374 AGK)**:
* The Shard began to falter and lose its magic, threatening to turn the continent into a barren wasteland.
* To prevent this, [[The God King]] sacrificed himself by fusing with The Shard.
* While [[The God King]] remains alive, his life force is entirely consumed to keep the Shard active.
* To sustain this preservation, individuals from across the continent are chosen twice a year to sacrifice their life energy to [[The God King]].

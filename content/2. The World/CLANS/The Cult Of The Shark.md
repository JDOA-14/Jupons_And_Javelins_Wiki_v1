## Overview
The Cult of the Shark appeared during a confrontation at a sacrifice while the party was investigating and protecting civilians from potential cultist interference.

## Physical Description and Attributes
- **Appearance:** Members are described as 8-foot-tall hulks.
- **Equipment:** They are equipped with weapons.
- **Combat Style:** They are described as being "BRUTAL" in combat.

## Notable Incidents
- During a fight at the sacrifice, one member of the cult was capable of eating a spiritual weapon (which destroyed its vital organs).

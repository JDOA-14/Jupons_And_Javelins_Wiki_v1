The Cult Of The Longest Cloak. Where true believers know the truth about The Shard; that the world is a giant coat rack, and The Shard is the sticking out point. They believe that one day, The Longest Cloak will arrive. A super massive cloak that is big enough to cover the entire continent and more. They believe that once it arrives, it will hang on the rack, endowing it's true believers underneath it with other-worldly powers.

Generally popular in [[1. Nyserith]] and nowhere else much.

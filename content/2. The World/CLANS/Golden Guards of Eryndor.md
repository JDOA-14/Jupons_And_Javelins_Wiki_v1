# Golden Guards of Eryndor  

**Type:** Military Order / Elite Guard  
**Region:** [[Eryndor]]  

---

## Overview  
The Golden Guards are the elite protectors of [[Eryndor]], the capital city of Valhyria. They safeguard the [[The Shard|shard]] at the heart of the city and serve as the military arm of the kingdom. Their golden armor is both a symbol of honor and a warning to potential enemies.  

---

## Origin & History  
- Established during the [[Age of the First Greed]] when wars over the [[The Shard|Shard]] intensified.  
- Became the official guard force after [[Eryndor]] was founded around the shard.  
- Strongly tied to the [[God King]]’s accords, ensuring stability across the continent.  
- They remain a central power during the [[Age of Thankfulness]], helping uphold peace alongside the [[Aethercrysts]].  

---

## Structure & Practices  
- Known for strict discipline and golden ceremonial armor.  
- Serve both as soldiers in wartime and as guardians of the [[The Shard|Shard]] in peacetime.  
- Commanded by generals who often come from noble families of Eryndor.

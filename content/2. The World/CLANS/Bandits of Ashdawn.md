# Bandits of Ashdawn  

**Type:** Bandit Group  
**Region:** [[Ashdawn]] and surrounding farmlands  

---

## Overview  
A band of raiders and bandits operating in the [[Ashdawn]] region. They are known for ambushing travellers and clashing with adventurers.  

---

- Encountered by the party in a farmhouse battle.  
- Included goblins, humans, and other assorted thugs.  
- Their presence ties into larger criminal activity in the [[Ashdawn]] region.

# Kenku  

**Type:** Race / Avian Humanoids  
**Region:** [[Wrethwood]] (majority Kenku town)  

---

## Overview  
Kenku are raven-like humanoids. Though unable to fly, they maintain tight-knit communities and have established themselves as record-keepers.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- In [[Wrethwood]], [[Session 07 Recap]] the party encountered Kenku guards searching for [[Tekka Glyde-Eye]].  
- The Kenku keep a large tome recording magical items across [[Valhyria]].  
  - Tome entries include: [[Amulet of the Ancestors]], [[Necrolexicon Aeternum]], [[Everfrost Pearl]], [[Crown of Thundermarch]].

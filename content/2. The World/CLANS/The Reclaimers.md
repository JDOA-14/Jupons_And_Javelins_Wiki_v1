# The Reclaimers  

**Type:** Group / Cult-like Movement  
**Region:** Unclear  

---

## Overview  
The Reclaimers are a faction devoted to restoring birdfolk dominance over Valhyria, resenting the rise of Aarakocra monks and others who embraced peace.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- Cultists encountered in [[Wrethwood]] and nearby caves were revealed to be [[Mallardfolk]] tied to this ideology.  
- Their rhetoric emphasizes: *“Peace lies in dominion. The sky was once ours — it will be again.”*

# Kua-Toa  

**Type:** Fish people
**Region:** Coastal villages such as the [[Kuo-Toa Fishing Village]]  

---

## Overview  
The Kua-Toa are amphibious folk, typically dwelling in fishing villages along Valhyria’s coastlines. They are known for their eccentric personalities and occasional dealings with outsiders.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- The party visited a [[Kuo-Toa Fishing Village]] during early sessions.  
- Several Kua-Toa were named:  
  - [[Shisha]], [[Shorsh]], [[Shola]], [[Sheosh]], [[Shoawan]], [[Shyle]].  
- [[Shisha]] and [[Shorsh]] were tied to the theft of the [[Everfrost Pearl]].  
- One Kua-Toa ([[Sheosh]]) was tricked into driving a cart for the party.

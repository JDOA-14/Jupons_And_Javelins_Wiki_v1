# Tabaxi Assassins  

**Type:** Mercenary Assassins (Tabaxi)  
**Origin:** Unknown — possibly syndicate-linked  

---

## Overview  
The Tabaxi Assassins are a group of stealthy killers who attempted to ambush the party. Their motives remain unclear, though their professional organization suggests ties to larger forces within Valhyria.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- **[[Session 05 Recap - Present]]**: The assassins struck against the party, displaying extreme agility and coordinated strikes. Their ambush marked one of the deadliest moments for the heroes so far.  

---

## Known Traits  
- Silent, coordinated tactics.  
- Skilled with blades and ambush maneuvers.  
- Professional mercenaries, identity of employer unknown.

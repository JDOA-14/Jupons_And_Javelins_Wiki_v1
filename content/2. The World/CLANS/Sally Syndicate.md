# Sally Syndicate  

**Type:** Criminal Syndicate  
**Region:** Widespread across Valhyria  

---

## Overview  
The Sally Syndicate is a sprawling criminal network, characterised by its many members named “Sally” with various epithets. Their operations range from assassination to infiltration.  

Sallies are never declared dead, they are always "M.I.A"

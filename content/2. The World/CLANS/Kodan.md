# Kodan  

**Type:** Clan / Polar Bearfolk (Ice Sorcerers)  
**Region:** [[Nyvaldra]]  

---

## Overview  
The Kodan are a race of polar bearfolk native to [[Nyvaldra]]. Unlike the more brutish [[BearBandit Lieutenant]], the Kodan are known as sorcerers, healers, and respected leaders. Their culture places emphasis on ice magic, both for battle and for practical uses such as healing and crafting.  

The Kodan clans often stand apart from other bearfolk, holding positions of respect and spiritual authority among northern peoples.  

---

## Distinction from Bearfolk  
- **Bearfolk:** Ferocious warriors, famed for strength and berserker charges.  
- **Kodan:** Polar bearfolk; sorcerers and leaders, using ice-based magic and craft.  
- **Greybears:** A mixed lineage between Kodan and Bearfolk, less refined but notably dangerous.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- In [[Cryos Frostjaw]]’s flashback, Greybear raiders (mixed Kodan/Bearfolk) attacked his home.  
- During this raid, the [[Everfrost Pearl]] was stolen.  
- The Kenku’s artifact tome records that the crown was later given to the Kodan leader [[Gundrik Ashclaw]].  
- Sivara Frostjaw, Cryos’ mother, was the previous bearer of the [[Everfrost Pearl]] before it was lost.

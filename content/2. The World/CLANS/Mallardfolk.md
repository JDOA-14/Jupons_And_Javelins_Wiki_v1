# Mallardfolk  

**Type:** Race / Avian Humanoids (Duck People)  

---

## Overview  
Mallardfolk are anthropomorphic ducks. They appear aligned with cult activity and anti-Aarakocra sentiment.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- A group of Mallardfolk cultists [[The Reclaimers]] fought the party in a cave near [[Wrethwood]].  
- Their leader poisoned an [[Owlbear]] with rage-inducing venom.  
- Expressed hatred for [[Aarakocra]] monks and pledged to restore birdfolk supremacy.

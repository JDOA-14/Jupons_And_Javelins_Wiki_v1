## Description

The Cult Of The Bard is a group involved in a planned disruption of a sacrifice ritual.

## Behavior and Capabilities

- **Power Level:** Described as "annoying but not super powerful."
    
- **Notable Actions:**
    
    - They appeared alongside the [[The Cult Of The Shark]] to disrupt a religious ceremony/sacrifice.
        
    - During an engagement, they were less brutal than their counterparts (the Sharks), who were described as 8-foot-tall hulks with weapons.

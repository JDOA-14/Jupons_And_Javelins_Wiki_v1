# Aethercrysts  

**Type:** Religious Order  
**Region:** Across Valhyria, centered in [[Eryndor]]  

---

## Overview  
The Aethercrysts are the dominant religion of Valhyria, venerating the [[The Shard|Shard]] and the [[God King]] who sustains it.  

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- Present during the [[Age of Thankfulness]] as guardians of peace.  
- Work alongside the [[Golden Guards of Eryndor]] to maintain order.  
- Their influence spans most of [[Valhyria]]’s cities.  


### Hierarchy

- Deacon - assistant to Priest / low level cleric
- Priest - general clerics
- Bishop - overseas a large region
- Archbishop - leads a major region 
- Duke/Duchess Vizier - Very high-ranking and the lowest ranking in [[The High Council of Eryndor]]. Public facing and controlling of certain sections required.
- High Hierophant - Well-respected veterans and leaders. 2 are on [[The High Council of Eryndor]]. 
- Grand Duke - second in command 
- Archduke - Leader of the Aethercrysts, basically ruler of the continent

- "Emeritus" - someone who is retired.

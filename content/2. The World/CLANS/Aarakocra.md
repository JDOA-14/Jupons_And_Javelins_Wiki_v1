# Aarakocra  

**Type:** Race / Avian Humanoids  
**Region:** Across Valhyria; historically dominant  

---

## Overview  
The Aarakocra are a race of birdfolk, once the rulers of Valhyria due to their ability to fly. Their legacy is marked by a rise to power, near-total collapse, and eventual survival through monastic traditions.  

---

## Origin & History  
- Rose to prominence during the [[Age of the Wing]] (623–250 BGK).  
- United and conquered vast swathes of Valhyria by exploiting their ability to fly.  
- Their overreach disturbed dormant dragons, leading to their downfall — 95% of the race was destroyed.  
- Survivors followed a monk who rejected conquest and founded the monastery of [[Aeryndor]], beginning the lineage of the [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]].  
- Some rare Monks have been known to be able to harness aural powers to aid their martial arts. This happens roughly every 100 years. 
- Cole is the first to be able to do this in 100 years (maybe more), yet no Aarakocra knows this as of now.

---

## Campaign Mentions  
- Their historical conquest and fall are referenced as context for the current balance of power.

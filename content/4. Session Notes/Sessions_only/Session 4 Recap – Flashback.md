
# Session 4 Recap – Flashback

## Session Overview
- **PC introduced:** [[Cryos Frostjaw]] (Ethan).  
- **Location:** [[Nyvaldra]] (icy island off the mainland).  
- **Races of Nyvaldra:** [[Kodan]] (polar bearfolk), water genasi, ice genasi, penguin-arakokra, [[Kua-Toa]], sealfolk, walrusfolk.  

---

## Nyvaldra Background
- Kodans: massive polar bearfolk, not brutes but sorcerers and diplomats.  
- Known for: ice magic, healing, building structures, and leading alliances between water and ice races.  
- Tradition: every ten years, Nyvaldra chooses a “Chosen One” — to be a beacon for Nyvaldrans and to be a leader (warrior, diplomat, or sorcerer).  

---

## Cryos Frostjaw’s Inauguration
- Ceremony: Cryos is chosen as the latest **Chosen One**.  
- Celebration: great hall of the ice palace in Nyvaldra’s center.  
- Atmosphere: joyful, though some guests conspire that Cryos was only chosen due to his mother’s council seat.  

---

## New Home
- Next morning: Cryos is shown to his new “Chosen One’s Home” — a palace-like house made from ice shards, located on a small island off Nyvaldra’s west coast.  
- Family: Cryos lives there with his mother (council member), father, and brother.  

---

## The Attack
- Unidentified vessels approach the island.  
- Invaders: brown bears, black bears, and grey bearfolk (half-polar/half-brown).  
- Leader: hulking scarred grey bear, thick leather armor, wielding a large maul.  

### Mother’s Intervention
- Cryos attempts to fight but his mother stops him.  
- She reveals: the true duty of the Chosen One is to protect **Nyvaldra’s secret**.  
- Produces: a glowing blue pearl.  The [[Everfrost Pearl]]
- Action:  
  - Presses it to Cryos’ chest, freezes it in place with frost magic.  
  - Pushes him against the icy wall, fully encasing him in the structure.  
  - Cryos is hidden, unable to intervene.  

### Capture and Loss
- Grey Bear leader enters, argues with Cryos’ mother, then abducts her.  
- By the time Cryos breaks free:  
  - His mother and invaders are gone.  
  - His father and brother lie face down in the snow.  

---

## Aftermath
- Nyvaldra’s armies arrive late.  
- They see Cryos alive but no one else.  
- Verdict: brand him a coward, unworthy of the title of Chosen One.  
- Punishment: banishment from Nyvaldra.  

---

## Exile
- Sent adrift on a slate of ice toward the mainland.  
- Survival: paddles with a branch, treacherous journey.  
- Reaches mainland, collapses unconscious.  

---

## Loss of the Pearl
- While unconscious, Cryos is looted by [[Kua-Toa]], including [[Shisha]] and [[Shorsh]].  
- They steal the glowing blue pearl.  
- Later, the [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]] seizes it during his thefts.

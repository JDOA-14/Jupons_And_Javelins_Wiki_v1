# Session 02 Recap

**Players Present:** Alex, Thomas, Rhys, Sam  
**PCs Present:** [[Sally the Smasher]], [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]], [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen Stormrage]], [[Gronk Shadowfang]]  
**Level:** 2  

---

## Crooked Tusk Tavern – Aftermath of Session 1
- Party stayed the night at the [[Crooked Tusk Tavern]] in [[Ashdawn]] after defending it from mutants.  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]] sent a letter to the monk-detectives of the southwest.  
- [[Sally the Smasher]] awoke to a note from the [[Sally Syndicate]], confirming her mission is real and to stay the course. She shared this with the group.  
- Cole received a response from [[Captain Verrik]] (highest-ranked monk detective in the southwest, based in [[Thundermarch]]), instructing him to continue with the group to find the stolen artifact.  

---

## Virka and the Pearl Shell
- The group met [[Virka Snapgut]], a goblin woman who had searched for stolen items with others that morning.  
- Virka recovered her grandmother’s pearl — cracked open, hollow inside.  
  - Arcana checks confirmed faint residual magic (enough to seal something inside).  
- Virka led the group west to the site where she found it.  
  - Evidence: dent in ground, broken branches, suggesting pearl shell was thrown.  
  - Footprints tracked to a farmhouse.  

---

## The Farmhouse Battle
- At the farmhouse, they met [[Maeve Tilden]], who said she and her husband [[Barret Tilden]] often housed travellers.  
  - She described a hooded, black-haired stranger who stayed the previous night — kind, but noisy upstairs.  
- Investigation inside:  
  - PCs left weapons outside.  
  - Cole triggered an invisible explosive rune upstairs, taking heavy damage.  
- Immediately after, 3 horns sounded. A group of goblins and human bandits attacked.  

### Combat Highlights
- [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen Stormrage]] used a spell through [[Turbo]] to suck a soul through a bandit’s skull.  
- [[Gronk Shadowfang]] found a bow and arrows upstairs, shot from windows with [[Virka Snapgut]] joining and impressed.  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]] jumped through a window, floated down, killed two goblins with martial arts. Punched one and spin kicked another into a wall.  
- A “cool goblin” failed checks repeatedly:  
  - Stepped on a shovel → injured.  
  - Next round, nat 1 → shovel knocked knife into his own skull, killing him.  
- [[Sally the Smasher]] smashed through a wall, launching a goblin.  
  - Later bit a tumor off a goblin, threw multiple enemies.  
- [[Cuddleclaw]] missed most attacks.  
- The group eventually won with no casualties.  

### Aftermath at the Farmhouse
- [[Barret Tilden]] returned home, panicked. Maeve convinced him the group had saved her.  
- They stayed overnight to help repair the damage.  
- Discoveries:  
  - Upstairs, Cole found a faintly magical blue shard (likely from pearl).  
  - Barret shared that coworkers at his workplace had recently stolen a blue pearl.  

---

## The Fishing Village
- Barret took them by cart to his workplace — a small [[Kuo-Toa Fishing Village]] south of Ashdawn.  
  - Layout: cluster of houses → tavern → 3 fishing docks/warehouses.  
- Barret departed to repair his house.  

### At the Tavern
- Majority of population: [[Kua-Toa]] (fish folk).  
- Sally shapeshifted into a bulky kua-toa, attracting mass infatuation.  
- Learned pearl (they don't know it yet, but it's Cryoss' [[Everfrost Pearl]] was found by [[Shisha]] and [[Shorsh]], who lived in town.  

### At the Houses
- Five kua-toa followed Sally in admiration.  
- At the house, met rude [[Shyle]] but allowed them to search.  
- Found Shisha and Shorsh never came home — belongings left behind.  

### At the Worksite
- Three rival fishing companies operated side by side.  
- Encountered worker [[Shoawan]], convinced by Kaelin’s divine bluff to support him.  
- At the Dried Fish docks, discovered dying workers outside and one inside.  
- Suddenly, **Black Rain** fell.  

---

## Black Rain Mutants & The Freaktopus
- Rain mutated kua-toa and two goblin workers into horrors.  
- PCs and companions remained indoors.  

### Battle
- Party fought off mutants inside.  
- From the water emerged the **Freaktopus** (massive mutated octopus) and a **Freakfish**.  
- The party devised a plan:  
  - Covered a mutant goblin in oil.  
  - Threw it into Freaktopus’s mouth.  
  - [[Turbo]] carried a torch, ignited the explosion.  
- Cole was briefly grabbed by a tentacle; Sally freed him.  
- [[Virka Snapgut]], Gronk, and [[Cuddleclaw]] fought the remaining mutants.  
- [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen Stormrage]]’s summoned skeleton rolled double nat 20s, spoke “I understand,” attacked, then exploded — shards damaging the Freakfish.  

### Victory
- Freaktopus burned to a crisp.  
- Freakfish and mutants defeated.  

---

## Aftermath
- Survivors: [[Shisha]] and [[Shola]].  
  - Report: hooded man stole the pearl and sliced up workers, but did not kill them.  
- [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen Stormrage]] made a business deal:  
  - Gave Shisha 20 gold.  
  - Convinced to join with three businesses, securing “10% from each.”  
- Shola also agreed to join the arrangement.

# Session 6 Recap

**Players Present:** Thomas ([[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]), Rhys ([[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]), Alex ([[Sally the Smasher|Sally The Smasher]]), Sam ([[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]]), Ethan ([[Cryos Frostjaw]]), Toby (Mae)  
**Player Characters:** [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]], [[Kaelen Stormrage]], [[Sally the Smasher]], [[Gronk Shadowfang]], [[Cryos Frostjaw]], [[May Mistbrooke]]

---

## Key Events

- **Gronk’s Ancestor Encounter**
    
    - Pulled into another dimension by a ghostly armored goblin on a skeletal warhorse — his great-grandfather [[Gor’ulthrak Umbrazhul]]. (Greenish flame covered too)
        
    - His full name (inc. titles) is - Gor’ulthrak Umbrazhul, First of the Umbrazhul Line, Dreadhelm of the Thousand Screams, Tamer of The Wolrikkae, Erupter of the Ancestral Pyre, The Maw That Hungers Beyond Death.
        
    - Accuses Gronk of failing the family, but fumbles his own timing — He aims to show Gronk his parents,  [[Grotch Shadowfang]] and [[Grivella Shadowfang]], in trouble -- but shows them peacefully gardening.
    
    - He sends Gronk back **days later** than he was picked up, into a prison cage at [[Camp Bearblood]], with Cryos Frostjaw.
        
- **Sally The Smasher’s Secret Mission**
    
    - Awakens in a carriage with [[Sally the Soldier]], her former trainer.
        
    - Learns she has not been using “[[Sally Speak]]” (graffiti-like symbols only Sallies can read).
        
    - Assigned to infiltrate an orc camp.
        
    - Disguises as an orc and lives among them, gathering intel on their leader’s stash.
        
- **The Prison Break**
    
    - Cryos and Gronk bicker, annoying their orc captors.
        
    - Sally (in orc disguise) discovers Gronk and Cryos imprisoned together, plus three angry bearfolk in an adjacent cage.
        
    - [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]], [[Kaelen Stormrage]], [[May Mistbrooke]], and mercenaries ([[Myranda]], [[Sir Fradd]], [[Sally the Sellout]]) observe the camp.
        
    - They send [[Turbo]] as a distraction — most orcs chase the floating head and fail to hit it.
        
    - The heroes storm the camp:
        
        - Sally reveals her true form.
            
        - Cryos breaks free with Gronk’s help.
            
        - Sally retrieves Cryos’ arcane focus.
            
        - Bears are convinced to fight the orcs first.
            
    - The group defeats the orcs, the orc leader, then the bears, and uncovers loot, including:
        
        - A map leading orcs north toward [[Skaldgrym]].
            
        - A bearfolk map leading a band of bandit bearfolk south to [[Nyvaldra]].
            
        - Both maps mark [[Nerton]], (cause of the [[Nerton Incident]]).
            
        - Orc map has a fresh mark at [[Everviewed]], site of a planned truce.
            
- **The Village of Everviewed**
    
    - The gang travels northeast, reaching the farming village on a lakeside cliff.
        
    - Party splits:
        
        - Sally the Sellout and the other mercs, and Cryos stay outside.
            
        - Cole and Kaelen scout from the cliff.
            
        - Gronk, Sally, and Mae explore town.
            
    - Observations:
        
        - Villagers act strangely: distressed around heroes, calm otherwise.
            
        - Out-of-place crab spotted.
            
        - Farmers approach with quests to lure the group away (one claims child kidnapped to [[Sarthmoor]]).
            
    - Sally finds Sally-Speak graffiti: _“Don’t trust the crab.”_
        
    - Strong magic sensed from a lakeside house. Inside, an old merchant woman warns them about a wizard in her basement.
        
    - Kaelen, Sally, and Gronk descend — meet an illusion of [[Stebonheath]].
        
- **Revealing Stebonheath**
    
    - Outside, Cole and Mae suspect a “villager” watching the house — the illusion collapses, revealing Stebonheath himself.
        
    - Cole corners him, but Stebonheath summons [[Black Rain]] over Everviewed.
        
    - All 33 villagers mutate into monstrosities.
        
- **The Mutant Siege**
    
    - Mutated villagers swarm the party.
        
    - Inside basement crew break out with Turbo’s help.
        
    - Sally The Smasher crushes mutants with her hammer; Kaelen uses dragon breath; Gronk fights mounted on [[Cuddleclaw]].
        
    - Ranged support from Sally the Sellout, Cryos, and Myranda; Sir Fradd tanks up front.
        
    - Cole chases Stebonheath into the barn district, knocks him out, and saves him from being torn apart by mutants.
        
    - More hordes gather — heroes retreat to rooftops and create choke points with Kaelen’s skeletons and Mae’s spiritual weapon.
        
    - A colossal mutant “[[Farm Freak]]” (fusion of livestock) bursts from the barn.
        
    - Sir Fradd and Sally The Smasher hold the line while others attack. Together, they slay the beast.
        
- **Stebonheath Captured**
    
    - Tied up and stripped of gear (rings, bracers, staff, chestpiece, necklace, magical tooth focus).
        
    - Reveals he:
        
        - Organized orc and bearfolk but struggled to control them.
            
        - Works for someone else.
            
        - Was tasked with retrieving the [[Crown of Thundermarch]].
            
    - Breaks free, triggers a hidden glyph stone, summoning **another black rain** as the session ends.

## Plot Developments

- Orcs and bearfolk both influenced by [[Stebonheath]], but his orders come from a higher master.
    
- [[Everviewed]] revealed as peaceful village that was overtaken but the powerful and desperate [[Stebonheath]] Basically doing a "Scartlet Witch" to the village.
    
- [[Black Rain|Black rain]] confirmed as being causable by magic means.
    
- The [[Crown of Thundermarch]] is a key objective for [[Stebonheath]]’s employer.
    
- [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]]’s secret [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]]-Speak network hints at deeper infiltration missions.
    
- The Umbrazhul family and their strange ancestor suggest [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]]’s lineage has cosmic weight.

## Highway Worg Battle
- Cole, Kaelen, mercs, and Sheosh in a cart — pursued by **worgs with goblin riders**.  
- Mae, traveling separately, joins mid-chase with her own cart.  
- Battle highlights:  
  - Mae rams her cart into worgs.  
  - Kaelen enlarges [[Turbo]], who then piledrives a goblin rider.  
  - Cole flies between carts, striking enemies.  
  - Worg attacks horse → but is slain before it can damage the horse too badly.  
- Gang unites post-battle; Mae introduced and agrees to join.  
- Mae reveals they were on their way to [[Thundermarch]] after receiving a letter for aid from [[Leif LúmëLóke]]

## The Rat Bounty
- Location: small hamlet/town called [[Nerton]].  
- Farewell given to Johnny & Derek because they thought they weren't doing shit to contribute.  
- Gang meets suspicious bounty giver [[Hiram Hobb]]→ leads them to basement.  
- Encounters:  
  - Swarm of tiny rats.  
  - Ambush by giant rats and **Rat Kings** (multiple rats tied by tails).  
- Rats defeated; upstairs, [[Hiram Hobb]] turns out to not be suspicious but is really scared. He warns of violent strangers right outside his house.

## Orc & Bearfolk Conflict
- Overhear **orc + bearfolk** arguing:  
  - Both claim they have rights to raid hamlet.  
  - Each says “Stebby 'eath" ([[Stebonheath]]) promised it to their clan.  
- Conflict escalates → full hamlet raid by orcs and bearfolk.  
- Gang fights both sides simultaneously.  
- Battle highlights:  
  - Bear suffers brain damage → charges on all fours.  
  - Seconds later, an orc suffers same fate → also charges on all fours.  
- Outcome:  
  - Hamlet saved.  
  - Johnny & Derek resurface — alive, each slain 4–5 enemies.

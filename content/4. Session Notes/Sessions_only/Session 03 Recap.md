# Session 3 Recap

## Session Overview
- **Players present:** Thomas (Cole), Rhys (Kaelen), Alex (Sally).  
- **PCs present:**  [[Cole Le Fev|Cole]], [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]], [[Sally the Smasher]], [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] (removed mid-session). 
- **Level:** 2  

---

## Events

### Departure from Drippledown
- After the battle at [[Dried Fish]], Kaelen confirms his deals with [[Shisha]], [[Shorsh]], and other Kua Toa.  
- The gang plans to travel to [[Thundermarch]] to investigate the [[Black Rain]] and stolen magical items.  
- A flaming ghostly goblin knight, [[Gor’ulthrak Umbrazhul]], appears and rips [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] and [[Cuddleclaw]] from the material plane.  
- The warp triggers latent power in [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]], sprouting astral arms.  
- [[Virka]] returns to Ashdawn but promises to monitor Kaelen’s business ventures.  
- They intimidate [[Sheosh]] to drive them by wagon to Thundermarch.  

### Arrival at Thundermarch
- Sheosh drives recklessly into the city, alarming guards.  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] networks his way to [[Captain Verrik]].  
- Description: Verrik is an old, gruff [[Aarakocra]] monk detective. Square head, white feathers, scarred wings full of arrow holes.  
- Verrik reports Thundermarch has also been struck by [[Black Rain]]. Scientists are studying it.  
- He takes them to the throne room.  

### Thundermarch Throne Room
- They meet:  
  - [[King Selos SkySplitter]] (ancient lightning Genasi, barely moves, crown).  
  - [[Prince Sier SkySplitter]] (half-human lightning Genasi, rules in his father’s stead).  
  - [[Stebonheath]] (town mage, human wizard).  
  - [[Leif LúmëLóke]] (town cleric, human).  
  - [[Helga Brightrun]] (maid, long black hair, exchanges knowing look with Sally).  
- Prince reports: Black Rain has also hit nearby towns; magical items have gone missing.  
- He offers information in exchange for a mission.  

#### Thundermarch’s Oath
- History: King Selos made a divine pact — *“If we march, the thunder will march with us.”*  
- This prevents the King, Prince, or soldiers from acting outside their walls. Any march could unleash catastrophic thunder.  
- Therefore, only outsiders can deal with external threats.  

### Orc Raid Mission
- Prince tasks the party with stopping a rogue band of Orcs raiding villages between Ashdawn and Thundermarch.  
- Confirmed by Verrik: They are not [[Urdakar]] orcs; they’ve even killed Orc villages.  
- Current threat: Orcs have overrun a hamlet with a fortified church, [[Mistthrow]], previously protected by [[Paladin Jayce]].  

### Shopping and Mercenaries
- The gang shops in Thundermarch:  
  - Orc blacksmith: [[Gorruk Anvilborn]].  
  - Goblin alchemist: [[Yibba Fizzwhistle]] (they sell loot to him; he brews a Freaktopus oil bomb).  
  - Bearfolk shopkeeper: [[Jonas Blackmere]].  
- They hire mercenaries at the guild:  
  - [[Fior]] (elf druid).  
  - [[Silch]] (unknown hairy rat-like humanoid) and partner to [[Fior]] (they come in a pair and you get a discount if you get both).  
  - [[Chains]] (mercenary gained via token, race & class both “Chains”). Introduced by giant Owlin drop, with “Fortunate Son”-vibes.  

---

## Orc Hamlet and Dungeon
- At the church hamlet: [[Paladin Jayce]] is found hung at the gates.  
- Guards on walls are quickly eliminated (destroyed) by sneaky beaky ranged attacks.  
- [[Chains]] launches a javelin made of chains from his arm.
- Orc inside church retreats through a secret staircase → dungeon → cave-like lower levels.  
- Gang pursues through cramped tunnels, slaying Orcs before they can raise alarms.  

### Dungeon Battle
- Final room: older stonework, ancient compared to rest of dungeon.  
- Ambushed by Orcs.  
- Combat notes:  
  - Hidden archers inside the walls.  
  - Sally smashes through walls to kill hidden Orcs.  
  - [[Silch]] wildshapes into a giant beetle, pins Orc leader.  
  - [[Fior]] spends most time healing (party too strong to need it).  
  - [[Chains]] is a disaster: misses all rolls, explodes his chain arm, fails dual-wield attempt.  
  - [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] + beetle Silch take out wall Orcs.  
  - [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] + skeleton summons decimate.  
  - Orc reinforcements flank — beetle Silch tunnels through ground to flank the flankers.  

---

## Discoveries
- Ancient inscription in the dungeon - in a forgotten language and only read by [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]'s use of "Comprehend Languages" it reads: *“Created, not born.”*  
- Orc map looted: shows raided villages, future targets, marked all the way to [[Skaldgrym]].  
- Symbol on map resembles Skaldgrym’s house sigil but altered.  

---

## Return to Thundermarch
- The city is in chaos: market destroyed, civilians + mutants dead.  
- Black Rain struck during their absence.  
- Missing: [[Stebonheath]] and [[Helga Brightrun]].  
- Also missing: the King’s crown.  

### Prince’s Report
- Prince shares all Thundermarch’s data on the rain:  
  - Black Rain so far has appeared at sites of stolen magical items.  
  - Trajectory: moving from north of Thundermarch → down toward Ashdawn.  
  - Rain evaporates too fast for study; cannot be contained.  
- He asks the gang to continue helping with bounties, as Thundermarch is too understaffed to act.  

### Final Note
- As they leave, [[Captain Verrik]] whispers something secretly into [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]’s ear.  

---

## Combat Highlights
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]: summons astral arms, clears wall Orcs with Silch.  
- [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]: skeletons overwhelm Orcs, uses *Comprehend Languages* on inscription.  
- [[Sally the Smasher]]: wall-smash kills, massive melee dominance.  
- [[Silch]]: wildshapes to beetle, MVP of battlefield control.  
- [[Chains]]: complete failure, comedic relief.  
- [[Fior]]: healer, low impact.

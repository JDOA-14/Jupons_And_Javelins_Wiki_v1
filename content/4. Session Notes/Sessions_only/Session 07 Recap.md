# Session 7 Recap

**Players Present:** Thomas ([[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]), Ethan ([[Cryos Frostjaw]]), Toby (Mae)  
**Player Characters:** [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]], [[Cryos Frostjaw]], [[May Mistbrooke]]

---

## Key Events

- **Astral Flame Transport**
    
    - Cole, Cryos, Mae, and the corpse of the [[Farm Freak]] are engulfed by aqua-green astral fire.
        
    - They awaken in a void-flame tunnel, where a skeletal llama person greets them.
        
    - The llama introduces himself as **[[Botswana]]**, sent by Gronk’s great-great-great-great-(maybe more idk)-grandfather to fetch _Gronk and allies_ (a dragonborn, a Sally, and an arakokra).
        
    - Mistaking Cole for Gronk, Botswana portals them into a cave on the material plane before collapsing from exhaustion.
        
- **Owlbear Encounter**
    
    - The trio approach the cave, only to meet an **owlbear**.
        
    - It ignores them, snatches the unconscious Botswana, and flees.
        
    - They track it across the wilderness but stumble into a raider camp; through stealth and Cryos’ mist magic, they eliminate the threat.
        
    - Picking up the owlbear’s trail again, they reach another cave.
        
- **Meeting Tekka Glyde-Eye**
    
    - Inside, they find mortally wounded [[Tekka Glyde-Eye]], a Hollow-Eye monk (rank between Draftling and Nestward).
	    - The ranking for field work goes: Draftling, Hollow-Eye, Nestward/NestSargeant
        
    - Tekka was investigating a cult in the [[Kenku]] town of [[Wrethwood]].
        
    - He tracked the Cultists, who had stumbled upon the owlbear and Botswana. They took great interest in Botswana and decided to come back with reinforcements by morning.
        
    - The group heals Tekka and sets traps in the cave.
        
- **Cultist Ambush**
    
    - Overnight, kenku guards from Wrethwood arrive and join forces.
        
    - At dawn, 5 cultists arrive — revealed to be [[Mallardfolk]] (duck people)**, not kenku.
    - Cult named : [[The Reclaimers]] However, they never said their name out loud.
        
    - Traps trigger: icy floors, falling icicles, and even a molotov-rigged shard.
        
    - Four cultists are proned immediately; one, a large axe-wielder, makes it through.
        
    - He poisons the owlbear with rage toxin, driving it mad.
        
    - Botswana escapes his ropes and joins the fight, tanking a LOT of damage.
        
    - The trio, Tekka, kenku, and Botswana fight the cultists, slay the leader, and — regrettably — the owlbear.
        
- **Cultist Interrogation**
    
    - The surviving cultist rants about:
        
        - Hatred of arakokra monks.
            
        - Says, “Peace lies in dominion. The sky was once ours - it will be again”
            
        - A prophecy that _“he”_ will return to restore birdfolk supremacy.
            
    - He is taken into kenku custody.
        
- **[[The Kenku Tome of Relics]]**
    
    - Kenku log magical artifacts across Valhyria in a great tome.
        
    - The trio examine it and find entries on their lost artifacts:
        
        - **[[Amulet of the Ancestors]]**
            
            - Line of Gronk’s ancestors → [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] → _stolen by undetermined_.
                
        - **[[Necrolexicon Aeternum]]**
            
            - Chain of monk detectives → [[CloudMonk Arvid Browbinder]] → _stolen by undetermined_ → [[Molus Mouthbreaker]] → [[Eyeches Writhal]] → [[Kaelen Stormrage]] → stolen by [[Edmund Stief]] → _stolen by undetermined.
                
        - **[[Everfrost Pearl]]**
            
            - [[Nyvaldra]] chosen line → [[Sivara Frostjaw]] → [[Cryos Frostjaw]] → stolen by [[Shisha]] & [[Shorsh]] → _stolen by undetermined_.
                
        - **[[Crown of Thundermarch]]**
            
            - [[King Selos SkySplitter]] → stolen by [[Stebonheath]] → given to [[Gundrik Ashclaw]] (the grey bear who raided Cryos’ home).
                
- **Mae’s Vision**
    
    - During another astral-flame travel, Mae is pulled into a terrifying vision:
        
        - Sees mortals as small as ants building a vast tower.
            
        - Ants turn to be arakokra -- a single arakokra grows enormous through potions and ritual. - Turns out she is as tall as a mountain.
            
        - The giant arakokra rips up the tower and stabs Mae with it.
            
    - She wakes shaken but alive.
        
- **Botswana’s Mistake**
    
    - Confesses he misidentified the group:
        
        - Thought Cole was Gronk, Cryos was Cole, Mae was Sally, and the dead [[Farm Freak]] was Kaelen.
            
        - Also mistook the owlbear as “Gronk’s father” due to thinking Cole (arakokra) must be Gronk.
            
    - Offers to return them either:
        
        - To the _current time_ (safe from black rain, but allies doomed).
            
        - Or to the _moment they left_ (dangerous, but with a chance to save their companions).
            
    - They choose to return to the exact moment, preparing to risk it all.

- **[[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]]** — Ice traps and mist tactics were pivotal against raiders and cultists.

# Session 8 Recap

## Key Events
- **Players present:** Thomas (Cole), Sam (Gronk), Alex (Sally The Smasher), Rhys (Kaelen), Toby (Mae).  
- **Player Characters:** [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]], [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]], [[Sally the Smasher]], [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]], [[May Mistbrooke]], [[Cryos Frostjaw]] (kind of).  

- The gang begins as the [[Black Rain]] falls on [[Everviewed]].  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]], [[Cryos Frostjaw]], and [[May Mistbrooke]] reappear where/when they were taken by [[Botswana]].  
- Cole tells Cryos to form an ice block above them, but it’s too small — [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] enlarges it.  
- They hold it up as a roof until [[Sally the Smasher]] smashes through the roof below, creating a hole for the gang to drop into.  
- [[Stebonheath]]’s body slides off the roof during the chaos.  

- The black rain starts melting through Cryos’ ice, but Sally saves Cole just in time.  
- Stebonheath mutates into a 10-foot amalgam of himself and his [[Crab Familiar]].  
- The gang fights mutant Stebonheath -- whose chaos magic explodes violently. 
- Arrows rain from the hillside (unclear at who they are targeting).  
- They defeat him. Cole vials his blood and instructs Cryos to keep it ice-cold.  

- [[Helga Brightrun]] appears as the one shooting the arrows, and reveals her true identity as **[[Sally the Sniper]]**, wielding a custom long-barrel crossbow.  
- She claims she was contracted to work with [[Stebonheath]] but she turned on him after witnessing his atrocities. She tells the gang of a big meeting in [[Highwatch]] where [[Gundrik Ashclaw]] will be.  

- The gang travels by kart to [[Highwatch]]. The mayor, [[Caldus Quince]] (a Half-Elf, as per Highwatch law), is unveiling a steam/magic train project.  
- Quince pays them for their bounties, gives them free drinks, and promotes his new ale “Quince’s Quencher” (actually just a stout).  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] quietly arranges for Cryos to escort Stebonheath’s blood sample to [[Aeryndor]] for safekeeping.  

- In the pub:  
  - The gang parties with Tabaxis and half-orcs.  
  - Sally and Kaelen canonically seduce tabaxis. Cole possibly does. Gronk rolls poorly and fails.  
  - Later that night, the tabaxi's turn out to be assassins and attack them. They fend them off. Cryos protects the blood vial.  

- The next day:  
  - [[Sally the Sniper]] warns via Sally Speak that the meeting is actually on the train.  But she is nowhere to be seen.
  - Cryos is flown away by an [[Owlin]] to deliver the specimen.  
  - The gang boards the train using tickets from [[Prince Sier SkySplitter]].  

- Splitting up:  
  - Cole & Kaelen check the rear (after spotting [[Peldo Satch]] looking anxious and heading back there).  
  - Gronk & Sally push forward with [[Turbo]] scouting.  
  - In the back car, they find [[Peldo Satch]], a murky water genasi alchemist, hiding frozen vials of black rain. One vial is missing.  
  - Turbo spies on the VIP cabin: [[Gundrik Ashclaw]], a Goliath general, an Orc, a Lizardfolk, a Tabaxi, a human with bowlcut, an earless elf, a Kobold. Five wizards guard them. The [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]] and a veteran wizard teleport in.  

- Tradition: each VIP votes using two drinks; one from the region and one outside. In this case they use [[Quince’s Quencher]] vs Elven Wine.  
  - The Kobold and Goliath choose Quencher. Others take wine.  

- Cole & Kaelen force Peldo to admit the missing black rain vial is in the Quenchers.  
- As Gronk and Sally fight their way inside, the Dragon-Aura Faced Man snaps his fingers — the stout drinkers throuhout the train mutate.  
- Wizards teleport away with most VIPs. The Kobold and Goliath remain and mutate into powerful foes.  

- Cole, Kaelen, and Peldo leap outside the train and fly along with astral arms.  
- They regroup with Gronk and Sally as mutants swarm. Cole severs most of the train, sending the bulk of mutants tumbling away.  
- The party defeats the Mutant Goliath and Mutant Kobold.  

## Character Highlights
- **Cole**: clever with astral arms, smart flying over cabins instead of through, and persistence in chasing leads.  
- **Kaelen**: Enlarges allies, coordinates via [[Turbo]], finds key secrets.  
- **Sally The Smasher**: brute force solutions (roof-smashing, hammer slams), deadly charisma with tabaxis.  
- **Gronk**: earnest but unlucky in romance; strong fighter against mutants.  
- **Mae**: protective of Cryos, keeps the specimen safe.  
- **Cryos Frostjaw**: maintains ice vial integrity, protecting critical evidence.  

## Plot Developments
- Confirmation that **Black Rain can be stored** when frozen (Peldo’s vials).  
- [[Stebonheath]] is dead — but his allegiance and motives are clearer.  
- [[Helga Brightrun → Sally the Sniper]] joins temporarily.  
- The Sally Syndicate’s involvement deepens.  
- [[Gundrik Ashclaw]] has the Crown, attends the meeting, but escapes.  
- The Dragon-Aura Faced can use magic to orchestrates widespread mutations on people who have ingested some form of the black rain.  They gang doesn't know if he uses it to mutate them, or if the magic is to hold off the mutation for a time.
- Highwatch’s new train becomes a stage for political manipulation.

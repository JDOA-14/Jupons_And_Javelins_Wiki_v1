# Session 8 Recap

## Key Events
- **Players present:** Thomas ([[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]), Sam ([[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]]), Alex ([[Sally the Smasher|Sally The Smasher]]), Rhys ([[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]), Toby (Mae).  
- **Player Characters:** [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]], [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]], [[Sally the Smasher]], [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]], [[May Mistbrooke]], [[Cryos Frostjaw]] (kind of).  

- [[The Gang|The gang]] begins as the [[Black Rain]] falls on [[Everviewed]].  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]], [[Cryos Frostjaw]], and [[May Mistbrooke]] reappear where/when they were taken by [[Botswana]].  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] tells [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] to form an ice block above them, but it’s too small — [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] enlarges it.  
- They hold it up as a roof until [[Sally the Smasher]] smashes through the roof below, creating a hole for [[The Gang|the gang]] to drop into.  
- [[Stebonheath]]’s body slides off the roof during the chaos.  

- [[Black Rain|The black rain]] starts melting through [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]]’ ice, but [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] saves [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] just in time.  
- [[Stebonheath]] mutates into a 10-foot amalgam of himself and his [[Crab Familiar]].  
- [[The Gang|The gang]] fights mutant [[Stebonheath]] -- whose chaos magic explodes violently. 
- Arrows rain from the hillside (unclear at who they are targeting).  
- They defeat him. [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] vials his blood and instructs [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] to keep it ice-cold.  

- [[Helga Brightrun]] appears as the one shooting the arrows, and reveals her true identity as **[[Sally the Sniper]]**, wielding a custom long-barrel crossbow.  
- She claims she was contracted to work with [[Stebonheath]] but she turned on him after witnessing his atrocities. She tells [[The Gang|the gang]] of a big meeting in [[Highwatch]] where [[Gundrik Ashclaw]] will be.  

- [[The Gang|The gang]] travels by kart to [[Highwatch]]. The mayor, [[Caldus Quince]] (a Half-Elf, as per [[Highwatch]] law), is unveiling a steam/magic train project.  
- Quince pays them for their bounties, gives them free drinks, and promotes his new ale “Quince’s Quencher” (actually just a stout).  
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] quietly arranges for [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] to escort [[Stebonheath]]’s blood sample to [[Aeryndor]] for safekeeping.  

- In the pub:  
  - [[The Gang|The gang]] parties with Tabaxis and half-orcs.  
  - [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] and [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] canonically seduce tabaxis. [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] possibly does. [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] rolls poorly and fails.  
  - Later that night, the tabaxi's turn out to be assassins and attack them. They fend them off. [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] protects the blood vial.  

- The next day:  
  - [[Sally the Sniper]] warns via [[Sally Speak]] that the meeting is actually on the train.  But she is nowhere to be seen.
  - [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] is flown away by an [[Owlin]] to deliver the specimen.  
  - [[The Gang|The gang]] boards the train using tickets from [[Prince Sier SkySplitter]].  

- Splitting up:  
  - [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] & [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] check the rear (after spotting [[Peldo Satch]] looking anxious and heading back there).  
  - [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] & [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] push forward with [[Turbo]] scouting.  
  - In the back car, they find [[Peldo Satch]], a murky water genasi alchemist, hiding frozen vials of [[Black Rain|black rain]]. One vial is missing.  
  - [[Turbo]] spies on the VIP cabin: [[Gundrik Ashclaw]], a Goliath general, an Orc, a Lizardfolk, a Tabaxi, a human with bowlcut, an earless elf, a Kobold. Five wizards guard them. The [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]] and a veteran wizard teleport in.  

- Tradition: each VIP votes using two drinks; one from the region and one outside. In this case they use [[Quince’s Quencher]] vs Elven Wine.  
  - The Kobold and Goliath choose Quencher. Others take wine.  

- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] & [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] force Peldo to admit the missing [[Black Rain|black rain]] vial is in the Quenchers.  
- As [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] and [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] fight their way inside, the Dragon-Aura Faced Man snaps his fingers — the stout drinkers throuhout the train mutate.  
- Wizards teleport away with most VIPs. The Kobold and Goliath remain and mutate into powerful foes.  

- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]], [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]], and Peldo leap outside the train and fly along with astral arms.  
- They regroup with [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]] and [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] as [[Black Rain Mutation|mutants]] swarm. [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] severs most of the train, sending the bulk of [[Black Rain Mutation|mutants]] tumbling away.  
- The party defeats the [[Mutant Goliath]] and [[Mutant Kobold]].  

## Character Highlights
- **[[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]**: clever with astral arms, smart flying over cabins instead of through, and persistence in chasing leads.  
- **[[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]**: Enlarges allies, coordinates via [[Turbo]], finds key secrets.  
- **[[Sally the Smasher|Sally The Smasher]]**: brute force solutions (roof-smashing, hammer slams), deadly charisma with tabaxis.  
- **[[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]]**: earnest but unlucky in romance; strong fighter against [[Black Rain Mutation|mutants]].  
- **Mae**: protective of [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]], keeps the specimen safe.  
- **[[Cryos Frostjaw]]**: maintains ice vial integrity, protecting critical evidence.  

## Plot Developments
- Confirmation that **[[Black Rain]] can be stored** when frozen (Peldo’s vials).  
- [[Stebonheath]] is dead — but his allegiance and motives are clearer.  
- [[Helga Brightrun → Sally the Sniper]] joins temporarily.  
- [[Sally Syndicate|The Sally Syndicate]]’s involvement deepens.  
- [[Gundrik Ashclaw]] has the Crown, attends the meeting, but escapes.  
- The Dragon-Aura Faced can use magic to orchestrates widespread mutations on people who have ingested some form of [[Black Rain|the black rain]].  They gang doesn't know if he uses it to mutate them, or if the magic is to hold off the [[Black Rain Mutation|mutation]] for a time.
- [[Highwatch]]’s new train becomes a stage for political manipulation.

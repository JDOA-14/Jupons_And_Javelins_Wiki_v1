# Session 01 Recap

**Players Present:** Alex, Thomas, Rhys, Sam  
**PCs Present:** [[Sally the Smasher]], [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]], [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen Stormrage]], [[Gronk Shadowfang]]

---

## Present Events

### Tavern Crime and Road-Log Fight

- Scene begins at the [[Crooked Tusk Tavern]] in [[Ashdawn]].  
- [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen Stormrage]] attempted a book trade with a witch, while [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole Le Fev]] observed. 
  - Cole, following clues about the orange-diamond book and dragon-aura killer, suspected Kaelin was the criminal.  
- A troupe of **bards/bandits** entered, distracting with a performance.  
  - Stole valuables including books and the tavern’s signature crooked tusk (a baby goblin tusk).  
- Kaelin pursued them to a broken log on the highway, Cole following.  
- Nearby, [[Gronk Shadowfang]] and [[Cuddleclaw]] were resting in the forest.  
  - A bandit urinated on Gronk → sparking a skirmish.  
- The bandits clashed with Kaelin, later joined by Cole and Gronk.  
- [[Sally the Smasher]] revealed herself from disguise as a massive bandit.  
- Kaelin nearly got killed but the group prevailed.  

### Road-Log Fight Highlights
- A fleeing bandit critically succeeded at hiding (nat 20).  
  - Both Sally and Kaelin rolled nat 20s to find him → lightning struck his tree, revealing him.  
  - Rolled nat 1 on morale save, killed himself.  
- During chaos, bandits stole Gronk’s [[Amulet of the Ancestors]].  
- Party learned of a secret bandit hideout: abandoned house in woods, near [[Thundermarch]] (Ashdawn side of river).  

---

### Travel & Bandit Hideout
- Party camped safely en route.  
  - Visited by eccentric halfling [[Tiffle Tattle]], ex-salesman turned seeker of a new life.  
  - He gifted two healing potions (nat 20 luck check).  
- At the abandoned house:  
  - Sally disguised herself again and infiltrated with [[Turbo]] (Kaelin’s grotesque familiar).  
  - Chest with valuables guarded inside.  
  - Sally lured guards away with convincing orders → but leader rolled well, resulting in *all* bandits leaving on false mission.  
  - Party ambushed the remaining forces and won.  
- Opening the chest triggered a magical wave.  
  - All failed Constitution saves → paralysed.  
  - [[Dragon-Aura-Faced Man]] appeared, stole precious items but spared lives.  
  - Group recovered once he left, looted chest and house.  

---

### After Freezing
- Found bandit mission letter signed cryptically: *“the rain will silence, drip drip.”*  

### Black Rain in Ashdawn
- Returned stolen goods to Ashdawn, including the crooked tusk.  
  - Barkeep revealed it belonged to his goblin son.  
- **Black Rain event:**  
  - Oozy black rain fell on Ashdawn.  
  - Goblins danced in it → transformed into mutants.  
  - The party defended the town successfully, with minimal structural loss but civilian casualties.

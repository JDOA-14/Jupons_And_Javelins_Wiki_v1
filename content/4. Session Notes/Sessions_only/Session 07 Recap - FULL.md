POST SESSION NOTES

- Players present: Thomas (Cole), Ethan (Cryos Frostjaw), and Toby (Mae).  
    Player Characters present: Cole, Cryos (pronounced “Cry-Oss”) Frostjaw, Mae.

- We start this session right as we ended the last session - the gang on the roof tops of Everviewed, and the black rain falling -
- Suddenly, Cole, Cryos, Mae, and the corpse of the giant farm freak are surrounded by aqua-green coloured astral flame.
- They appear in a void like tunnel - the walls, cielings and ground all made of these astral flames. 
- Suddenly, the trio is greeted by a very old, very dead skeletal llama person.
- The llama person skeleton walks up to Cole and introduces himself as Botswana, and says “your ancestor has sent me to get you”.
- Botswana calls Cole, “Gronk”, and says that Gronk’s great great great grandfather has asked for Botswana’s help. Gronk’s immediate family is in dire need of help and Botswana has been sent to collect Gronk and his allies - a Dragonborn, a Sally, and an Arakokra.
- The gang go along with it and say they’ll do whatever they can to help. 
- Botswana travels them to a cave, back on the material plane. 
- He says to “Gronk” (Cole) your grandfather is around the corner. The traveling through planes, however, took a lot out of him and he passes out.
- Inside the cave, Cole, Cryos, and Mae leave the massive carcus of the farmfreak and head around the corner - where they find an Owl bear. 
- They are able to show they aren’t a threat to the beast, but it has a taste for bones and rushes past the trio — picks up the passed out Botswana in it’s mouth, and leaves the cave!
- The group stealthily track the owlbear all through the day.
- but as they are tracking it, they stumble upon a camp of raiders.
- They use the darkness to stay in the shadows. Cryoss creates mist around the biggest raider. Eventually the gang take them all out.
- But in the commotion, the Owlbear has ran off
- The trio think it couldn’t have gotten too far, and are able to pick up its trail. 
- They track it to another cave.
- But inside the cave they see a mortally wounded arakokra monk! His name is Tekka Glyde-Eye. And his rank in the monastery is Hollow-Eye. (The ranking for field work goes: Draftling, Hollow-Eye, Nestward/NestSargeant)
- Tekka has been working in Wrethwood (a majority Kenku town). He had recently heard rumblings of a cult forming and had been sent to check it out.
- The gang realise they are in a forest near Wrethwood and Faldwyn — half the continent’s distance from Everviewed!
- The rumblings of the cult had lead Tekka to multiple cult members who themselves had stumbled upon the Owlbear and Botswana. They seemed interested in Botswana and Tekka was able to hear that they will be back in the morning with reinforcements.
- However Tekka got caught and they sliced him up.
- The trio heal Tekka and follow him to where he saw the Owlbear, lucky for them it’s there, but Botswana isn’t…
- They bank on the hope that Tekka’s intel is accurate and the cultists are probably coming back. They decide the wait until morning. 
- They set traps and wait.
- Through the night, there are footsteps in the cave - luckily for the owner of the footsteps, the trio realise these aren’t the cultists and hold off their traps.
- Some Kenku guards from Wrethwood have come in search of Tekka - when they hear of what happened, they agree to help.
- In the morning, the gang hears the cutlists returning. They arm their traps and wait inside; on the edges of the cave entrance so they can’t be seen.
- Their traps include icy ground, and sharps icycles above them ready to drop. On one of the icyicles has a molotov cocktail strapped to it.
- All but one of the 5 cultists trip on the ice and are stuck with icycles from above.
- One cultist makes it through, he is much bigger than the rest and weilds an axe.
- The gang start attacking as the cultists get up and stab and use magic.
- The main cultist teleports to the owlbear and starts slicing it. As he slices, it gets angry - losing it’s mind to a rage poison coated on the axe.
- During the battle, it’s clear these cultists aren’t Kenkus — but Mallards! (Duck people)
- Botswana is there, but he’s in the cave passage tied up.
- The main cultist eventually fully destroys the owlbear’s mind with his poison, and it begins to attack mindlessly
- Luckily, Cole uses some dead cultists as meat shields, and Botswana frees himself and enters the fight. He is VERY durable and is able to take a lot of damage while the gang take down the rest of the cultists, the leader, and unfortunately, the lost owlbear.
- The gang takes the main cultist hostage and ask questions.
- He doesn’t reveal much, but monologues in anger about how he hates Arakokras, how they’ve ruined the lives of all bird people by becoming monks and that someone “he” will return to bring the true believers back to the top of the Valhyria foodchain. “Peace lies in dominion. The sky was once ours - it will be again”.

### Arrival at Nyserith

- Nyserith is the huge Wizarding university city
	- it's a combination of massive old buildings and newer rune magic technology
	- Separated into 11 sectors divided by the "houses" of the university, which are the 11 different schools of magic.
- The gang roll in on their 1/10th of a train.
- The driver sneaks out the window as the gang are greeted by several old wizards
- The older wizards are baffled, but the acting headmaster, Sylak Appletree (a Minotaur wizard) arrives and says he knows what's going on
- He comforts the gang and thanks them for their great work on the train. He understands they've been through a lot and says they can stay in the city for as long as they need.

They go through the school and see mural but don't look, they look at wall of good alumni
they go to Headmaster's office, now being used by Sylak as the headmaster has gone.

They meet some of the members of Eryndor-wide guilds that generally have outfits in every major city/region.
They briefly meet:
- Aaron Balfurs
	- Golden Guard of Eryndor 
- Michonne Flessle
	- Aethercryst Cleric
- Lerance Sootfeather
	- Aarakocra Monk-Detective
Both Aaron and Lerance are overweight and do nothing all day because they are generally just law enforcement for a city with incredible magic ability and oversight. Not much crime happens and it gets taken care of very quickly and very magically, but they still need to be there.

Leif is also there, on a mission since leaving Thundermarch at the same time as the gang.

## Nyserith Exploration and Investigation

They talk to Sylak, and he properly introduces himself as the acting Headmaster while Galahad Guzzletome is away. He shows them a magical holo-message from Galahad. Roughly along the lines of:

"Sorry ol' chap I had to bounce, there's some crazy shit going on and it's big and it's crazy and it could be shit but it could be revolutionary! Look after the school for me".

The gang talk to Sylak about what's going on, Sylak tells them it's concerning and that the black rain sounds like a spell that's not been taught before. He says there's an Alchemy headmaster who could help them and also mentions the library has a book of all the spells currently known across Eryndor by the Wizards Guild. Someone mentions that Stebonheath was a student here. Sylak says he was before his time, but that the head of Necromancy, Darkle Charkle, was in the same year.

The gang are mocked by some cocky students and Kaelen is pulled into the "Drop The Gloves Contendum" which has students fight out their differences with magic only. Kaelen is teleported to a battle room and wins the fight as the others watch through a portal-like screen.

They pass a room called the "Challenge Room" where teachers can give students massive challenges for their studies. Unlike the Drop The Gloves Contendum, the Challenge room can be incredibly deadly if not structured correctly. (This isn't known fully to the gang yet, but the Challenge Room is designed so that the teachers put their arcane focuses into a device that acts as a "safety release feature". They can remove their arcane focuses and immediately teleport anyone in the challenge room to safety).

They move to visit Darkle, and find an animated head like Turbo. Darkle is a bit more educated than Turbo, but they find he doesn't have much information on Stebonheath. But after some digging they do find that he chatted to Stebonheath recently, and that Stebonheath had been in Nyserith recently. Although it is played off as Stebonheath working as a mage, and as the train from Highwatch travels through Nyserith, he'd most likely be working there at some points.

The gang head to the Library and find the book. It showcases not only every spell they can think of, but the building blocks of how they are made. Generally, new spells can be made with a current spell combined with something physical or magical. 

They find the black rain spell - but see it has some missing components that no-one has cracked yet. 
They get curious and search for more spells with missing components, and they find several with not only missing components, but different symbols. 

The gang notice they are being watched by one of the students they met at the train. They chase them down, and catch them. They realise the school has magic detectors that instantly counter-spell any harmful magic. But they interrogate him the old fashioned way using Cole's detective status. They find out this kid is a low ranking member of a cult.

The Cult Of The Longest Cloak. 
Where true believers know the truth about The Shard; that the world is a giant coat rack, and The Shard is the sticking out point. They believe that one day, The Longest Cloak will arrive. A super massive cloak that is big enough to cover the entire continent and more. They believe that once it arrives, it will hang on the rack, endowing it's true believers underneath it with other-worldly powers.

The Gang is validly bemused, but get more info out of them. They find out his name is Gungus Bungus, and that his student friends are other members of the cult and are having a meeting tonight at a rave.

## The Rave

The Gang drop Cryos off at their accommodation and head to the rave. It's near the Challenge Room, and near the Transmutation and Necromancy sections of the city.

The rave is pumping. Sally shapeshifts into a jacked version of Gungus Bungus. She speaks with the "leader" of the students who helped out on the train. The leader notices Gungus is jacked but surprisingly doesn't see through the disguise (they failed a lot of perception checks).
Sally gets some info -- that the students have been told to hide what happened on the train, and that they will be attempting to move the train that night. 

Cole heads to the entrance to make sure none of the other student cult members does a runner.

One of the student cult members spots some of The Gang and alerts the leader. The leader seems calm. But that student leaves.

As soon as they leave the rave, they begin sprinting. Cole spots them and chases them down. Cole with some help from some summoned skeletons, knocks out the student. 
The rest of the students make a run for it, one of them screaming "GET TWIGGY".
The Gang chases, and they fight out the front of the rave. Cole hides the unconscious student, and through the sea of screaming partygoers, The Gang notice the counter-spelling system is switched off. The Cult Students attack The Gang.
One runs out of the rave to immediately get firebreathed by Kaelen. Many are bonked by Gronk. 
They knock another one unconscious but just as our heroes start to get the advantage, Twiggy Marshdust, Head of Transmutation, arrives. He looks like a tabaxi/owlin/human hybrid owlcat person. 

Twiggy summons a powerful companion - a giant pink spectral cat that immediately bites the neck out of the unconscious student. It's clear Twiggy is involved and he doesn't want any loose ends.

The gang are able to defeat Twiggy and his spectral cat, whilst keeping two students alive. 
After they've killed Twiggy, Sylak arrives and is shocked to find what happened. He believes the group that Twiggy was up to something, but tells them he desperately needs their help to clear the bodies so it doesn't look as bad under his watch as acting headmaster.

The Gang appear in a hoard room of an old castle. 
There is treasure and knick-knacks everywhere. 
At the end of the room, is a large mirror, in front of it, two cressets holding two magical gems. (arcane focuses) 

And standing in front of those:
- Lester Saffron -- Head of Evocation
- Wrenchbot 4000 -- Head of Artifissiory

The Gang reads a scripture on the floor:
"The quill may be mightier than the sword, but I lose my quill a lot more often"

Suddenly, what looks like a HUGE BOMB is lowered from the room. 
It's clear that whatever puzzle this floor of the Challenge Room had for them, the bomb is an addition to that.

The fight begins.

Lester uses powerful beams of fire and ice. Wrenchbot summons a small wrench familiar then heads in wrench-fists first.

The gang pile up on Wrenchbot -- but as they slowly take it apart with magic and powerful hacks, bonks, and punches -- they notice that every time they break off one of it's parts, the parts begin to rocket towards Lester.

Sally is able to destroy one of the legs flying towards Lester, but it's too late - he's now got Wrenchbot's old body as a powerful new exo-suit. (Minus one leg).

Cole flies up and inspects the bomb. He spots three wires - red, green, and blue.

The red looks suspiciously like one of the curtains in the room. Someone destroys the curtain and the wire dissipates inside the bomb. Two left.

Someone spots a blue wire in Wrenchbot's familiar. They destroy it.

Cole has a genius idea and SMASHES the mirror. But nothing happens.

Time is ticking down on the bomb. The Gang notice the last wire alias is a necklace around Lester's neck -- but protected by Wrenchbot's carcass acting as a breastplate!

They eventually defeat Lester and cut his wire just before the bomb explodes. 

But now they're stuck in first floor of the challenge room. They go over the clue again and inspect the mirror. Luckily there are big enough shards and they are able to look around the room.

In the reflection of the mirror, it shows the same room -- except a quill is in a different spot. They move the quill in the real room to the spot it shows in the reflection, and they hear a big click.

The two arcane focuses on the cressets become loose, and when The Gang knock them off, a portal to the next room opens.

They do a quick loot and step through -- entering a new room -- presided over by Moris Boris,

and Darkle Charkle.

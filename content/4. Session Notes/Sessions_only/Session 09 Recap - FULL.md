## [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]] Exploration and Investigation

They talk to Sylak, and he properly introduces himself as the acting Headmaster while [[Galahad Guzzletome]] is away. He shows them a magical holo-message from Galahad. Roughly along the lines of:

"Sorry ol' chap I had to bounce, there's some crazy shit going on and it's big and it's crazy and it could be shit but it could be revolutionary! Look after the school for me".

[[The Gang|The gang]] talk to Sylak about what's going on, Sylak tells them it's concerning and that [[Black Rain|the black rain]] sounds like a spell that's not been taught before. He says there's an Alchemy headmaster who could help them and also mentions the library has a book of all the spells currently known across [[1. Eryndor|Eryndor]] by the Wizards Guild. Someone mentions that [[Stebonheath]] was a student here. Sylak says he was before his time, but that the head of Necromancy, [[Darkle Charkle]], was in the same year.

[[The Gang|The gang]] are mocked by some cocky students and [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] is pulled into the "Drop The Gloves Contendum" which has students fight out their differences with magic only. [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]] is teleported to a battle room and wins the fight as the others watch through a portal-like screen.

They pass a room called the "[[Challenge Room]]" where teachers can give students massive challenges for their studies. Unlike the Drop The Gloves Contendum, the [[Challenge Room|Challenge room]] can be incredibly deadly if not structured correctly. (This isn't known fully to [[The Gang|the gang]] yet, but the [[Challenge Room]] is designed so that the teachers put their arcane focuses into a device that acts as a "safety release feature". They can remove their arcane focuses and immediately teleport anyone in the [[Challenge Room|challenge room]] to safety).

They move to visit Darkle, and find an animated head like [[Turbo]]. Darkle is a bit more educated than [[Turbo]], but they find he doesn't have much information on [[Stebonheath]]. But after some digging they do find that he chatted to [[Stebonheath]] recently, and that [[Stebonheath]] had been in [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]] recently. Although it is played off as [[Stebonheath]] working as a mage, and as the train from [[Highwatch]] travels through [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]], he'd most likely be working there at some points.

[[The Gang|The gang]] head to the Library and find the book. It showcases not only every spell they can think of, but the building blocks of how they are made. Generally, new spells can be made with a current spell combined with something physical or magical. 

They find [[Black Rain|the black rain]] spell - but see it has some missing components that no-one has cracked yet. 
They get curious and search for more spells with missing components, and they find several with not only missing components, but different symbols. 

[[The Gang|The gang]] notice they are being watched by one of the students they met at the train. They chase them down, and catch them. They realise the school has magic detectors that instantly counter-spell any harmful magic. But they interrogate him the old fashioned way using [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]]'s detective status. They find out this kid is a low ranking member of a cult.

[[The Cult Of The Longest Cloak]]. 
Where true believers know the truth about [[The Shard]]; that the world is a giant coat rack, and [[The Shard]] is the sticking out point. They believe that one day, The Longest Cloak will arrive. A super massive cloak that is big enough to cover the entire continent and more. They believe that once it arrives, it will hang on the rack, endowing it's true believers underneath it with other-worldly powers.

[[The Gang]] is validly bemused, but get more info out of them. They find out his name is [[Gungus Bungus]], and that his student friends are other members of the cult and are having a meeting tonight at a rave.

## The Rave

[[The Gang]] drop [[Cryos Frostjaw|Cryos]] off at their accommodation and head to the rave. It's near the [[Challenge Room]], and near the Transmutation and Necromancy sections of the city.

The rave is pumping. [[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] shapeshifts into a jacked version of [[Gungus Bungus]]. She speaks with the "leader" of the students who helped out on the train. The leader notices Gungus is jacked but surprisingly doesn't see through the disguise (they failed a lot of perception checks).
[[Sally Syndicate|Sally]] gets some info -- that the students have been told to hide what happened on the train, and that they will be attempting to move the train that night. 

[[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] heads to the entrance to make sure none of the other [[Student Cult Members|student cult members]] does a runner.

One of the [[Student Cult Members|student cult members]] spots some of [[The Gang]] and alerts the leader. The leader seems calm. But that student leaves.

As soon as they leave the rave, they begin sprinting. [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] spots them and chases them down. [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] with some help from some summoned skeletons, knocks out the student. 
The rest of the students make a run for it, one of them screaming "GET TWIGGY".
[[The Gang]] chases, and they fight out the front of the rave. [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] hides the unconscious student, and through the sea of screaming partygoers, [[The Gang]] notice the counter-spelling system is switched off. The Cult Students attack [[The Gang]].
One runs out of the rave to immediately get firebreathed by [[Kaelen Stormrage|Kaelen]]. Many are bonked by [[Gronk Shadowfang|Gronk]]. 
They knock another one unconscious but just as our heroes start to get the advantage, [[Twiggy Marshdust]], Head of Transmutation, arrives. He looks like a tabaxi/[[Owlin|owlin]]/human hybrid owlcat person. 

Twiggy summons a powerful companion - a giant pink spectral cat that immediately bites the neck out of the unconscious student. It's clear Twiggy is involved and he doesn't want any loose ends.

[[The Gang|The gang]] are able to defeat Twiggy and his spectral cat, whilst keeping two students alive. 
After they've killed Twiggy, Sylak arrives and is shocked to find what happened. He believes the group that Twiggy was up to something, but tells them he desperately needs their help to clear the bodies so it doesn't look as bad under his watch as acting headmaster.

And standing in front of those:
- [[Lester Saffron]] -- Head of Evocation
- [[Wrench Bot 4000|Wrenchbot 4000]] -- Head of Artifissiory

[[The Gang]] reads a scripture on the floor:
"The quill may be mightier than the sword, but I lose my quill a lot more often"

Suddenly, what looks like a HUGE BOMB is lowered from the room. 
It's clear that whatever puzzle this floor of the [[Challenge Room]] had for them, the bomb is an addition to that.

[[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] has a genius idea and SMASHES the mirror. But nothing happens.

Time is ticking down on the bomb. [[The Gang]] notice the last wire alias is a necklace around Lester's neck -- but protected by Wrenchbot's carcass acting as a breastplate!

They eventually defeat Lester and cut his wire just before the bomb explodes. 

But now they're stuck in first floor of the [[Challenge Room|challenge room]]. They go over the clue again and inspect the mirror. Luckily there are big enough shards and they are able to look around the room.

In the reflection of the mirror, it shows the same room -- except a quill is in a different spot. They move the quill in the real room to the spot it shows in the reflection, and they hear a big click.

The two arcane focuses on the cressets become loose, and when [[The Gang]] knock them off, a portal to the next room opens.

They do a quick loot and step through -- entering a new room -- presided over by [[Moris Boris]],

and [[Darkle Charkle]].

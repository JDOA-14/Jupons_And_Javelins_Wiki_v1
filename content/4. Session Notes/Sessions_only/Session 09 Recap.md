# [[Session 09 Recap - FULL|Session 09]] Recap

---

## Cryos’ Journey to Aeryndor

- The session opens with [[Cryos Frostjaw]] being flown by an [[Owlin]] toward [[Aeryndor]].
- Cryos keeps a vial of mutated [[Stebonheath]]’s blood frozen using his ice sorcery cantrips.
- Mid-flight, another owlin approaches from Aeryndor carrying a [[Penguinfolk]] mage.
- The mage prepares a frost cantrip and instructs Cryos to throw the specimen.
- The transfer is completed safely, and Cryos is flown back toward where his companions are believed to be.

---

## The Train Aftermath

- On the train, [[The Gang|the gang]] search the wreckage and finds two maps similar to earlier orc and Bearfolk raider maps:
  - One from the mutated **Goliath General**, showing a route from [[Skaldgrym]] → [[Taurakhan]]
  - One from the mutated **Kobold Governor**, showing a route from [[Drassith Cairn]] → [[Wyrmshatter]]
- They find the train driver still operating what remains of the train.
- When he realises what happened to the rest of the carriages, he panics.
- [[The Gang|The gang]] promise to divert responsibility once they reach [[Nyserith]].
- They acknowledge that they’ve accidentally released multiple carriages of [[Black Rain Mutation|mutants]] along the tracks.
- They decide to return and clean up the danger themselves.

---

## Clearing the Abandoned Carriages

- The driver reverses the train back toward the detached carriages.
- They find:
  - Several open carriages with mutants roaming outside
  - One sealed carriage packed full of mutants
- [[Cole Le Fev]] enters an open carriage alone and fights a surprisingly powerful mutant.
- The rest of the party handles surrounding threats:
  - [[Sally the Smasher]] takes heavy damage but survives with help from [[Kaelen Stormrage]]’s summoned skeletons, even using one mutant as a weapon against another.
  - [[Cryos Frostjaw]] arrives by owlin, firing ice bolts from the air.
  - [[May Mistbrooke]] takes flight and attacks mutants from above.
  - [[Gronk]] and [[Cuddleclaw]] charge in, smashing mutants head-on.
- Cole defeats a mutant tied to his past (not actually, just a joke)

### The Locked Carriage

- Kaelen devises a plan:
  - Gronk and Cuddleclaw open the front door.
  - [[Turbo]] flies into the centre holding an oil flask, surrounded by **21 mutants**.
  - Kaelen breathes fire, igniting the oil and setting the entire carriage ablaze.
- Mutants spill out but are weakened:
  - Mae fends off attackers through the windows.
  - Cryos throws an ice knife, shattering into deadly shards.
  - Gronk uses thorn whip to pull mutants out.
  - Kaelen entangles the rest inside.
  - Cole flies in and crushes at least **9 mutants** with his astral arms.
- Cryos finishes the last straggler by dropping a frozen block of ice.
- **Total mutants slain: 28**

---

## Arrival of [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]] Wizards

- Thunder rolls as **four student wizards** arrive from [[Nyserith]].
- They confirm they were sent to investigate [[The Train Incident|the train incident]].
- Seeing the situation handled, they remain behind to mop up stragglers.
- They inform [[The Gang|the gang]] that [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]] is expecting the train.

---

## Arrival in Nyserith

- [[Nyserith]] is revealed as a massive wizarding university city:
  - Ancient stone architecture mixed with rune-powered technology
  - Divided into **11 sectors**, each representing a school of magic
- The gang arrive aboard their remaining fraction of the train.
- The driver escapes through a window.
- Several elder wizards arrive, confused by the situation.
- [[Sylak AppleTree]], a Minotaur wizard and acting Headmaster, takes charge.
- Sylak thanks the gang and offers them refuge for as long as they need.

---

## Meeting the Guild Representatives

- The party meets representatives of [[1. Eryndor|Eryndor]]-wide organisations:
  - [[Aaron Balfurs]] — [[Golden Guards of Eryndor]]
  - [[Michonne Flessle]] — [[Aethercrysts]]
  - [[Lerance Sootfeather]] — [[Monk-Detectives of Aeryndor]]
- Both Aaron and Lerance are noted as complacent, due to [[1. Nyserith|Nyserith]]’s overwhelming magical security.
- [[Leif LúmëLóke]] is also present, having arrived on assignment.

---

## Nyserith Investigation

- Sylak explains he is acting Headmaster while [[Galahad Guzzletome]] is away.
- He shows a magical message from Galahad implying urgent, potentially world-changing events.
- Sylak notes:
  - The black rain spell is unknown and incomplete
  - The Wizards Guild library holds a catalogue of all known spells
- The gang learns [[Stebonheath]] was once a student here.
- The Head of Necromancy, [[Darkle Charkle]], was in the same year.

### The Library

- The gang discover:
  - The black rain spell has missing components
  - Other spells with missing or altered symbols also exist
- They notice a student watching them and pursue him.
- Magic detection systems prevent hostile spellcasting, so interrogation is physical.
- The student reveals himself as a member of the **[[Cult of the Longest Cloak]]**.
  - Belief: The world is a coat rack, [[The Shard]] is the hook
  - One day, a cosmic cloak will descend and empower true believers
- The student’s name is [[Gungus Bungus]].
- He reveals that a cult meeting is happening tonight at a rave.

---

## The Rave

- [[The Gang|The gang]] attend the rave near the [[Challenge Room]].
- [[Sally the Smasher]] disguises herself as a muscular [[Gungus Bungus]].
- She learns:
  - Students are hiding evidence from the train
  - They plan to move the train that night
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] guards the exits.
- A cultist flees — [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] chases and knocks him unconscious.
- Combat breaks out when counter-spell systems deactivate.
- [[Twiggy Marshdust]], Head of Transmutation, arrives and kills the unconscious student with a giant, pink, spectral cat.
- [[The Gang|The gang]] defeats Twiggy and his summon, keeping two students alive.
- Sylak arrives and asks for help concealing the bodies.

---

## Speak With Dead & The Challenge Room

- Bodies are hidden in the Challenge Room.
- A necromancer professor casts *Speak With Dead* on Twiggy:
  - Confirms cult involvement
  - Confirms Darkle Charkle is also a member
  - Reveals Twiggy was not the leader
  - Confirms the cult was ordered to erase train evidence
- Everyone fails a save and is teleported into the Challenge Room dungeon.
- Only Kaelen resists initially.
- Sylak expends a powerful resource to send Kaelen in after them.

---

## [[Challenge Room]] — Floor 1

- [[The Gang|The gang]] appears in a hoard-filled castle chamber.
- Two arcane focuses sit in cressets before a large mirror.
- Enemies revealed:
  - [[Lester Saffron]] — Head of Evocation
  - [[Wrenchbot 4000]] — Head of Artificery
- A massive bomb descends.
- During the fight:
  - Destroyed Wrenchbot parts fly to Lester
  - Lester dons Wrenchbot as an exo-suit
- [[Detective Cole Le Fev|Cole]] investigates the bomb, and [[The Gang|the gang]] identifies three bomb wires:
  - Red — a curtain (destroyed)
  - Blue — Wrenchbot’s familiar (destroyed)
  - Green — Lester’s necklace
- Lester is defeated just before detonation.
- The mirror puzzle is solved by repositioning a quill seen only in reflection.
- The arcane focuses fall, opening a portal.

---

## End of Session

- The party steps through into the next chamber.
- Awaiting them:
  - [[Moris Boris]]
  - [[Darkle Charkle]]

**Session ends.**

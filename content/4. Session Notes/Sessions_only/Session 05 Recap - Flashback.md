# Session 5 Recap – Flashback

## Mae’s Backstory
- **Player Character:** [[May Mistbrooke]] (human/elfish cleric, new PC played by Toby).  
- **Religion:** [[The Shard]] (main religion of the continent).  
	- Followers of the Religion of the Shard are known as:
		- Aethercrysts - Practising **Aethercrysm**.
		- Commonfolk sometimes refer to it as Shardism/Shardists/Shardfaith (Ye of Shardfaith)
- **Mission:** Sent with other clerics to a plague-stricken village, Mirelift.  [[Mirelift Lore]]
  - All magic fails to cure the plague, despite hours of spellcasting.  
- **Event:**  
  - While trying to save a child, Mae is teleported to a pitch-black void.  
  - A deep, booming voice whispers: *“There are more ways.”*  
  - She is teleported back, but the child and villagers cannot be saved.  
- **Aftermath:**  
  - High priest, [[Archbishop Alric Fenrik]] dismisses the event as “the Shard’s will.”  
  - Mae leaves with shaken faith and unanswered questions.
